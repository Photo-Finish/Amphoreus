## On the Virtue of Chimera's Work

- **Type**: Adventure (from the "The Awooo Firm" Event)
- **Requirement**: Trailblaze Mission "Passage, Reveal the Past Once More" completed
- **Summary**: The soon-to-be-dissolved chimera squad, under your leadership, defeated numerous opponents one after another, earning the title of the strongest. Your legacy as an administrator will be forever remembered by all the chimeras of the Garden of Life.
- **Characters**: Trailblazer; Hyacine; Aristocratic Man; Arielle; Professional Manager; Mityphon; Absentee Freak; Tough Cookie; Slacker; Bad-tempered Person; Tribbie; Kind Praiser
- **Start**: Amphoreus — "Eternal Holy City" Okhema
- **Next**: On the Virtue of Chimera's Work: Epilogue
- **Rewards**: Stellar Jade ×30; Traveler's Guide ×2; Refined Aether ×2; Lost Crystal ×2; "Work Champion" Medal ×1; Credit ×30,000

### Steps
1. Head over to the Garden of Life and listen to what Hyacine has to share.
2. Stop the man who is causing a scene.
3. Do a demonstration with Hyacine
4. Consult the Gardener at the Garden of Life.
5. Work hard and aim for 7th place.
6. Check out what is happening.
7. Keep working and aim for 4th place.
8. Take a peek at the chimeras' daily routine (0/3)
9. Discuss with Hyacine about your next step.
10. Have a chat with chimeras demonstrating sub-par performance.
11. Keep working and aim for 2nd place.
12. Keep it up and defeat Tribbie's squad.
13. Confront the Horn of Freedom.
14. Keep pushing and defeat the final opponent.
15. Attend the award ceremony as the ultimate winner.
16. (Optional) Chat with the King of the Baths
