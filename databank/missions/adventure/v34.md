## Ripples Destined to Rise Again

- **Type**: Adventure
- **Requirement**: (Hidden mission completed)
- **Summary**: (No summary provided)
- **Characters**: Cyrene; Trailblazer
- **Start**: Amphoreus — Aedes Elysiae
- **Rewards**: Stellar Jade ×10; Lost Gold Fragment ×2; Credit ×5,000

### Steps
1. Search for the "Mysteriously Lost Item" by the Seashore of Departure
2. Search for the "Mysteriously Lost Item" by the rear courtyard pond
3. Search for the "Mysteriously Lost Item" on the second floor of the Sacrament Courtyard
4. Search for the "Mysteriously Lost Item" in the Field of Golden Waves
