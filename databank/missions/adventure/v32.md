## Heartbroken Dromas

- **Type**: Adventure (Amphoreus Annals)
- **Requirement**: Trailblaze Mission "Passage, Reveal the Past Once More" completed
- **Summary**: A dromas caretaker left the Dromas Steward's Office and headed to the city, hoping to find a caretaker's sense of self-worth in Okhema... During the process, you also heard the true thoughts of the dromas.
- **Characters**: Trailblazer; Dromas Caretaker; Kokopo III; Gorgeous Dromas
- **Start**: Amphoreus — "Eternal Holy City" Okhema
- **Rewards**: Stellar Jade ×10; Traveler's Guide ×1; Refined Aether ×1; Credit ×10,000; Obtain Achievement "Footsteps Frenzied By Thoughts"

### Steps
1. Head to the Dromas Steward's Office.
2. Take a look at the dromas at the front of the inn.
3. Go to the Dragoman Post Office to take a look.
4. Go to the Sanctum of Prophecy to take a look.
5. Return to the inn and report the situation to the Dromas Caretaker.
6. Accompany the Dromas Caretaker and visit the citizens.
7. Escort the dromas back to the inn.
