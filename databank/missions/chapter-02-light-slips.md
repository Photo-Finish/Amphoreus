## Story Recap

A mysterious celestial body entangled by Three Paths appears in the Memokeeper's mirror — Amphoreus. But the landing is not a smooth one. After surviving the crash, the Trailblaze squad meets the local Chrysos Heir heroes. They learn from them that the Titans supporting the world are rotting away. In Okhema, the final holy city, the two members of the squad undergo great trials and gain the trust of Aglaea. Facing a threat from beyond the fog, everyone decides to band together to defeat the Strife Titan Nikador. In order to destroy Nikador's true form, the group plans to infiltrate Castrum Kremnos of the past. At the end of the Abyss of Fate, the Trailblazer communes with the Time Titan Oronyx and receives a gaze from Fuli. With the help of Mem, everyone pieces together Nikador's divinity and obtains the Titan's Coreflame. At last, Phainon faces his own trial in the holy Vortex of Genesis...
