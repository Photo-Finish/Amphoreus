## Story Recap

Following Nikador's defeat, the Chrysos Heirs prepare for the final campaign against Aquila, the Sky Titan. But first, Aglaea must secure the future of the Flame-Chase Journey at a citizens' assembly. Dark forces conspire against the Chrysos Heirs — the Council of Elders' "Cleaners" strike, leading to Aglaea's dramatic "death." Phainon rises to lead, receiving Dawnmaker from Chartonus and rallying the people. Cipher is entrusted with protecting Kephale's Coreflame. Hyacine seeks ancestral blessings to forge a rainbow bridge to the Eye of Twilight. The expedition ascends to Aquila's sky fortress, where the dark truth of the hero Seliose is revealed. Aquila falls, but Okhema plunges into darkness — the Dawn Device was sustained by Cipher's millennium-long lie. Cipher sacrifices herself against the Flame Reaver. In the final confrontation, Phainon claims Kephale's Coreflame and enters the Vortex of Genesis alone, as the world teeters on the edge of Era Nova.
