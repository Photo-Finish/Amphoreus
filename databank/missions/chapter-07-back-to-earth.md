## Story Recap

After the events of the previous chapter, the Trailblazer returned the Coreflame of Worldbearing and completed the first Era Nova — but something is terribly wrong. The Coreflame of Time was never returned; a mysterious being wearing March 7th's face — "Evernight" — reveals that March 7th entered Amphoreus 97 days before the Trailblazer and Dan Heng, and sacrificed all of her memories to protect them. Now Evernight seeks to filter the Trailblazer's memories and recreate a "flawless" Amphoreus. Dan Heng, having returned to the Astral Express, learns of the Garden of Recollection's plot to steal the "Memory of an Aeon's Fall" and journeys back to Amphoreus. The Trailblazer, trapped in an illusion of a perfect world, is rescued by Sunday and Cyrene, and uncovers Kafka's pre-awakening prophecy about four Paths. Black Swan travels upstream along the Path of Remembrance and discovers Evernight's origins. Dan Heng allies with Zandar and awakens Phainon with the world-cleansing golden blood, then accepts the Earth Coreflame from Terravox. Together with the Trailblazer, they descend into the Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan — the buried heart of Amphoreus — where Evernight's true plan is revealed. March 7th awakens and reunites with her companions, and finally, all forces converge for the final Era Nova.
