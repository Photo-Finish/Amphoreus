## Story Recap

The final truth of Amphoreus is unveiled. After the Trailblazer and Phainon return the final Coreflame, Lygus drags them into an "Immersive Theater" of Phainon's memories — showing his childhood in Aedes Elysiae, his fateful duel with Mydei, and the doomed first Flame-Chase Journey. Meanwhile, March 7th journeys through the Path Space, where Cyrene warns her of the gate to Amphoreus. The truth is revealed: Amphoreus is the Scepter's endless extrapolation, a deep-learning program meant to nurture the black tide — the birth of the Lord Ravager Irontomb, destined to destroy the Aeon of Erudition. Phainon's predecessor, the Flame Reaver Khaslana, has stolen the Coreflames across thirty million cycles to prevent the Era Nova. The Flame Reaver is defeated, and Phainon — embracing the accumulated Coreflames and his own fury — declares war on Nanook himself. The Trailblazer, carrying the title of "Deliverer," is sent back to the beginning of time, where Cyrene's spirit bids them farewell before the new cycle begins.
