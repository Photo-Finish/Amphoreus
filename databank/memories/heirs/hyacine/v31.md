### Find Old Fabio and talk with him

**Kind:** personal
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v31.md`
**Heading:** Adventure Missions — Version 3.1 (Light Slips the Gate, Shadow Greets the Throne) > Frequent Partings > Dialogue > Find Old Fabio and talk with him
**Lines:** 1296–1326
**Id:** `v31__find-old-fabio-and-talk-with-him`
**Heirs:** hyacine (on-stage)

*(You promised to visit the families of those who lost their lives at the Grove and find out about how they're doing at Hyacine's request. Aside from this, however, you might be able to do something more...)*

**Old Fabio:** Ah, young one. Thank you for bringing word fo them. What brings you here?
> *(Trailblazer)* Just checking how you're doing.
> *(Trailblazer)* How are you managing?
>
> **Old Fabio:** ...Ohh... Thank you, such a kind child... Do you have some time to chat with an old man if you're not too busy?
> **Old Fabio:** It's funny how people work. I rarely kept in touch with my two girls before, but now they're... well, I have so many things I wish I could tell them...
> **Old Fabio:** Those two were quite the handful growing up... I saved every penny to send them to school, thinking maybe if they became scholars and spent more time with books, they'd settle down a bit.
> **Old Fabio:** But every time they came home and told me about life in the Grove, it was nothing but crazy stories... about "secretly reading forbidden books" or "getting paid to fake attendance"...
> **Old Fabio:** Just imagine — making such a mess even in a place like the Grove! How in the world did I raise two daughters with such special talents for mischief!?
> **Old Fabio:** ...
> *(Trailblazer)* I'm sorry for your loss...
>
> **Old Fabio:** No, no, it's alright... I've already made my peace with it. They might not have been the destined Chrysos Heirs, but they lived life their own way, you know? I'm proud of who they were.
> **Old Fabio:** All I regret is that these old bones won't let me travel to the Grove anymore.
> **Old Fabio:** I heard young Titus got word about his old man and left with the expedition team a few days ago.
> **Old Fabio:** He's like me — we both know the black tide doesn't leave anything behind, but still... we want to find whatever that's left of them...
> *(Trailblazer)* I could go on your behalf.
>>
>> **Old Fabio:** What— You mean it? I mean... No, that's asking too much. It's too risky.
>> **Old Fabio:** I really appreciate it, young one, but don't put yourself in danger for an old grandpa like me.
>> *(Trailblazer)* No worries. I can handle anything.
>> *(Trailblazer)* I am the Galactic Baseballer!
>>
>> **Old Fabio:** ...
>> **Old Fabio:** I... I can't thank you enough! Please be careful out there, and make sure you come back to us safely!
>> **Old Fabio:** And one more thing, if I may. Could you possibly check on young Titus too? His father's an old friend, and I'm concerned.
>> **Old Fabio:** Thank you for all this...
>
> *(Trailblazer)* (Remain silent)
