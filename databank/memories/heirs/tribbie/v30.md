### Investigate the outlanders headed to the Abyss

**Kind:** personal
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v30.md`
**Heading:** Adventure Missions — Version 3.0 (Heroic Saga of Flame-Chase) > Whereto Shall We Go > Dialogue > Investigate the outlanders headed to the Abyss
**Lines:** 1469–1551
**Id:** `v30__investigate-the-outlanders-headed-to-the-abyss`
**Heirs:** tribbie (on-stage)

*(As your discussion was coming to an end, Soctires' disciple came with word: A group of wounded outlanders has hidden themselves away in the Abyss. You cannot help but think of the refugees that Tribbie and the others accepted into their land... The Abyss is no place to live.)*

*(Approach the marked location)*
**Palaten the Scholar:** Master, look! Those things...
**Soctires:** Though expected, the Mad King's Kin are admittedly resilient. Palaten, you stay in a safe place. Young friend, I'll be counting on you to watch my back.
> *(Trailblazer)* Will do.
> *(Trailblazer)* Ooooh! That line's got me all fired up!
> *(Trailblazer)* I can't guarantee our survival...
**Palaten the Scholar:** Master... you knew martial arts all along?
**Soctires:** No, I simply seized the chance to rally those refugees, so they would keep calm...
**Refugee:** Aahhh! Get away from me, monsters!
**Soctires:** Now!
*(Enter battle against Furiae Archer ×2; Furiae Warrior ×2; Furiae Troupe ×1)*
**Soctires:** You all hurry to the temple. It's safe there — follow me.

*(After the battle)*
**Wounded Outlander:** Ahem!
**Palaten the Scholar:** Master, these people... they're not doing well. Some were already injured before we got here.
**Soctires:** Indeed. And by the looks of them, they haven't gotten a proper meal for a while. Who's in charge here?
**Outlander Leader:** H—Here...
*(A woman holding a child emerges from the crowd.)*
**Outlander Leader:** Thank you all so much, especially this gray-haired hero. How... How can we ever repay you?
**Soctires:** That can wait. Why didn't you go to the holy city's designated shelter? There are sentries garrisoned there. It's much safer than here.
**Outlander Leader:** Shelter... Guards...
**Outlander Leader:** I overheard the guards before, talking about some "dromas workshop incident," "drawing lots," "resource allocation" and whatnot, so I didn't dare ask them to clarify.
**Outlander Leader:** I think the burden on the holy city is already heavy enough. Even if we beg the guards to let us in, it would only add to their troubles, and in the end, it would just lead to an unpleasant situation.
**Outlander Leader:** If that's the case, we might as well look for a nearby place to rest our feet, to see if we could fend for ourselves. But who would've thought...
**Clueless Outlander Child:** Mama, will we die?
**Outlander Leader:** No, baby... It won't happen as long as mama is around.
**Clueless Outlander Child:** But Uncle Anthony, and Sister Aisha... They really look like they're going to die.
**Clueless Outlander Child:** My tummy is hungry too... Didn't you say that we'd have something to eat once we got to the holy city? Mama, when will we get there?
**Outlander Leader:** I'm sorry, darling. I'm sorry...
*(The woman crouches and holds her child tight, tears silently streaming down the corners of her eyes.)*

*(Soctires softly clears his throat and pulls you to the side.)*
**Soctires:** Anyone who just suffered so much hardship would find it difficult to keep their emotions in check. Best give that mother and child some space.
**Soctires:** The outlanders' situation is much worse than I imagined. If we keep turning a blind eye, they will at best survive for a week. That is... if they aren't attacked by wild beasts first.
**Palaten the Scholar:** *sigh* ...Such is the tragedy of life.
> *(Trailblazer)* How can we help them?
>> **Soctires:** It would be hard to bring them directly into the city, so we can only provide supplies for them. And I have an idea about that...
> *(Trailblazer)* We can't save everyone...
>> **Soctires:** But we can at least help those in front of us, can't we? I already have an idea...
> *(Trailblazer)* They brought this on themselves.
>> **Soctires:** Before you pass judgment, please remember that not everyone has a choice.
> *(Trailblazer)* (Remain silent)
>> **Soctires:** At least the worst of the journey is over for these outlanders. Now they just need some simple help to live on. I have an idea...
>>
>> **Soctires:** The holy city's shelter is only a half-day trek away from the city walls. There are guards there, so it'll be safer. It's just that the way these refugees are now... getting there would be difficult.
>> **Soctires:** They haven't much food to begin with, and that last attack has left them in urgent need of emergency medical supplies. We can provide them with these two for now. When their wounded are healed, we can escort them to the shelter.
>> **Palaten the Scholar:** But, teacher... All medicine is now considered wartime supplies in Okhema — distribution is under strict control. Most people won't even be able to buy any.
>> **Soctires:** I know. I will think of a way... at least to guarantee their survival.
>> **Soctires:** The good news is, food isn't strictly regulated. You can easily get enough food to feed these people for a week at the **Marmoreal Market**.
>> **Soctires:** So, I suggest we split up: Palaten and I will secure the medicine, while our young friend buys some food back, after which we will reconvene here.
>> **Palaten the Scholar:** Hey, do I really need to come along? I was thinking of doing the procurement with this gray-haired hero.
>> **Soctires:** I need a helper. Remember my instructions last month? I'm getting old, and there are many things that I need help for.
>> **Soctires:** If there comes a day when I haven't the strength to walk a step farther, you'll have to take my mantle and carry on guiding the people of Okhema.
>> **Palaten the Scholar:** A—As you wish, teacher...
>> **Soctires:** Well then, before we set out, is there anything our young friend wants to say?
>> *(Trailblazer)* Leave it to me.
>>> **Soctires:** Thank you, young friend... I really appreciate you accompanying me on my errands. When this is all over, I have to thank you in earnest.
>>> **Palaten the Scholar:** Didn't you say you were going to treat her to a meal? Don't forget my share.
>> *(Trailblazer)* Don't forget to reimburse me then.
>>> **Soctires:** Haha, of course! When everything's over, just give the invoice to Palaten — he's the best at doing the accounts.
>>> **Palaten the Scholar:** Teacher, being good at the ledger isn't the same as paying for stuff... Please don't tease me!
>> *(Trailblazer)* Can't we notify the officials?
>>> **Soctires:** Lives are at stake. We've got no time to do it by the book... We'll report this to the Council of Elders when the problem is resolved.
>>> **Palaten the Scholar:** It sounds like these people's fates are relying on our success. I never thought a lowly apprentice like me would get a chance to perform such heroics... Sounds awfully exciting!
>> *(Trailblazer)* What do these people have to do with us?
>>> **Soctires:** You're asking the wrong question. We should always provide all the help we can to the weak, not just because it is moral. It is the correct action, even from a utilitarian perspective.
>>> **Soctires:** Who knows when the roles of the strong and weak will be reversed? When we will also need their help? Besides, isn't Kephale's majesty in bearing the burdens of the world for the people?
>>> **Palaten the Scholar:** I never thought a lowly apprentice like me would get the chance to carry on Kephale's will. It sounds very exciting!
>> **Palaten the Scholar:** Then, we'll meet later, gray-haired hero.

*(Outlander dialogue, optional)*
**Clueless Outlander Child:** Mama, I don't want to eat this rock-hard bread anymore. My teeth are going to fall out!
**Outlander Leader:** Be a good girl and have one more bite. We won't have to eat hard bread next time, I promise...
**Clueless Outlander Child:** Yay! I want baked apple pie when we reach the holy city!
**Wounded Outlander:** Ah... Agh...
**Wounded Outlander:** My leg... Is it still there? I can't... seem to feel it...
**Impatient Outlander:** Hang in there. The leader is already trying to help. You'll get better real soon... Don't go to sleep. Don't sleep!
**Depressed Outlander:** Is there no recourse in our search of a safe haven? I simply wish to live out a peaceful, simple life...
**Depressed Outlander:** Do we have to return north in the end? No, I'm not making that trek again!
**Depressed Outlander:** By Kephale, may Oronyx protect us, may Zagreus grace out presence... Any of them will do. Please help us.
