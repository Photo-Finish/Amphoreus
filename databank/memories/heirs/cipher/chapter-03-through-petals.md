> **Cipher:** ...Oh?

> **Cipher:** I really don't get how...

> **Cipher:** But did you ever live for your own pleasure? Even for just one day?
> **Castorice:** What are you trying to say?
> **Cipher:** I'm saying, contrary to your beliefs, Aglaea didn't take you in just so you can die for her Era Nova.
> **Castorice:** The Flame-Chase Journey walks hand in hand with sacrifices. Isn't that why you're always running away from Lady Aglaea?

> **Cipher:** But listen to me: Styxia is no tourist destination. Sure, the treasures buried in there are ten... No, fifty times richer than Aglaea's vault —
> **Cipher:** Yet I've never been there and I never plan to! Because even a demigod can't be sure they can walk out of that place alive.
> **Cipher:** And now I'm supposed to escort you to Styxia? Sure. Pay up.
> *(Trailblazer)* I'll give you however much you want!
> **Castorice:** M—Me too.
> *(Trailblazer)* Aglaea has already paid for me.
> **Cipher:** Her payment was in gold and precious stones, which only covers the living passenger. You need to pay me as a member of the dead.
> **Castorice:** I don't like this, however...

> **Cipher:** You know, you're not wrong. At least it's a part of my responsibilities.
> **Castorice:** I don't like this, however...
> **Castorice:** I have lived a longer life than most people. Though I'm not as good at doing business as Lady Aglaea, I still have some savings.
> **Castorice:** I... I can give them all to you.
> **Cipher:** Then how are you going to live the rest of your life?
> **Castorice:** Th—Then I'll give you ninety... no, eighty percent...
> **Cipher:** ...
> **Cipher:** Well, don't mind if I do. Eighty percent of Castorice's savings and a bottle of Dew of Divine Blood... Heh, not a bad trade.
> *(Trailblazer)* Don't forget to give Castorice's jewelry back.
> **Cipher:** Okay, okay! I didn't know you hold grudges, Gray Mystery.
> *(Trailblazer)* I'll show you who's boss once I've recovered with full HP!
> **Cipher:** Waaaah, I'm so scared! Tee-hee, can you even catch my tail?

> **Castorice:** Ah! Be careful with that...!
> **Cipher:** A minor change in plans, though. Don't go to the bath tomorrow. Come look for me at Okhema's city gates instead.
> **Castorice:** So why are you changing the meeting place...?
> **Cipher:** Oh, nothing really. Just a bit scared of the seamstress' frosty face. I wouldn't stay a second longer in Okhema if it were up to me.
> **Cipher:** Well, now that we've struck a deal, why don't I give you guys a lift back?
> **Cipher:** It's gonna be a long ride, so have a good rest!
