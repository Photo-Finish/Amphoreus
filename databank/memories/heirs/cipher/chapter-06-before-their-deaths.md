**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Never thought we'd be the pair to finish this final setup.
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** Can't catch that Tin Man without your little sleights. Besides, this is our last shot. Not the time to be picky, right?
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Don't oversimplify it. Lygus just found out Okhema's been an empty shell for the past fifty years. He's bound to be furious. At this rate, he could've done anything.

**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Easier said than done. You still have to pass yourself off as the Deliverer without anyone catching on...
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** And you really think a human alchemical array can bind a godlike being? That's just nonsense.
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** What, getting cold feet? Or you just don't believe in the almighty worth of the demigod of Trickery?
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** Just tell me straight: can you do it or not?
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** ...
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Hmph. Of course I can. And it'll be child's play.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** But I've got two conditions. Since this is the final act, if we're doing it, we go all in. No half-measures.
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** Oh? I'm listening.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** First, shift the battlefield to the Vortex of Genesis. It would be more convincing if (Trailblazer) were to appear there.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Besides, the Vortex is cut off from the world. No place in Amphoreus makes a better prison.
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** If you pull that, wouldn't our Deliverer have to face tank Lygus as soon as they come back?
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** That clash is inevitable. All we can do is create more chances for him. And that's the second condition...
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** The Vortex belongs to Phagousa in the first place, remember? And there happens to be a Demigod of Ocean among the few survivors in the world.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** **Loop Hysilens in. Let her join the final act. I'll transmute myself into a Philosopher's Stone and embed myself into the formula...**
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** **And when the time comes, she needs to shatter me and scatter that stone's ashes into the ocean within the Vortex.**
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** ...Heh. I guess people weren't wrong when they said you're insane, Grove boy.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** I've got no idea how to outwit a reclusive genius... But making an Antikytheran suffer? The Demigod of Reason has ten thousand ways to do just that.
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** And what I just mentioned was one of them.
**Cipher Etched in Memory:** So what exactly does your formula do?
**Anaxa Etched in Memory:** Now that would spoil the climax, wouldn't it?
