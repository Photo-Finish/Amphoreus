### Trace the lingering Fragment of Recollection near the fallen guards

**Kind:** personal
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v33.md`
**Heading:** Adventure Missions — Version 3.3 (There Lies the Truth in Dreams) > Bad Kid, Good Gift > Dialogue > Trace the lingering Fragment of Recollection near the fallen guards
**Lines:** 132–162
**Id:** `v33__trace-the-lingering-fragment-of-recollection-near-the-fallen-guards`
**Heirs:** cipher (on-stage)

*(Beneath the armor of a fallen guard, you discover a peculiar relic. Trace what's recorded within the Fragment of Recollection to uncover what happened here.)*

> *(Trailblazer)* Activate Fragments of Recollection
> *(Trailblazer)* Investigate
>
> **Standing Guard:** Phedes, still fiddling with that amplifier? I remember that was something that belonged to your father.
> **Phedes:** Yeah... He liked to use it to play pretend... making voices for me when I was small.
> **Phedes:** After he died in battle, I kept it. I thought it would never come in handy again, but it turns out I ended up mimicking voices with it sometimes too... And that day, at Dawncloud...
> **Phedes:** It was just an unexpected moment born of desperation, but seeing how happy it made the kid, I thought it was a worthwhile lie. I really hope she's still keeping the faith now!
> **Standing Guard:** Really? Who did you impersonate?
> **Phedes:** Hm... If I said I impersonated Kephale, would you believe me?
> **Standing Guard:** Ha! Stop joking around. Say, you're not deliberately jesting to lift our moods with how desperately the war's going, right?
> **Standing Guard:** Oh no! Look there! Black tide monsters incoming!
> **Phedes:** Which wave of battle are we on now... Lets go. Lets get ready. We can't let the kids of the holy city lose their smiles!
>
> **Trailblazer:** So that's how it was... I understand.
> **Trailblazer:** Phedes, Phedes... Oh "Mr. Nice Guard" who sneaked us into the Worldbearing Altar and impersonated Kephale for Serena — that was his name.
> **Trailblazer:** But, sadly, he fell...

*(As you sink deep into your thoughts, a despairing wail comes off from the side...)*
**Despairing Priest:** All-knowing sky father! See your fallen guards, your people! Why does death stain the holy city? Have you abandoned them?
**Despairing Priest:** The Era Nova... Our Era Nova... I always believed that we would awaken to that warm, radiant future in tranquil dreams.
**Despairing Priest:** But why does your Dawn Device darken? Didn't the chaplain say it would protect us till... forever?
*(The priest's sobs fade. You know the "eternal protection" was a lie that Cipher's created. When truth is revealed and faith shatters, will Serena be able to handle the following consequences?)*
> *(Trailblazer)* We can't let the kids lose their smiles!
> *(Yes, indeed. You recall Phedes' last words — "We can't let the kids of the holy city lose their smiles!" But... How should we proceed?)*
> *(A plan forms in your mind: You take Phedes' stone-made amplifier and tell that upright guard the whole story, asking him to bring the children before the colossus... And then you use your lies to complete the children and Phedes's final wish.)*
>
> **Trailblazer:** Lets do that then!
> *(The next day, after clearing the monsters roaming Dawncloud at Entry Hour, you stand before Kephale's statue...)*
> **Trailblazer:** Perfect timing. I'll hide before Serena and them arrive.
