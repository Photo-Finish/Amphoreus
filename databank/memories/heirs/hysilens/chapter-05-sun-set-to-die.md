*(Do you still remember them, Khaslana, the one who won't reach the dawn? The hero tore through the sky, the Holy Maiden fell from the cliff, and the chessmaster ignited the first Flame, all in the name of prophecy of Worldbearer. What noble deeds! And yet fate, ever generous, ensures that the death-bound nymphs shall find their death nevertheless. O Deliverer, behold the lifeless ash they've become! Do you still futilely dream of saving them? Those who push the boulder upward will see the earth swallow them whole. Those who meddle with time earn nothing but a shattered shell. Hear me. You're no different from them: The singers still scream, and the bright-eyed still go blind. Go, then! March on alone — Revel, dance, throw yourself into the tireless flame, and never stop!)*

*(The 1st Eternal Recurrence)*

**???: ** So, what you're saying is: You've traveled back down the river of time, finally reaching this untouched seabed, just to tell us—

**???: ** The Flame-Chase Journey, and even all of Amphoreus, are nothing but illusions in an Aeon's dreams?

**Khaslana:** Exactly. So, Dux Gladiorum, please take me to meet Her Majesty Cerydra. I must share the truth with her.

**???: ** Since Her Majesty is not here, no need for formalities. The word "Gladiorum" always reminds me of my fallen homeland buried deep in the depths...

**Hysilens:** Call me Hysilens, nameless swordmaster.

**Khaslana:** Please allow me to add one more thing. You actually already know my name.

**Hysilens:** You mean Phainon? Such a name could never be your true one...

**Hysilens:** But I won't question you further about it. Ever since Her Majesty issued the Flame-Chase decree, heroes from all over have sent their letters of introduction. What's the harm in adding one more name like Phainon or Pie-non?

**Hysilens:** Besides, the deep sea is dark. Isn't it the perfect place to hide secrets best left unseen?

**Khaslana:** ...

**Hysilens:** Alright, nameless swordmaster. Hold onto my wake. Let's find somewhere else to talk.

**Khaslana:** Aren't you even a little suspicious of what I've said?

**Hysilens:** Harboring suspicions is not my concern...

**Hysilens:** My sword dances only when the Imperator harbors doubts. There's no use in speaking further. Show me your true intentions through action.

*(Approach the marked location)*

**Hysilens:** Dux Goldweaver, little hostage. Apologies for keeping you waiting.

**Khaslana:** It's you...

**Hysilens:** You know them? Good, that makes things easier.

**Khaslana:** Before that, I'm more curious about one thing: Where is Her Majesty Cerydra?

**Hysilens:** To avoid any misunderstandings, nameless swordmaster: Although we spoke and laughed along the way, I never said you were entitled to meet her.

**Hysilens:** The Imperator decreed: "The 'Deliverer' will not appear in this age. Be wary of suspicious individuals."

**Hysilens:** Come now, both of you, let's finish our job.

**Khaslana:** Ah... I see, it's a trap.

**Khaslana:** I had no intention of fighting. So, remember this, Lady Hysilens: If my sword shows any hesitation...

**Khaslana:** Don't thank me — thank them instead.

**Tribbie:** No, you misunderstand.

**Tribbie:** We don't want to see Chrysos Heirs hurt each other. Just give us some time to ask you a few questions.

**Aglaea:** Then, Mnestia's "golden threads" will judge you and decide your fate.

**Khaslana:** ...As you wish.

**Tribbie:** White-haired swordmaster, if what you say is true, then you must know the fate of the world...

**Tribbie:** So, how much do you know about what we read from the prophecy?

**Khaslana:** ...

> *(Trailblazer)* Speak of Tribbie's prophecy.

**Khaslana:** "You shall shatter into a thousand fragments and wither in the soil of foreign lands."

**Khaslana:** ...Is that correct, Lady Tribbie?

**Aglaea:** How...

**Tribbie:** How is this possible...?

**Hysilens:** He got it right?

**Tribbie:** Yes. But the prophecy never mentioned that he'd appear here. At least, not in this era.
