> *(Trailblazer)* Why did you pick me?
>> **Anaxa:** Castorice mentioned you had a great time with the dromases when you first got to Okhema. Anyone the dromases like is a good person in my book.
> *(Trailblazer)* Off-grid? What for?
>> **Anaxa:** I'm researching how to hatch this egg, actually. I have thirty-two promising plans so far, but I need time to test them all.
> *(Trailblazer)* Super Dromas? What's that?
>> **Anaxa:** The strongest, mightiest, most legendary of dromas-kind. The shepherd to all dromases, guiding all beings toward a better future... *Ahem* Let's leave it at that.

***(Trailblazer):*** I did absolutely nothing today. Why am I so tired?
