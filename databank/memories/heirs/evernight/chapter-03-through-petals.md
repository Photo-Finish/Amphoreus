### Listen to the Discussion Between Black Swan and Himeko

**Kind:** personal
**Source:** `databank/missions/chapter-03-through-petals.md`
**Heading:** Chapter 3 — Through the Petals in the Land of Repose (v3.2) > Sub-Mission 11: Scholar, Let Us Meet Again Before the Gates of Truth > Listen to the Discussion Between Black Swan and Himeko
**Lines:** 4594–4648
**Id:** `chapter-03-through-petals__listen-to-the-discussion-between-black-swan-and-himeko`
**Heirs:** evernight (on-stage)

*(March 7th's condition seems to be getting worse. Black Swan still seems to be hiding something... What does Amphoreus mean to the Garden of Recollection? And why is it so important?)*

**Black Swan:** ...Thankfully, she's still alive.
**Himeko:** ...
**Black Swan:** Was March 7th also in this condition when she was first rescued by the Express?
**Himeko:** Her condition was very similar. It's just that at that time, the Express did not have a Memokeeper who could diagnose her condition.
**Black Swan:** But she still woke up naturally. Looking on the bright side, that might happen this time as well.
**Himeko:** When March awakened from the ice, her memories were completely wiped clean. I want to prepare for the worst and prevent something like that from happening again.
**Himeko:** Can you share with us your hypothesis?
**Black Swan:** I do not have conclusive evidence, but judging by her current condition...
**Black Swan:** **Perhaps March 7th's memories have been hijacked.**
**Himeko:** Hijacked?
**Black Swan:** Think about it, Miss Himeko. What will happen when someone loses their ability to remember?
**Himeko:** I'm not a biologist, but I hear that there's of a species of fish on Vonwacq that has the memory span of less than a second. It forgets anything that occurs in the very next instant. Perhaps something similar will happen?
**Black Swan:** Unfortunately, short-term memory is an entirely different concept from the inability to remember. Perhaps it is difficult for you to imagine...
**Black Swan:** But an individual who has lost their ability to remember turns into nothingness.
**Himeko:** Nothingness?
**Black Swan:** The belief of the Garden is that everything in this universe can be interpreted through memories. This is the driving force behind the continuance of this Path.
**Black Swan:** The past is made up of memoria while the future is a possibility that will eventually be converted to memoria. But the present... it doesn't really exist and is only an abstract expression.
**Black Swan:** Therefore, when a person is affected by the Path and loses their ability to remember... their physical form will also be affected. These ice crystals are a manifestation of that.
**Himeko:** ...Miss Black Swan, there is one thing that I haven't looked into deeply until now.
**Himeko:** You've guided us to Amphoreus but refused to tell us your true intentions. "Collecting memories"... is simply not a convincing answer.
**Black Swan:** Is Miss Himeko suspicious of me?
**Himeko:** I mean no offense. I am merely speculating. But I'd appreciate your honesty, if possible.
**Black Swan:** In other words, now is the time for me to prove my innocence...
**Black Swan:** That's not a problem, of course. The true reason behind my intentions is that Amphoreus holds special significance. For some Memokeepers of the Garden with extremist views, it... is a "perfect example."
**Himeko:** Perfect example?
**Black Swan:** A world that can only be seen through the Garden's mirror means that Memokeepers have the right to keep the world in their private collection. If those pathstriders of Remembrance were to uncover the secrets of Amphoreus first...
**Black Swan:** They might unleash their wild ambitions and remodel more worlds into a similar form.
**Himeko:** So, the reason you did everything you could to convince the Express to come to this starzone is just to uncover the secrets and foil the plans of these people?
**Black Swan:** I chose to remain quiet before because I did not want to involve you in the Garden's internal conflicts.
**Himeko:** Remodeling a world is no easy feat. These extremists likely aren't just simple pathstriders. Do they wield the powers of Emanators?
**Black Swan:** You can be even bolder with your guesses. The people that I'm talking about... They aren't just individuals. They are an organization that has already attached itself to the pinky finger of Fuli.
**Himeko:** Could they be the ones who hijacked March 7th's memories?
**Black Swan:** I cannot be certain, but at the very least, they are capable of doing so.
**Himeko:** ...
**Black Swan:** I am deeply sorry about what has happened to March 7th, but the deal that I proposed still remains a fair one. Amphoreus is still an excellent destination, and investigating further might allow us to uncover the truth behind the accident.
**Black Swan:** Miss Himeko, the wisest choice right now is for us to continue cooperating...
**Black Swan:** ...!?
**Himeko:** What's wrong?
**Black Swan:** ...
**Black Swan:** ...Let me take back a part of what I said just now, Miss Himeko.
**Black Swan:** Although we indeed struck a deal, it seems like I have involved you in some unforeseen danger... Please accept my deepest apologies.
**Himeko:** Has something changed?
**Black Swan:** ...
**Black Swan:** **It's Fuli... THEIR gaze has just swept across Amphoreus.**
