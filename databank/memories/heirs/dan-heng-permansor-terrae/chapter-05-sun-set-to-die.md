### Screwllum's Revelation

**Kind:** personal
**Source:** `databank/missions/chapter-05-sun-set-to-die.md`
**Heading:** Chapter 5 — For the Sun is Set to Die (v3.4) > Mission 1 — Hero, Honor That Crimson Call > Screwllum's Revelation
**Lines:** 46–72
**Id:** `chapter-05-sun-set-to-die__screwllum-s-revelation`
**Heirs:** dan-heng-permansor-terrae (spoke)

**Dan Heng:** Phainon... is he going to be alright?

> *(Trailblazer)* Leaving like this... I'll definitely regret it.

**Dan Heng:** I... feel the same. Someone should stay and help Phainon complete Era Nova. For now, let's get back in contact with the Express and plan our next move. That's the safest course of action. We need more information.

**Screwllum (appearing):** On that note, I have a proposal— I hope to convey a message to the two "Nameless." It has been a while, Nameless of the Astral Express. I'm glad I arrived in time to stop that black-clad one from harming you further.

**Screwllum:** I fully understand your situation. For now, the only thing I can do is temporarily freeze that black-clad one in place. Time is limited. So, I'll keep this brief— The place where you stand, Amphoreus, is a world constructed from data and memoria, an experimental field of a certain Aeon. And now, this isolated sandbox world is rapidly falling into a third Path — Destruction.

> *(Trailblazer)* A Lord Ravager... Like Phantylia?

**Screwllum:** The disaster that this Lord Ravager intends to trigger may be far more severe than the Luofu's Stellaron Crisis. Because its goal is... The Aeon of Erudition, Nous.

**Screwllum:** Putting aside the complex details of the inferences involved, I have a request to make, for one of you Nameless, to stay behind on Amphoreus, acting as an informant, in order to prevent the wrath of Destruction from ravaging the cosmos. This world is enveloped in a "firewall" that defies explanation, and the flow of time here is also different from reality. Madam Herta and I can only tear open a gap and project images inside.

> *(Trailblazer)* I will shoulder this responsibility.

**Dan Heng:** Mr. Screwllum, I believe you, so I only have one thing I'd like to confirm with you. As Amphoreus is already facing its end, how can we guarantee the safety of the person who stays behind?

**Screwllum:** Look at this, if you would— This Curio is called a "Chronocognitive Anchor." The two of you may view it as a kind of Space Anchor in the world of Erudition. As long as you carry it with you, your individual data structure will be protected and will remain unaffected by the influence of digital tsunamis.

*(The connection becomes garbled.)*

**Screwllum:** Two ███ final ███ messages. One: The Intellitron ██████ named ███████ Lygus ████ possibly █████ the primary culprit behind everything. Two: Herta ██ discovered a clue ██— Miss March █████ has ████ already been ████ drawn into ███ Amphoreus██

*(Trailblazer obtains Chronocognitive Anchor. Dan Heng departs for the Express.)*
