> **Cyrene:** By the way, you both just mentioned a "banquet," could you elaborate on that?
> **Hysilens:** Oh, right, you don't know. Every time before a battle or after a victory, the Imperator holds a grand feast for the Chrysos Heirs, to lift our spirits.
> **Hysilens:** For us warriors who swim constantly between life and death, these feasts are more worth looking forward to than victory.
> **Aglaea:** Hah... A banquet to prelude the hunt for the god of banquets, and honey brew as a tribute to commemorate the ancestor of honey brew... How absurd.
> **Hysilens:** Don't look so grim. All has been decided. Since it's a banquet for Phagousa, why not take a leaf out of the Titan's book, live in the moment and enjoy life to the fullest?
> **Aglaea:** I'm not as carefree as you. That little Dolos kitten is already causing enough trouble for me, and on top of that, I still have tailoring commissions to complete...
> **Aglaea:** By the time the Coreflame illuminates my eyes, I might not be able to focus as I used to.
> **Cyrene:** Aglaea's eyes...
> **Aglaea:** Curious about these eyes? People often compare them to jewels. But I've already made my decision to ask "Romance" for eyes that can see everything.
> **Aglaea:** I wonder what this world will look like when I take the divine authority?
> **Cyrene:** ...
> **Cyrene:** In the future, you... will still be the finest weaver in the world.
> **Aglaea:** Is this also a prophecy from the Three Fates?
> **Cyrene:** It's even more accurate than a prophecy. After all, (Trailblazer) has already witnessed it, right?
> *(Trailblazer)* You will weave the most romantic thing in the world...
> *(Trailblazer)* You will forge the long stairs to Era Nova...
> *(Trailblazer)* You will bathe in the golden light of hope...
> **Aglaea:** ...
> **Aglaea:** "Become a demigod and lose your humanity." That is the only reply I've received in the countless times I've sought wisdom from the prophecy.
> **Aglaea:** But destiny's fragments are like stitches. Before they're threaded into splendid garments, no one can see their true charm, can they?
