**Mydei Etched in Memory:** If breaking me apart buys this world even one more second... Then so be it.

*(Approach the Fragments of Recollection)*
**Trianne Etched in Memory:** B—But... it's gonna hurt! A lot!
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** Pain brings endless torment, but it can never destroy me.
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** I believe that you were thinking the same thing when you stepped through the Gate of Infinity and jumped off that cliff.
**Trianne Etched in Memory:** Ugh... can't argue with that...
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** In the current situation, this is the only path Okhema has left. Just do as I say...
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** **Split my soul into five pieces, forge me an immortal vessel...**
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** **Let the blade of Strife hang forever overhead, locked in battle with their enemies to the world's bitter end!**
**Trianne Etched in Memory:** ...
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** There is no need to be sad, Lady Tribios. Haven't we already taken great strides towards that promised tomorrow?
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** In this life, my people no longer have to drown in false guidance. Kremnos has washed the blood off its fate.
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** Amphoreus no longer needs a god named Strife. So let me shatter the former laws and turn the Lance of Fury back into a weapon that protects the world.
**Trianne Etched in Memory:** We... understand.
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** By the way, can you do me another favor?
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** The Seven Sages from Grove of Epiphany are already en route. They'll arrive in waves to join the resistance in a couple of days. Among them is a scholar named Anaxagoras...
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** He has extraordinary insight and fears no sacrifice. This Soul-Rending Ceremony wasn't just my decision. It was his counsel. His wisdom will surely be the backbone of Amphoreus' survival.
**Mydei Etched in Memory:** Watch over him. Carry on this everlasting flame for me.
*(narrative)* ...
**Cyrene:** "Through Anaxagoras's ritual, the Demigod of Strife split his soul into five: Courage, Honor, Tenacity, Sacrifice, and Reason, each sealed in blood crystal and cast into battle."
