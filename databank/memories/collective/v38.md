### Become a chimera trainer

**Kind:** collective
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v38.md`
**Heading:** Amphoreus — Adventure Missions (v3.8) > Chrysos Awoo Championship > Dialogue > Become a chimera trainer > Talk to Hyacine
**Lines:** 31–73
**Id:** `v38__become-a-chimera-trainer-2`
**Heirs:** hyacine (spoke), tribbie (spoke)
**Witness note:** full scene lives here; each Heir's personal file keeps a pointer plus what they said/heard.

*(Talk to Hyacine)*
*(Just another day in peaceful Okhema)*
**Hyacine:** Ah, Grayie, you're here! Are you ready for the Chimera World Tournament?
**Tribbie:** With Little Gray's abilities, they'll surely get a high ranking. Hehe, but Lady Tribbie isn't someone to be messed with either.
> *(Trailblazer)* Hold on, let me explain first...
>
> **Hyacine:** Grayie, you must be quite familiar with Little Chimera by now. In Amphoreus, there's a long-standing tradition of arranging battles for chimeras, and those who command them are known as **Chimera Trainers.**
> **Hyacine:** This world tournament is where chimera trainers compete against each other.
> **Tribbie:** Although it's a competition, no chimeras will be harmed. Don't worry, Little Gray. Everyone knows their boundaries!
>
> *(Trailblazer)* Oh, so the chimera's not getting enough work, huh?
>
> **Hyacine:** All work and no play makes even the smartest chimera dull. Grayie, why don't you give them a nice long break?
> **Tribbie:** Combat is part of their job too, Little Gray. Just think of it as team building!
> *(Trailblazer)* They have to fight to survive!
> *(Trailblazer)* Not this kind of team building...
>
> **Hyacine:** All the Little Chimeras from the Garden of Life will participate in this tournament. You'll get to see many familiar faces there, Grayie.
> *(Trailblazer)* How do I enter the tournament?
>
> **Hyacine:** Don't worry, we've already made arrangements for you, Grayie.
> **Tribbie:** Ahem, attention class! Lady Tribbie will now explain everything to you, so pay close attention!
> **Tribbie:** Well, it all started at the finals of the last "Chimera World Tournament"...
---
**Tribbie:** Back then, I faced off against little Imperator in the grand finals.
**Hyacine:** Let me help you review the battle situation!
---
*(After several grueling rounds of battle, Tribbie's chimera squad was finally defeated...)*
**Hyacine:** It was a match that had all of Okhema watching, and I was there to witness it. In the end, the Great Imperator won by a narrow margin.
**Tribbie:** Little Imperator, that's so unfair! That ability is way too overpowered! But hey, after hearing all this, Little Gray must be getting it now, right?
**Tribbie:** The Chimera World Tournament is a tournament where trainers compete against each other with their chimeras. There is a total of thirteen contestants, and with Little Gray joining as a special guest, that makes fourteen!
> *(Trailblazer)* Thirteen... that number rings a bell.
> *(Trailblazer)* So you're the prophesied chimera trainer, huh?
>
> **Hyacine:** Lady Tribbie and I will be there too. We're all your friends, Grayie, so don't be nervous. Just think of it as a game with everyone.
> **Tribbie:** Also, considering you don't have your own chimera, we discussed and decided...
> **Tribbie:** **Little Gray can use any player's lineup to compete. In other words, you have complete freedom!**
> *(Trailblazer)* Select your hero!
> *(Trailblazer)* To give freely is to show true courtesy.
>
> **Hyacine:** I'll explain the more complex rules during actual matches. Lady Tribbie, let's begin now.
> **Tribbie:** All right! Little Gray, join **us** and become a chimera trainer!
> *(Trailblazer)* Go, chimera!
