### Ask Aphrea and Aglaea at the tailor's shop

**Kind:** collective
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v30.md`
**Heading:** Adventure Missions — Version 3.0 (Heroic Saga of Flame-Chase) > I Once Was In Arcadia > Dialogue > Ask Aphrea and Aglaea at the tailor's shop
**Lines:** 403–429
**Id:** `v30__ask-aphrea-and-aglaea-at-the-tailor-s-shop`
**Heirs:** aglaea (spoke), cyrene (spoke)
**Witness note:** full scene lives here; each Heir's personal file keeps a pointer plus what they said/heard.

*(Approach Aphrea)*
**Aglaea:** ...I know that those who revere Mnestia are fond of the so-called "golden thread dress," but weaving clothing out of malleable gold not only makes it very expensive, it also makes it very hard for the wearers to move in. Would it not be better to use dyed silk or cotton?
**Aphrea:** But everyone says that Mnestia's silk is made of gold. Using dyed silk would just be a forgery, wouldn't it?
**Aglaea:** Compared to the creations of the Titans, dyed threads and metal threads are all merely works that imitate the work of the gods. Do you not think that obsessing over the quality of an imitation and ruining the clothes' intended beauty is a sign of disrespect to "romance" itself?
**Aphrea:** I see. Thank you for your guidance, knowledgeable Aglaea... Ah, a customer!
**Aglaea:** Hello, dear customer. Are you here for an item of clothing to suit your physique? Or could it be an answer to a question that you seek?
**Mem:** Woah. You saw right through me. I know you are a lady of great learning, Aglaea. I'm certain you know about what we seek!
> *(Trailblazer)* Do you know Paris?
>> **Aglaea:** Paris? There is indeed no citizen of Okhema by this name. I imagine they are a member of the garrison.
>> **Mem:** Do you know where they are right now, Miss Aglaea?
>> **Aglaea:** Well... It has been decades since I last heard that name. I was away from the holy city for a time and upon my return to Okhema, they were no longer here.
>> **Mem:** Decades? Is he still alive?
>> **Aglaea:** I cannot say for certain. The duration of a life is hard to predict in this world. Perhaps there exists some method to escape Thanatos' touch, or perhaps there is a land that death itself cannot reach.
> *(Trailblazer)* Have you heard of Arcadia?
>> **Aglaea:** Arcadia... Now that is a name that stirs memories.
>> **Aglaea:** A long time ago, this name spread through the streets and alleys of Okhema. People said that it was an ideal city that was protected by the Chrysalis of Gold. In the era of strife, people flocked to it.
>> **Mem:** Then... Do you know how to find this place, Aglaea?
>> **Aglaea:** My apologies, I do not.
>> **Aglaea:** Much like clothing, legends are often things that come and go with the times. Arcadia is already a story of the "past." Many disciples of Mnestia don't even know this name.
>> **Aglaea:** It could be the case, according to one story, that in this war-torn world savaged by the black tide, a flock of butterflies carried that city to a place that no one could see.
>
> *(After selecting both options)*
> **Distant Calling:** Lady Aglaea! Lady Aglaea!
> **Aglaea:** What's wrong, soldier?
> **Reporting Guard:** Iason has returned with supplies, but refuses to enter the city. He says that he is waiting for someone... There is a chance that he failed in the task that you gave to him. Could I trouble you to come and see him yourself?
> **Aglaea:** ...Dear customer, it seems that we must leave our conversation here for now. I hope that you find the answers you seek soon. I bid you farewell.
> **Mem:** These descriptions, along with the statue lady's memories, should be enough for us to return to the past. Let's go and find the statue lady!

### Browse the tailor's shop with Paris and the Furiae Archer

**Kind:** collective
**Source:** `databank/missions/adventure/v30.md`
**Heading:** Adventure Missions — Version 3.0 (Heroic Saga of Flame-Chase) > I Once Was In Arcadia > Dialogue > Browse the tailor's shop with Paris and the Furiae Archer
**Lines:** 733–757
**Id:** `v30__browse-the-tailor-s-shop-with-paris-and-the-furiae-archer`
**Heirs:** aglaea (spoke), cyrene (spoke)
**Witness note:** full scene lives here; each Heir's personal file keeps a pointer plus what they said/heard.

*(Approach Kremnos' Furiae Archer)*
**Furiae Archer:** ...
> *(Trailblazer)* What is she looking at?
> *(Trailblazer)* She's not still collecting those things here, is she?
**Mem:** Shh, there is a familiar scent nearby.
**Furiae Archer:** The city of Okhema... There are so many butterflies here.
**????:** They are nymphs. They are Titankin, like yourself.
**Mem:** That's Aglaea from the past! She seems no different to how she is today?
> *(Trailblazer)* Probably because she's a Chrysos Heir.
> *(Trailblazer)* Probably because of Mnestia's power.
> *(Trailblazer)* We'll have to ask her for her skincare routine.
**Aglaea:** How unusual! A soldier of the Lance of Fury browsing the Marmoreal Market is a rare sight indeed... Welcome to the Eternal City, guest from Kremnos.
**Furiae Archer:** ...Thank you.
**Furiae Archer:** You said that these butterflies are Titankin. Which Titan are they descended from?
**Aglaea:** Well, when it comes to butterflies, they are naturally descendants of Mnestia.
**Aglaea:** Legend has it that Mnestia longed for the love of Cerces, but their love was unrequited. They died with a sense of deep regret. Feeling great pity, Thanatos cast those that had lost their lives to love into a cocoon of romance and allowed them to be reborn as golden butterflies... These were the resulting Titankin.
**Aglaea:** They now work hard for the love of their beloved god. They trace the trail of romance and collect love to heal Cerces for Mnestia's sake...
**Furiae Archer:** Hah. The people here praise Mnestia for their unconditional love. Turns out they are no more than a foolish deity caught in a one-sided infatuation.
**Aglaea:** That is because if you look at "romance" through the eyes of "reason," it is an existence that one cannot explain.
**Furiae Archer:** If you ask me, immersing oneself in a fantasy and forcing that onto another person is no more than acting out a self-produced tragedy... Such a selfish "romance" is no more than "strife" that one suffers alone.
**Aglaea:** ...Perhaps you are right. Many of the things that bring romance to our lives are in fact so unremarkable they are almost crude. However, this is something that those who have fallen in love struggle to realize for themselves. They see a swamp as a sweet spring and rough twine as a golden thread.
**Aglaea:** But, like parasites, the larvae of romance dwell in the hearts of all. We know not when they will metamorphose into butterflies and change our perception from clarity to bedazzlement. This is the blessing that Mnestia grants to all, though it may also be a curse.
**Paris:** It's almost the Parting Hour, girl! Come on!
**Aglaea:** Is that your friend calling you? Farewell. I pray that the golden thread will not hinder your footsteps.
**Furiae Archer:** ...
