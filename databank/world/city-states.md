# The City-States of Amphoreus

> Six major cities stood on Amphoreus during its last cycle: **Okhema**, **Janusopolis**, **Grove of Epiphany**, **Castrum Kremnos**, **Styxia**, and **Aidonia**.
> Each city-state was defined by the Titan it worshipped and the region the Titan governed. Since the coming of the black tide, most have fallen to the eternal night — only Okhema remains as a sanctuary under the light of the Dawn Device.

## Okhema — "Eternal Holy City"

> Humankind established settlements at the feet of Kephale, giving birth to the predecessor of "Okhema."
> Today, it stands as both the sanctuary in the end times and the starting point of the Era Nova journey.
> — Loading Screen, Okhema

> A city that worships Kephale, the Worldbearing Titan who protects this world. Under the eternal night brought by the black tide, this remains the only place where the comfort of dawn can be experienced.
> — Area description, "Eternal Holy City" Okhema

- **Demonym**: Okhemans (Okheman, adjectival)
- **Titan worshipped**: Kephale, the Titan of Worldbearing — though followers of many other Titans can be found within the city.
- **First civilization**: According to legend, Okhema was the **first civilization of Amphoreus**, supported by Kephale and the Dawn Device.
- **Age**: Founded "in the third century of the Light Calendar, when people built settlements at the feet of Kephale." In Light Calendar year 4931, this makes Okhema **almost five thousand years old**.
- **Government**: Governed by the **Council of Elders**, who temporarily transferred their power to Aglaea and the Chrysos Heirs due to the dire situation on Amphoreus.
- **The slumbering Kephale**: Kephale currently slumbers above the city, holding the **Dawn Device**, which keeps the city safe from the night.
- **International hub**: Prior to the black tide, Okhema was expansive, ruling many vassal states stretching to the horizon (including Adian, Sabany, and Alflanke). Since the black tide disaster, it has become a sanctuary for people whose cities have succumbed to night.
- **Marmoreal Palace**: Okhema's great bathhouse, where people enjoy the blessings of Phagousa. Its waters can not only physically cleanse, but can even cure wounds of the heart.

### City Life (Overhear Lore)
- **Migrants and the black tide**: The number of migrants fleeing devoured cities keeps increasing; squads are dispatched to maintain order. "Lots of cities out there are devoured by the black tide. The situation outside... is not looking very good."
- **Council of Elders' expeditions**: Expeditionary squads funded by the Council return from beyond the city — dangerous work; a squad of seven returning unscathed is considered a feat (and sometimes the numbers don't quite add up...).
- **Ceremonial officiants**: Becoming one requires training in the Grove of Epiphany and passing a special examination put in place by the Venerationists. There is only one officiant in all of Okhema.
- **Massage tradition**: Skills passed down from the end of the Era Chrysea — more than two thousand years of history.
- **Baths culture**: Cold baths for "youthful vigor," hot baths for comfort, and a test bath that people keep mistaking for a water dispenser.
- **Sea seals of Phagousa**: The "divine beasts of Phagousa" — giant sea seal dolls, soft to the touch, said to bring good fortune.
- **Chimera care**: Caring for chimeras is "a science" — they are picky eaters; dromases are easier (just feed them soil).
- **Guild smithing**: Custom-made authorities of the Titans (e.g., a Cerces' authority hammer of wood cut along the grain for prayers; Nikador's authority) are forged in the markets.

### Connections
Connected to "Abyss of Fate" Janusopolis, "Demigod Council" Dawncloud, "Dragonbone City" Styxia, "Murmuring Woods" Grove of Epiphany, and the Vortex of Genesis (one-way). *Most of these connections were removed after the mission "Dawn, Shine at the World's End."*

### Etymology
*Okhema* (ὄχημα) is the Neoplatonist "vehicle" which bears the soul within the body.

## Castrum Kremnos — "Capital of Might"

> A massive mobile fortress, the city of warriors who worship Nikador.
> The Kremnoans take pride in fighting to the death and are renowned throughout Amphoreus for their strict discipline.
> — Loading Screen, Castrum Kremnos

- **Demonym**: Kremnoans / Kremnoan (adjectival)
- **Titan worshipped**: Nikador, the Titan of Strife.
- **Nature**: A massive mobile fortress; a battle-oriented society where fighting and strength are valued above all.
- **Areas**: "Bloodbathed Battlefront" Castrum Kremnos (Dawn); "Strife Ruins" Castrum Kremnos (Evernight).

### Overview
- Before Nikador's descent into madness by the black tide, Castrum Kremnos worshipped him for his use of extreme violence to protect the city and bring balance.
- **Losers were intended to die in battle.** Returning to the city in defeat was seen as shameful and traitorous — they would be offered as sacrifices to Nikador to seemingly appease his disappointment.
- Up until the end of the **Chrysos War**, the city regularly held gladiatorial festivals celebrating Nikador.
- The city's **outskirts were constructed by convicts** (per Mydei), leaving some areas particularly questionable in quality.
- **Individual life was not valued** — the souls of the dead fueled the power of Nikador's **Blade of Fury**, where they continued to fight alongside Nikador.
- Governed by a **king**, with approval from the Castrum Kremnoan Council of Elders.
- Known for being very aggressive — it destroyed **Aquila's sky kingdom** and many other city-states.

### History
#### Era Bellica
- Founded by **King Gorgo** and their tribe during their campaign against the Foundation Titans.
- The tribe had followed Nikador against the Foundation Titans, fighting many battles; after receiving a prophecy from a city devoted to Oronyx, they chose to settle down.
- When passing by to fight Aquila, Gorgo took the head of the great lion of Tretos and used its hollow skull as a resonator. This caught Nikador's eye, who bestowed upon the city the **Blade of Fury** — becoming the centerpiece of the later-named Castrum Kremnos, with Gorgo as its first king.
- Official history begins in **Light Calendar year 2506**.
- In **Light Calendar year 2523**, the **Kremnos Arena** finished construction.

#### Era Erasa
- In **year 4931, Month of Balance**, **Mydei** declared the end of the Castrum Kremnos dynasty, with him as the last king.

## Grove of Epiphany — "Home of Sages"

> A courtyard-like structure interspersed with gardens, built around Cerces's manifestation and one of Amphoreus's foremost academic institutions.
> It has been destroyed in an attack by the black tide.
> — Loading Screen, Grove of Epiphany

- **Also known as**: simply "the Grove."
- **Titan worshipped**: Cerces, the Titan of Reason.
- **Areas**: "Radiant Scarwood" Grove of Epiphany (Dawn); "Murmuring Woods" Grove of Epiphany (Evernight).

### Overview
- Founded by **Thalesus, the First Scholar**, who left his home city of **Mileutus** in search of spiritual meaning within the **Mnestia** faith. His transformative journey across Amphoreus led to revelations about nature and existence, culminating in an encounter with Cerces, where he established a courtyard and named it the Grove of Epiphany — a sanctuary for spiritual awakening, philosophical inquiry, and the pursuit of wisdom.
- From Thalesus's teachings, diverse schools of thought emerged, eventually forming the **seven schools of the Grove**, led by **Seven Sages**.
- The Grove became a symbol of collective knowledge — people from all city-states went there to seek wisdom.
- Over time it amassed vast knowledge, becoming Amphoreus' most prestigious academic center — sometimes through legitimate means, sometimes through questionable actions like intellectual theft and espionage.
- The scholars revered reason itself, treating questions as sacred offerings. In silence, Cerces bore witness to the ever-growing "noise" — the Grove's unending exchange of thought — never interrupting the flow of knowledge.
- **Present day**: After Nikador's fall, the Grove was rapidly engulfed by the black tide and its corruption, led by the **Flame Reaver**. Several scholars and Chrysos Heirs died while trying to protect the **Coreflame of Reason**.

### The Schools of the Grove

| School | Overview | Notable Members |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Caprists** | Stewards of nature; propagate and protect bizarre beasts and forests, ensuring harmony between humanity and the environment. | — |
| **Erythrokeramists** | Explore philosophical questions about souls and existence; take charge of artistic education to nurture creativity and perception. | Socrippe (Sage) |
| **Helkolithists** | Dedicated to physical and mental discipline; study combat and train to achieve balance and mastery of mind-body unity. | Stagira |
| **Lotophagists** | Experts in plants and medicine; cultivate both physical and mental health through medicine and meals. | Medea (Sage) |
| **Nodists** | Assert that "all things are numbers"; regard mathematics as the means by which Cerces governs the world. | Apuleius (Sage), Nicomachus, Metitalus |
| **Nousporists** | Specialize in the transformation and ascension of life and matter; primarily research the soul. Their study revolves around the theory that all beings originate from the same source and have the same composition. | Anaxa (Sage, founder), Hyacine, Castorice, Phainon |
| **Venerationists** | Guardians of tradition; oversee rituals, navigate political dynamics, and create ceremonial artifacts to maintain the Grove's spiritual connection to the divine. | Empedocles (Sage), Anaxa (formerly) |

*(Note: The Grove is canonically said to have formed "seven schools" — all seven are listed above. The INDEX "Six Schools" entry refers to the schools of the Grove generally.)*

### Etymology
- **Lotophagist** — from the lotophages, the "lotus-eaters."
- **Caprist** — possibly from *caprae* (Latin for "goat"), connecting to Pan.
- **Nodist** — possibly from *nod* (Latin, "knot").
- **Helkolithic** — from *helkuo* (ἑλκύω, "to draw, drag, or lead") + *lithos* (λίθος, "stone").
- **Erythrokeramist** — from *eruthrós* (ἐρυθρός, "red") + *keramos* (κέραμος, "potter's clay"/"tile").
- **Nousporist** — from *nous* (νοῦς), the classical concept of "intellect."

## Styxia — "Pearly Shores" / "City-State of Dragons and Waves"

> Styxia, the City-State of Dragons and Waves, now lies in ruins.
> Countless lost souls remain unable to cross to the other shore, while the River of Souls rises and never recedes. Thanatos, whose fate remains unknown, is intricately tied to this place.
> — Loading Screen, Dragonbone City

- **Demonym**: Styxians / Styxian (adjectival)
- **Titan worshipped**: Phagousa, the Titan of Ocean — before the city was attacked by an evil dragon.
- **Areas**: "Warbling Shores" Styxia (Dawn); "Dragonbone City" Styxia (Evernight).

### Overview
- Located near the **River of Souls**; its inhabitants were mostly **alchemists**.
- The city was destroyed by an **evil dragon — Pollux**.
- Countless lost souls remain unable to cross to the other shore; the River of Souls rises and never recedes.
- **Thanatos**, the Death Titan, whose fate remains unknown, is intricately tied to this place.

### Known Characters
- **Thanatos** — Death Titan
- **Pollux** — the evil dragon that destroyed the city

### Etymology
*Styxia* originates from *Styx* (Στύξ), the river of the underworld in Greek mythology.

## Aidonia — "Snow City" / "The Frozen Snow City"

- **Titan worshipped**: Thanatos, the Titan of Death (one of the twelve Titans).

### Overview
- Aidonia is known as the **snow-dusted kingdom of death**, located in Amphoreus.
- It was established by **Eleusis**, the leader of the **Ascetics**, who sought an end to the endless cycles of life.
- The eerie city is deeply connected to the faith of Thanatos.
- The **Aidonians built colossal monuments on the snow plains** to honor the Holy Maiden who brings them the blessing of death — who is currently **Castorice**.
- Related readables: "Rising and Falling of River of Souls (Fragment)," "What We Know About Death."
- Related relic set: Aidonia's Deathward Bone Chains, Aidonia's Deceased Gravestones.

### Known Characters
- **Castorice** — the current Holy Maiden bearing the blessing of death
- **Amunet** (deceased)

### Etymology
*Aidonia* (Αηδόνια, "nightingales") shares its name with several real-world Greek villages, but the in-game location likely comes from the Ancient Greek epithet **Aïdōnia** (Ἀϊδωνια), "of Hades," derived from a feminization of *Aïdōneús* (Ἀϊδωνεύς), a poetic variation on Hades' name.

## Appendix: Amphoreus World Context

> Amphoreus, The Eternal Land is a segmentum hidden deep in the cosmos. It is severed from the outside and hard to detect or be encountered.
> — Honkai: Star Rail Official Website

- Amphoreus is the **fifth accessible world** in *Honkai: Star Rail*, accessed upon completing "A New Venture on the Eighth Dawn."
- It is an **isolated sandbox world** created by **Scepter δ-me13** under the administration of **Lygus**, in a project known as **The Amphoreus Experiment**.
- The world was constructed using **memoria and data**; core factors operate as electrical signals.
- The experiment had extrapolated at least **28,371,292 cycles** of Amphoreus by the time of the Astral Express' arrival.
- The last stage, "**Era Nova**," involved **twelve factors** loaded into Amphoreus as Titans and Coreflames, with associated Chrysos Heirs tasked to retrieve each Coreflame in an attempt to save the world from the **black tide** — the black tide being a form of the **Destruction Equation**.
- Following "As Tomorrow Became Yesterday," Scepter δ-me13 was destroyed; Amphoreus is emerging from the Memory Zone, being reborn through Remembrance. Herta claims spreading the story of Amphoreus will quicken its creation.
