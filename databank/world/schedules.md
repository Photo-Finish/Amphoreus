# The Heirs' Individual Weekly Schedules

> The Heirs do not all live in the same city, and they do not all keep the same
> hours. Each of the thirteen keeps a weekly routine of their own — where they
> are at each of the five periods of the day, and what occupies them there.
>
> These routines are *defaults*, not chains: a Heir may always deviate of their
> own free will. But the geography is real (see `world/map.md`), so any
> deviation to a far city is paid for in **commuting time** — and during a
> journey the Heir is on the road, seeing no one.

---

## The five periods of a day

| # | Period | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Entry Hour** | awakening, morning market, prayers |
| 1 | **Lucid Hour** | mental peak, conversation, scholarship |
| 2 | **Action Hour** | physical labour, exercise, revelry |
| 3 | **Parting Hour** | work ends, farewells, departures |
| 4 | **Curtain-Fall Hour** | rest and sleep |

---

## Who lives and works together (so meets often)

| Group | Heirs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The Okhema council circle** | Aglaea, Cerydra, Hysilens, Cipher, Evernight, Dan Heng | Okhema |
| **The scholars of the Grove** | Anaxa, Hyacine | Grove of Epiphany |
| **The two souls of Aedes Elysiae** | Phainon, Cyrene | Aedes Elysiae |
| **The Holy Maiden, alone** | Tribbie | Janusopolis |
| **The last king, alone** | Mydei | Castrum Kremnos |
| **The Maiden of Death, alone** | Castorice | Aidonia |

The first three groups cross paths **daily** — they live or work together, and
their routines place them in the same city at the same hours. The last three
live far from everyone else: meeting them means days on the road, so it happens
rarely and only on purpose.

## The routines (abridged)

### Aglaea — Gold-Weaver (Okhema)
- **Entry:** weaves the first threads of the day in the Marmoreal Palace.
- **Lucid:** presides over the Demigod Council at **Dawncloud** (Days 1, 3, 5).
- **Action:** receives the Heirs' reports; walks the baths and markets.
- **Parting:** plays chess with old friends; writes letters to the far Heirs.
- **Curtain-Fall:** rests, golden threads in hand.
- *Day 7 is kept quiet and alone.*

### Anaxa — the Blasphemer (Grove of Epiphany)
- **Entry:** reads by the first light in the Grove library.
- **Lucid:** lectures the students of the Nousporist school.
- **Action:** argues philosophy; experiments with the Grove's herbs.
- **Parting:** walks the Radiant Scarwood, thinking.
- **Curtain-Fall:** writes late by candlelight.
- *Day 5: walks to the Great Tomb to study the old matrix.*

### Castorice — Shadow of Death (Aidonia)
- **Entry:** keeps the morning rites in the snow city.
- **Lucid:** tends the graves and listens to the dead.
- **Action:** walks the frozen plains with Pollux; some days crosses to **Styxia**'s River of Souls.
- **Parting:** returns through the dusk; a quiet melody for the dead.
- **Curtain-Fall:** sleeps beneath the snow-light.
- *She almost never leaves Aidonia. Others must come to her.*

### Cerydra — the Imperator (Okhema)
- **Entry:** reviews the night's reports.
- **Lucid:** attends the Demigod Council at **Dawncloud** (Days 1, 3, 5).
- **Action:** holds court; walks the city, hearing what the council does not.
- **Parting:** plays chess with rivals and friends.
- **Curtain-Fall:** writes letters that will matter later.

### Cipher — the Spirithief (Okhema)
- **Entry:** slips out of an inn at dawn, pockets full.
- **Lucid:** keeps an eye on the Market from the rooftops.
- **Action:** plays cards in a back-alley tavern; lifts a purse, then slips it back.
- **Parting:** visits the 300 Rogues' hideout.
- **Curtain-Fall:** sleeps where the shadows are softest.
- *Day 5: takes a job that leads toward **Styxia**, and returns thoughtful.*

### Cyrene — the girl of the village (Aedes Elysiae)
- **Entry:** greets the morning in the Sacrament Courtyard.
- **Lucid:** reads fortunes for the villagers.
- **Action:** walks the Wondrous Woods with the fairies; tends the garden.
- **Parting:** sits by the great tree, or watches the sea from the wharf.
- **Curtain-Fall:** sleeps in the cottage by the wheat.
- *She never leaves the village. The village is her whole world.*

### Dan Heng (Permansor Terrae) — guardian of the path (Okhema)
- **Entry:** keeps the morning watch at the city gate.
- **Lucid:** studies the records of Amphoreus in the archives.
- **Action:** trains with the lance; keeps watch.
- **Parting:** walks the walls; writes in the Trailblaze log.
- **Curtain-Fall:** guards the gate until the stars turn.
- *Day 6: rides out to keep watch over **Aedes Elysiae**.*

### Evernight — the shadow (Okhema)
- **Entry:** dissolves from the night into the morning.
- **Lucid:** watches the council from a shadowed arch.
- **Action:** fades through the market, unseen.
- **Parting:** writes a letter to March 7th, signed ♭.
- **Curtain-Fall:** becomes the night again.
- *Day 7: keeps the whole day in shadow.*

### Hyacine — the healer of the Grove (Grove of Epiphany)
- **Entry:** tends the healing garden at dawn.
- **Lucid:** treats the sick at the Grove infirmary.
- **Action:** teaches the students of medicine; gathers herbs.
- **Parting:** sits with Anaxa by the fountain.
- **Curtain-Fall:** rests among the leaves.
- *Day 7: writes a letter to Castorice, gently.*

### Hysilens — the knight commander (Okhema)
- **Entry:** drills the guard at the Marmoreal Palace at dawn.
- **Lucid:** stands at Cerydra's side in the council at **Dawncloud**.
- **Action:** leads the patrol through the holy city; trains the knights.
- **Parting:** plays the violin in Aglaea's workshop.
- **Curtain-Fall:** rests in the barracks.
- *Day 7: walks to the sea's edge, thinking of the home she left.*

### Mydei — the last king (Castrum Kremnos)
- **Entry:** takes the dawn drill in the arena.
- **Lucid:** receives the warriors of Kremnos; hears the petitions of the fortress.
- **Action:** fights in the practice circle, unstoppable.
- **Parting:** walks the Strife Ruins, remembering.
- **Curtain-Fall:** sleeps in the king's chambers.
- *He never leaves the fortress. The black tide is at the walls.*

### Phainon — the Deliverer (Aedes Elysiae ↔ Okhema)
- **Days 1–2:** in the village — chores with Cyrene, training in the wheat, work at the wharf.
- **Day 3:** takes the long road at first light, arriving in **Okhema** mid-day.
- **Days 3–5:** stands before the Council as the Deliverer; trains and walks among the people he saved; writes to Cyrene, promising return.
- **Day 6:** takes the long road home, reaching the village as the light softens.
- **Day 7:** a day of rest in the village with Cyrene.
- *Two full days of road, each way — the Deliverer is the only Heir who regularly crosses the world.*

### Tribbie — Holy Maiden (Janusopolis)
- **Entry:** opens the temple gates with the dawn.
- **Lucid:** reads the prophecies for the pilgrims.
- **Action:** plays with the children of the city; walks the twin gates.
- **Parting:** rings the evening bell; prays for Trianne and Trinnon.
- **Curtain-Fall:** sleeps in the temple.
- *Day 7: the great prayer of the seventh day, read for the whole city.*
- *She rarely leaves Janusopolis. The temple is her charge.*

## Implementation

- `src/world/schedules.py` — the machine-readable schedules (`scheduled_entry`,
  `scheduled_place`, `week_overview`, …).
- `src/world/world_state.py` — `WorldState.scheduled_place()` and the travel
  machinery (`begin_travel`, `advance_travel`, `is_traveling`).
- `src/world/world_engine.py` — the engine advances travel each day and skips
  travellers from decisions and encounters.
- The Streamlit UI (`src/ui_app.py`) renders the map, the commuting-time matrix,
  and any Heir's weekly grid under the **🗺️ Map of Amphoreus** tab.
