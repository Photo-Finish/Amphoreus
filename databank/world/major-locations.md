# Major Locations of Amphoreus

> Beyond the six city-states, Amphoreus holds other great places: the council seat in the clouds, the village frozen in time, the sanctuary of the primal Coreflames, the tomb that buries "all worlds," and the fallen sky kingdom of Aquila.

---

## "Demigod Council" Dawncloud

> The spiritual and political center of Okhema, where priests gaze upon the Worldbearing Titan, and Elders debate endlessly in the circular assembly hall.

- **Region**: Dawn
- **Evernight counterpart**: "Lightless Chapel" Dawncloud
- **Connections**: "Eternal Holy City" Okhema; "Fortress of Dome" Eye of Twilight (both connections removed during "Dawn, Shine at the World's End")
- **Explorable during**: "Olive, Cast to the Conference Chair"; fully unlocked in "Debate, Discourse Without Spears"

### Overview
- The seat of Okhema's **citizens' assembly** and the **Council of Elders** — where priests gaze upon Kephale (slumbering on the Titan Cliff) and elders debate in the circular assembly hall.
- Collectables: 14 Treasures, 10 Creation Nymph, 3 Golden Scapegoat, 1 Hand of Zagreus (Trail of Whimsy), 2 Prophecy Tablet, 2 Spirithief.

### Key Sub-Areas
- **Sacred Path of Dawncloud** — the processional way up the sacred cliff.
- **Central Assembly Hall** — the circular debating chamber.
- **Titan Cliff** — where Kephale's slumbering form can be seen; includes the Worldbearing Altar: Audience Hall.
- **Demigod Council: Forecourt**
- **Hand of Zagreus** — a puzzle/landmark dedicated to the Trickery Titan.

### Local Life (Listenable Lore)
- **Citizens' assembly**: "No matter what comes of the citizens' assembly, it stands as the promised result given unto us by Kephale." The Council of Elders has been accused of refusing to admit assembly results; some citizens accuse the Council of never taking the prophecy seriously.
- **Festival of Phagousa**: Okhema's playwrights showcase new works; the audience decides the best script and performers. The last award-winning play featured a prince who slew his father and married his mother — saved by a mechanical "deus ex machina" of Talanton descending from the sky (to Thalia's skepticism).
- **Rumors of the Dawn Device**: A man named Moiton claims Kephale asked to speak with him "privately after the Dawn Device goes out" — dismissed as heresy or a follower of Zagreus ("If I believed you... then I might as well be a Flying Amphora").
- **Lament for the old world**: "O beautiful old world, where are you now? O legendary Era Chrysea, return to the mortal realm!"

### Verax Leo
- A riddle-loving lion at Dawncloud ("Bucca Leonis"). Players can test it with questions; the answer to "the creature most beloved by Okhema's citizens" is, inevitably, the lion itself. He claims to have once roamed Castrum Kremnos, where he "held martial honor in high regard."

## Aedes Elysiae

> A remote village in a certain part of Amphoreus. It is protected by the Veil of Evernight and was left behind outside of the rest of the world. Wheat fields, windmills, and the ocean watch over this tranquil and serene moment.

- **Region**: Outworld
- **Unlocked after**: "Hero, Sing That Anthem of Creation"
- **Playable characters**: Cyrene, Phainon

### Overview
- A **peaceful village frozen in time** — the home of **Cyrene** and **Phainon**. Time passes very slowly there; it was the first place the Trailblazer awakened after the light of Era Nova.
- Behind the **Sacrament Courtyard** stands a great tree, beneath which lies a magical maze home to the fairies (the **Membrance Maze**).
- Grain and flour are key exports, shipped out by boat from **Voyager's Wharf**.
- Collectables: 15 Treasures, 10 Creation Nymph, 2 Golden Scapegoat, 2 Prophecy Tablet, 2 Spirithief.

### Landmarks
- **Sacrament Courtyard** — central square; site of a faceless statue of the Time Titan (Oronyx), like those in the Abyss of Fate; Cyrene's welcome scarecrow stands here.
- **Voyager's Wharf** — the village pier; the Trailblazer fishes with Cyrene here.
- **Membrance Maze** — a magical maze beneath the great tree, home to fairies.
- **Wondrous Woods** — woodland with wind chimes (All-Channel Resonance).
- **Garmentmaker** — the village's garmentmaker outpost.

### Significance
- The village where **Cyrene**, bearer of the power of "Time," once lived — she sacrificed herself to reset the flow of time, weaving an endless dream for Amphoreus.
- The village of **Phainon**, once hailed as the Deliverer — he carried the Coreflame within himself across thirty million cycles of rebirth to defy the world's doom, and entrusted the fate of salvation to the Trailblazer.
- In the 33,550,337th recurrence, Aedes Elysiae did **not** fall to the black tide, as the Flame Reaver drove the tide back to protect his sense of self.
- The **Little Chimera** (pet) lives here near the swing, bringing fallen leaves and nuzzling baby seals.

## Vortex of Genesis

> The starting point of the world, hidden by the waves. A grand sanctuary that houses the primal divinity of the Twelve Titans. It's also the promised land that will see the descent of the Miracle of Genesis, as prophesized by the oracles.

- **Region**: Outworld
- **Collectables**: 2 Treasures

### Overview
- The **sanctuary of the primal Coreflames** — the starting point of the world, hidden by the waves.
- Where the Chrysos Heirs gather the Coreflames to create the world anew; where Phainon submitted the Coreflame of Worldbearing; where the final confrontation with the **Flame Reaver (Khaslana)** took place; where Cipher and Anaxa later entrap **Lygus** (he is sealed within the Vortex for ~250 years).
- Features **Tidal Bounty** and **Voice of Constellation** exploration mechanics.
- Exploration flavor (Collecting Thoughts): the Trailblazer reflects here on Phainon, the black tide, Tribbie's past, Mydei, the Flame Reaver, Anaxa's truth of the Era Nova ("The New World is built upon the remnants of the old... The Titans... were once no more than mere mortals"), Castorice ("Daughter of the River of Souls"), Hyacine supporting the sky dome, and Cipher's fate.

## "Universal Matrix" Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan

> The deepest ruin in Amphoreus, the great tomb where pasts of "all worlds" are buried. Stripped of its "mythical" embellishments, the colossal storage matrix flickers incessantly, recording an unknown past.

- **Region**: Dawn
- **Evernight counterpart**: "Nightmare's Echo" Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan
- **Connection**: "Radiant Scarwood" Grove of Epiphany
- **Unlocked during**: "Great Tomb, Hide the Secrets of Incarnations"

### Overview
- The **deepest ruin in Amphoreus** — a colossal storage matrix where the pasts of "all worlds" are buried. In truth, it functions as the matrix in which **Cyrene preserves the memories of all Eternal Recurrences** — and where the **Demiurge** was imprisoned before awakening as Mem.
- Reveals the mechanical truth of the world: the Trailblazer discovers "hard drives" behind walls, circuit-board patterns in the Data Advection Sector, and runes of the **Three Titans of Foundation** (Ocean, Sky, Earth) above the Compass on the path to the **Demiurge Matrix**.
- Collectables: 16 Treasures, 3 Golden Scapegoat, 2 Prophecy Tablet, 2 Spirithieves, 10 Creation Nymph.
- Activities: Cavern of Corrosion: Path of Hidden Salvation; Hand of Zagreus; Miracle Orbs; Shrine of Prayers; Tome of Miracles.
- Enemies: Ichor Memosprite: Canine; Ichor Memosprite: Pheasant; Lordly Trashcan (during "Spirithief" Bartholos encounter).

### Key Sub-Areas
- **Space-Time Superposition Room**
- **Data Advection Sector** — circuit-board-like patterns; "The world is a massive data component, and all the information one can access in a lifetime is merely one billionth of it."
- **Tidal Cooling Sector** — a flooded chamber with fans, described as "cosmic feng shui" in the Emperor's Scepter.
- **Demiurge Matrix** — the prison of the Demiurge.

## Eye of Twilight — "Sky Castrum"

> The city-state in the clouds, named after a Titan and once the dwelling of Aquila, looks down upon the world from the skies. Murals within reflect Amphoreus' shifting skies, hinting at secrets long buried in starlight.

- **Titles**: Sky Castrum
- **Areas**: "Fortress of Dome" Eye of Twilight (Dawn); "Cloudedge Bastion Ruins" Eye of Twilight (Evernight)

### Overview
- The city-state **in the clouds**, established by humans out of reverence for **Aquila**, Titan of Sky.
- Within lies a **Celestial Mural** said to depict Aquila's mood, capable of altering Amphoreus' weather. The city-state was **powered by golden magma**.
- One of the most prominent and powerful city-states during the **Era Bellica**; Aquila and the Sky Castrum fought against Nikador in the Chrysos War, able to stand toe-to-toe with Castrum Kremnos itself.

### The Fall of the Sky Castrum
- After the black tide's arrival, Aquila began **ascending into the skies to avoid it**, sparking discord among the **Skyfolk** tribes — particularly between the **Sunfolk** and **Rainfolk**. The Sunfolk interpreted Aquila's actions as divine favor, leading to the persecution of the Rainfolk.
- **Seliose the Daythunder Knight**, with her companions and steeds **Solabis** and **Lunabis**, challenged Aquila to end the strife. Upon defeating Aquila and touching the **Coreflame of Sky**, Seliose learned of Aquila's **cold indifference and loathing for humanity** — and, seeing the Skyfolk persecute one another even in her name, **lost all faith in humanity**.
- She commanded her two steeds to serve as executioners, casting **all but one** of the Chrysos Heir Skyfolk into the golden magma.
- The city fell into ruin; **Seliose, fused with Aquila**, remained behind, awaiting the return of the one Skyfolk descendant she had spared on a whim — the unnamed ancestor of **Hyacine**.
