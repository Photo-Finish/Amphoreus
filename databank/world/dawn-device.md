# The Dawn Device

> The light of Okhema — the only source of dawn under the eternal night of the black tide.

## Overview

The **Dawn Device** is a device on Amphoreus, carried by the Worldbearing Titan, **Kephale**, inside Okhema.

- The Amphoreans believe it was **forged by Aquila, Titan of Sky**, as a gift to Kephale — who abandoned their throne to become a ray of light for the people of Okhema, bearing the Dawn Device.
- With its help, the people of Okhema built a strong city-state and became the **origin of the Amphoreus people** (Amphoreus' first civilization).
- The people of Amphoreus use its light for **astrology** — deducing changes in the light to correspond with the positions of the world (see "Janusopolis Journeyman Astrology").
- During the **sixth month** (Month of Everday), the Dawn Device shines noticeably brighter, contributing to the growth of crops and the well-being of the people.

## The Black Tide and Kephale's Sacrifice

- When the Black Tide's corrosion swept across Amphoreus and **Aquila closed their eyes**, sending Amphoreus into a state of night, **Kephale sacrificed their Coreflame to ignite the Dawn Device**.
- Since then, the Dawn Device has been **Okhema's only source of light**.

## The Lie of Cipher — "Slate, Why Neglect That Light's Shade"

- Unbeknownst to most, the Dawn Device was slated to **shut down 300 years after its activation**.
- Kephale attempted to warn **Phoros**, an Okheman chaplain, through a vision — but Phoros lacked the courage to reveal the truth. On his deathbed he asked his apprentice **Atticus** to relay the secret.
- In truth, the "Atticus" he spoke to was **Cipher in disguise**, in his room looking for things to steal. After Phoros's death, Cipher **lied to the Okheman people** that the Dawn Device's light would last forever — and used her powers as the demigod of **Trickery** to make the lie real, sustaining the Dawn Device **700 years past its original shutdown date**.
- After **Cipher's death at the hands of the Flame Reaver**, her lie unraveled: the Dawn Device shut down, allowing the **Black Tide to invade Okhema**.

## Periods of a Day

The Dawn Device shifts its light five times during a day in Amphoreus:

- **Entry Hour** — the time period when various activities gradually begin.
- **Lucid Hour / Ascent Hour** — the period of the day when one is most awake.
- **Action Hour / Descent Hour** — the period when enlightenment of the mind starts to wither.
- **Parting Hour** — the period when production activities start to come to rest.
- **Curtain-Fall Hour** — the period of pillowed rest.
