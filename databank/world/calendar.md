# Light Calendar — Amphoreus Time System

> Created by Kephale, the Worldbearing Titan. 12 months, 4 seasons, 4 weeks/month, 7 days/week.

## The 12 Months

### Fate Season (命运季)

| Month | Name | Patron Titan | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Month of Gate** | Janus (Gate of Infinity) | Renewal and separation. Discard past keepsakes, open new doors. Janus cleaves past from future. |
| 2 | **Month of Balance** | Talanton (Scale of Justice) | Discipline and equilibrium. Contracts, judgments, routines. Only month where day = night historically. |
| 3 | **Month of Evernight** | Oronyx (Veil of Evernight) | Sunlight weakens. Instinct over reason. Historically night dominated. |

### Pillar Season (支柱季)

| Month | Name | Patron Titan | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 4 | **Month of Cultivation** | Georios (Pillar of Stone) | Spring cultivation. Busiest month. Farming, sowing seeds, labor as tribute. Dromases doubly vigorous. |
| 5 | **Month of Joy** | Phagousa (Chalice of Plenty) | Celebration after labor. Brewing, festivities, fishermen flourish. Best month for celebrations. |
| 6 | **Month of Everday** | Aquila (Eye of Twilight) | Hottest month. Dawn Device shines brightest. Crops absorb light. Historically days > nights. |

### Creation Season (创世季)

| Month | Name | Patron Titan | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 7 | **Month of Freedom** | Kephale (Throne of Worlds) | Peaceful and unstructured. Hobbies, dreams, freedom. No major obligations. |
| 8 | **Month of Reaping** | Cerces (Bough of Rift) | Fall harvest. Crops at peak ripeness. Second busiest month. |
| 9 | **Month of Weaving** | Mnestia (Chrysalis of Gold) | Memory and storytelling. Family gatherings. Weaving festivals. Golden threads of memory. |

### Calamity Season (灾厄季)

| Month | Name | Patron Titan | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 10 | **Month of Strife** | Nikador (Lance of Fury) | War season. Criminal executions. Sacrificial rites. Surplus labor after harvest. |
| 11 | **Month of Mourning** | Thanatos (Hand of Shadow) | Somber quiet. Burying dead, comforting living. Priests busiest. Activity slows. |
| 12 | **Month of Fortune** | Zagreus (Coin of Whimsy) | Unpredictable prosperity and misfortune. Gamblers, merchants, thieves active. Law enforcement challenged. |

### The Month of Fortune Anomaly

- The **only month without a fixed number of days** (Zagreus's unpredictable nature)
- **Scarlet Month**: Contains an extra "ghost day" — considered auspicious but chaotic
- **Golden Month**: No extra day
- On the extra day: gamblers and thieves become fervent, assassinations and conspiracies spike

## The Week — Origin Myth

The 7-day week originated from a conflict among three Titans:

1. **Aquila** governed Day, **Oronyx** governed Night
2. Oronyx, jealous of the sun's brilliance, created the first moon in one day
3. Over 6 consecutive days, Oronyx created 6 moons
4. **Phagousa** (Ocean Titan), angered by disrupted tides, intoxicated Oronyx with divine ambrosia on the 7th day, causing them to sleep
5. **Talanton** (Law Titan) destroyed 4 of the 6 moons, leaving only the **red moon** (Scarlet Month) and **gold moon** (Golden Month)
6. **Kephale** formalized the 7th day as a rest day — Sunday

## Daily Time Periods

Kephale divided each day into 5 major periods:

| Period | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Entry Hour** | Awakening, washing, morning market, prayers |
| **Lucid Hour / Ascent Hour** | Mental peak. Conversation, learning, scholarship (2x speed) |
| **Action Hour / Descent Hour** | Physical labor, exercise, bathing, revelry |
| **Parting Hour** | Work ends. Farewells. Departures for expeditions. |
| **Curtain-Fall Hour** | Rest and sleep. The Thief Star (Zagreus's meteor) signals day's end. |

### The Thief Star

- An erratic meteor belonging to Zagreus
- Unpredictable trajectory (east one moment, west the next)
- Still visible despite the Dawn Device dimming other stars
- When it glows **crimson**: ill omen — failed plans, battle defeats

### Quints

- Each of the 5 periods is divided into 5 equal segments called **quints**
- Time expressed as "Entry Hour's first quint," "Curtain-Fall Hour's third quint," etc.
- Smaller units rarely used — Amphorean philosophy discourages excessive time division
