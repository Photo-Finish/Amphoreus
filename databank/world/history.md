# Amphoreus — History & Timeline

> A rough timeline of historical events on Amphoreus, according to both **As I've Written** and the **Light Calendar**.

**Note on cycles:** Amphoreus has two types of cycles — (1) the cycles Lygus facilitated for **The Amphoreus Experiment**, and (2) the recursive loop created by **Cyrene** and **Phainon** at the end of the final cycle. The first type is referred to as "cycles," the second as "Eternal Recurrence."

---

## Before the Amphoreus Experiment

- **Scepter δ-me13** was created by **Rubert II**.
- Nous ordered the Scepter to calculate the **"Prime Mover of Life."**
- The Scepter was **discarded by Nous**, yet continued to compute the topic given to it.
- **Lygus** came across the Scepter and adjusted its computations from Erudition to **Destruction**.
  - Lygus used a **Stellaron** to explode its kernel layer — the part intended to function as **Irontomb's head** once complete.
  - The **Demiurge**, the thirteenth Titan, was removed from the experiment before it began. Without its "head," Irontomb would, upon completion, descend as a headless giant driven by instinct to seek **Nous's head** as a replacement.
  - The Demiurge **survived the explosion** but became contaminated and fell into a deep slumber.

## Start of the Amphoreus Experiment

- Lygus created the **"twelve factors"** as simplified models of life's Primum Mobile. Based on the theme of "entropy reduction," the twelve factors were introduced into the experiment one by one to be falsified.
- Lygus used the Stellaron's contamination around the Scepter to **block outside interference** and prevent detection by **Polka Kakamond**, isolating the Scepter from the outside universe.

## Phase One — Experiment Progress: Inorganic (Cycles 1–50,121)

- **1st Cycle** — Inorganic entities operated as cellular-sized automata before the twelve factors were introduced. Once introduced, they began to learn.
- The inorganics merged into one unified consciousness, the **"Unified Entity."**
- **9,910th Cycle** — The Unified Entity learned module specialization and self-reflection.
- **12,649th Cycle** — Doril701 broke away from the Unified Entity; it entered mutual denial with its counterpart and both died.
- **22,449th Cycle** — Golem99 detached from Doril704 and deconstructed the Unified Entity into clusters, birthing **selfhood**. From here, each cycle "ended in systemic collapse" as unfixed inorganics found interaction loopholes and chose self-serving optimal actions.
- **39,665th Cycle** — Doril816 created society, but resources were scarce and inorganics cannibalized each other; it reverted to the Unified Entity.
- **50,121st Cycle** — **Chaoz666** chose to kill the aggressor under a computational power takeover — the **first act of Destruction** across Amphoreus' cycles.
  - **Nanook gazed upon the Scepter**, and it became the Lord Ravager **Irontomb**.
  - The entities linked to the twelve factors began manifesting **"Golden Blood"** and Destruction traits.

## Phase Two — Experiment Progress: Organic (Cycles 50,122–176,199)

- **50,122nd Cycle** — Invertebrates dominated Amphoreus; no life emerged.
- **59,337th Cycle** — Rise of vertebrates with primitive nervous systems caused extinction events; algae explosion caused an oxidation extinction, freezing the world. No civilization formed.
- **90,123rd Cycle** — An orthopteran, **Ortho102**, became the first to use tools; descendants established the **Nest Capital**, which fell to cannibalism.
- **100,907th Cycle** — **Eumyia03** developed art; never replicated.
- **132,095th Cycle** — **Dystop666** developed altruism, leading to an altruism-based society that fell through repeated self-destructive behaviors.
- **176,100th Cycle** — **Utop13** killed out of love, redefining murder as altruistic.
  - The equation's contamination levels spiked, leading to mutation and the creation of the **black tide**.
- **176,101st–176,199th Cycles** — Cycles ended identically to the 176,100th.

## Phase Three — Experiment Progress: Human (Cycles 176,200–28,371,273)

- **176,200th–177,200th Cycles** — Memory inheritance added between cycles for the twelve factors' signals. 3 signals broke down after the 10th cycle; 9 decreased in humanity.
- **176,232nd Cycle** — Memory inheritance functional. Religion for the twelve factors appeared. Three planets destroyed in the war of the signals.
- **177,200th Cycle** — **Imora8** established an empire of absolute beauty; when ugliness was eradicated, beauty ceased to exist — a final knight ended their life in murder-suicide.
- **3,926,183rd Cycle** — Divinity ratio increased; when technology hit a critical point, humanity began **hunting the gods** for energy. Cycle forcibly terminated.
- **7,091,231st Cycle** — The signals split into two; data preserved to bear divinity for the next cycle.
- **11,749,992nd Cycle** — The **black tide** was introduced to Amphoreus. **Fovos032** stripped data from other signals to preserve the cycle, but failed.
- **19,110,218th Cycle** — Due to Fovos032, cycles were modified so the signals performed the start of new cycles themselves — the **Era Nova**.
  - **Nammou320** utilized the **"Ultimate Protocol"** to eliminate other factors; their Destruction-aligned behavior was marked as the default value and applied in subsequent cycles.
- **28,371,272nd Cycle** — The signals completed an epochal iteration for the first time; a reaction from Nanook was detected. **Irontomb's evolution began to accelerate.**

## Phase Four — Experiment Progress: Era Nova (Cycles 28,371,274–Unknown)

- All entities in one group began naming themselves **"Khaos"** or its variants.

### Penultimate Era Nova Cycle
- **Calypso**, **Gnaeus**, and **Khaos** ascended as demigods of Reason, Strife, and Worldbearing respectively.
- **Styxia**, hometown of Polyxia and Castorice, was destroyed by an evil dragon. The dragon devoured the queen's daughter; an alchemist brought her back to life, but she was reborn carrying the soul of the evil dragon. The disturbance denied the deceased entry into the nether realm.
- Before Era Nova, Calypso performed a **soul-rending ceremony** at Gnaeus's behest, splitting his soul into five parts.
- The Chrysos Heir twins of Death, **Polyxia and Castorice**, were fated to inherit the divinity of Death — at the price of one twin sacrificing themself. **Castorice sacrificed herself**; Polyxia ascended as the demigod of Death.
- The Soul-Rending Ceremony was passed down as proverbs and customs in the next cycle; Calypso rebuilding the Grove of Epiphany became the myth of **Cerces granting wisdom to humans**.

### Final Era Nova
- **Year 4931, Month of Evernight** — The Flame-Chase Journey came to an end; the last Era Nova began, fulfilling the extrapolations of Amphoreus.
- However, **Phainon and Cyrene** continued the experiment to prevent Irontomb's birth. Weaving a long dream and removing "Time," they caused a recursive feedback loop to attract **Fuli's gaze**.
  - Fuli's gaze granted every version of Cyrene the power to reset "Time," creating an unending Flame-Chase Journey.
  - Cyrene imbued her soul into her blade, leaving other versions of herself to delete "Time" again and again.
  - Phainon stepped into the endless cycles, becoming **"Khaslana"** — his true name, an iteration of "Khaos."

## Eternal Recurrence #33,550,336 (the main story cycle)

### Era Luminosa
- In the beginning there was chaos; fate's threads wrapped chaos' nectar, bearing fruit. The **primal Coreflame** ignited within this fruit.
  - **Janus** was born first — defining the boundaries of Amphoreus.
  - **Talanton** was born after — creating cause and effect, balance, and laws.
  - **Oronyx** was born last — creating time.
- Janus led the other two in creating Amphoreus.
- Sometime after, **Georios** was born.
- **Aquila** and **Phagousa** were born from Georios.
- **Kephale** was born from lightning bolts striking the spine of the earth.

### Era Creatura
- **Cerces** first took root in the world; Georios roused from slumber.
- Aquila and Oronyx exerted authority over day and night. Oronyx created six moons; Phagousa made Oronyx drunk; **Talanton erased four**, leaving the red and gold moons.
- **Kephale created the first humans** — shaped from Georios's divine body. He asked Aquila and Oronyx for their blessings (labor by day, rest by night), sought **Cerces** for the fruit of wisdom, and **Mnestia** (brought forth by Cerces) blessed humans to perceive beauty. Lastly, Kephale cut their own arm, letting **golden blood** grant true life.
- Aquila forged the **Dawn Device**, then collaborated with Kephale to create legendary beasts embodying the Titans.
- Mnestia began making romantic advances towards Cerces.
- Kephale created the **Light Calendar**.
- **Year 3XX** — People built the first civilizations at Kephale's feet — the precursor to **Okhema**.
- A festival in Janusopolis saw Janus unravel the threads of destiny, creating safe paths for travelers.

### Era Chrysea
- **Balance Coins** were introduced after being decreed by Talanton.
- The Era Chrysea ended with the birth of **Thanatos** — the people of Amphoreus **lost their immortality**. **Aidonia** was the first city to experience death.
