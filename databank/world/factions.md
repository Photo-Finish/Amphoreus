# Factions & Organizations of Amphoreus

> The governing bodies, armies, and secret societies of Amphoreus — from the councils that rule the city-states to the assassins who hunt the Chrysos Heirs, and the cosmic coalition that rose to stop Irontomb.

---

## Council of Elders

> The main governing bodies within the cities of Amphoreus — each city has its own Council, with roles varying between cities.

- **Locations**: Okhema, Castrum Kremnos, Sabany (and other city-states)
- **Role**: In charge of the day-to-day affairs of Amphoreus' cities.

### Okhema's Council
- Temporarily transferred power to **Aglaea and the Chrysos Heirs** due to the dire situation on Amphoreus.
- Most of the Council dislikes the Chrysos Heirs and the prophecy — nearly **half the council voted against** transferring power and continues plotting against the Heirs to this day.
- Positions are "entrusted to them by the people of Okhema" (per Caenis), hinting at a democratic election process.
- **Known members (Okhema)**: Caenis, Elder Etro, Callictis, Diogena (implied), Diogena's grandfather (deceased), Oenone.
- In the 33,550,337th recurrence, Cerydra executed the Elders and their families after a failed rebellion (Year 3910), and later a Council was purged by Seliose (Year 3960).

### Castrum Kremnos' Council
- Before the city's fall, the Council gave the **Mountain Dwellers** in their service freedom after **Chartonus** agreed to create a Sword Vessel capable of sealing divinity.
- The Castrum Kremnoan Council of Elders approved the king's rule.

## The Kremnoan Detachment

> A military group formed by Kremnoan exiles who chose to follow Mydei and leave Castrum Kremnos. Currently allied with Okhema, supporting the Flame-Chase Journey led by Aglaea. Only Kremnoans may join.

- **Leader & founder**: Mydei
- **Locations**: Amphoreus, Okhema

### History
- After returning from the **Sea of Souls**, Mydei was accompanied by five loyal companions: **Hephaestion, Perdikkas, Leonnius, Ptolemy, and Peucesta** — who stood by him through exile, sharing a decade of hardship, camaraderie, campfires, honey brews, and songs.
  - **Hephaestion** — scrawny but a formidable warrior; teased Mydei for adding goat milk to the honey brew.
  - **Perdikkas** — a skilled healer of unconventional remedies.
  - **Leonnius** — swift and reliable; the group's primary messenger.
  - **Ptolemy** — a scholar who endlessly quoted tomes (and took part of Castrum Kremnos' grand library — though Mydei's family owned it).
  - **Peucesta** — the quiet one, a masterful musician whose melodies proved crucial in battle.
- **Tragic deaths before reaching Okhema**:
  - Perdikkas fell first — struck by a poisonous arrow from the people of **Ladon**; he could not save himself.
  - Leonnius died in the fifth winter during **Aidonia**'s three-month snowstorm; his legs severed covering the group's retreat from a bottomless swamp, his body vanished into the mire.
  - Ptolemy fell with his cavalry before victory in Aidonia; collapsing obelisks became his tomb.
  - Peucesta sacrificed himself luring enemies into **Aenionus** with his music; the city and Mydei's soldiers were set ablaze.
  - Hephaestion, Mydei's most trusted friend, fell ill before Mydei's duel with his father **Eurypon**; concealing his condition, he lived just long enough to see Mydei's victory and passed away.
- The detachment slew King **Eurypon**, allowing Mydei to become king. He initially refused the title, eventually ascending as the **demigod of Strife**, then **abolished the Kremnoan Dynasty** to end Castrum Kremnos' thousand-year history of bloodshed.

### Known Members
- **Mydei** (leader)
- **Krateros** — Royal Wing Elite; Godshield Brigade Elite; mentor of Mydei
- **Miltiades**
- Past: Hephaestion, Perdikkas, Leonnius, Ptolemy, Peucesta (all deceased)

## The Cleaners

> A group of assassins on Amphoreus, tasked with killing the Chrysos Heirs for the Council of Elders.

- **Leader**: Caenis
- **Nature**: A group of assassins who eliminate the Chrysos Heirs on the Council of Elders' behalf.
- **Unique trait**: Cleaners can **pass on their memories and experiences to future successors**, so a successor retains much of the original's memories and personality — a chain of incarnations.

### History
- In the **33,550,336th recurrence**, **Caenis** led the Cleaners as the **twenty-seventh incarnation** of the original, additionally holding a position in Okhema's Council of Elders. She distrusted Aglaea and attempted to thwart her at every turn, believing Era Nova needed to be achieved. This Caenis was killed by black tide creatures after the Flame Reaver led them into Okhema.
- In the **33,550,337th recurrence**, the Cleaners were caught committing a crime by **Cerydra**, who sentenced them to death. **Darius**, Caenis' successor, pledged loyalty, and Cerydra commuted the sentence in return for their service. She gave Darius and his companions a "**cleaning**" list — their first target being the original Caenis. They followed the letters in order, killing those Cerydra had taken offense to. The last target, **Dux Ironclad**, fiercely resisted; one Cleaner executed him, angering Cerydra (who wanted him subdued, not killed) — she commanded the Cleaner be burned to death.

### Known Personnel
- **Caenis** (deceased)
- **Daros** (formerly)
- **Darius**
- Gregoni, Koroni, Livia, Olivia (deceased)

## The Flame-Chase Army & the Flame-Chase Journey

> The great undertaking of the Chrysos Heirs — to "pluck the gods' Coreflames" and create the world anew. (Compiled from mission and history lore; the wiki's dedicated page was not retrievable.)

### The Flame-Chase Journey
- The central quest of Amphoreus: the **Chrysos Heirs**, blessed with golden blood, set out to hunt the **Titans**, retrieve their **Coreflames**, and recreate the world through the **Era Nova**.
- Originated from the **prophecy of Tribios** (Year 3760): golden blood would "converge into a boiling river that flows to the heroic heirs of this world."
- Cerydra turned the prophecy into law and officially started the **First Flame-Chase Journey** after the Second Siege of Okhema (Year 3910).
- Known Flame-Chase Journeys across recurrences: Cerydra's First Flame-Chase, Aglaea's Second Flame-Chase, and the near-perfect Flame-Chase of Eternal Recurrence #4,048,128.

### The Flame-Chase Army
- The military force that carries out the Flame-Chase — the elite army of Chrysos Heirs and their allies, led across eras by **Cerydra** (Imperator), then **Aglaea**, then **Castorice**.
- Notable campaigns (from the Imperator War Chronicles): the Liberation of Loukas (3850–51), the First/Second/Third Sieges of Okhema, the conquest of Janusopolis (Coreflame of Law, 3960), the battles against Georios (3957, with Terravox slaying the Titan), the battle of the Dolos Riverbed (3959), and the sacrifice of 500 Chrysos Heirs against Phagousa (3960) for the trial of Law.
- Key generals and heroes: **Seneca**, **Labienus**, **Seliose** the Daythunder Knight, **Hysilens** (Knight Commander), **Terravox**.

## Cosmic Alliance (Anti-Irontomb Cosmic Alliance)

> A coalition of various factions formed to combat Irontomb.

- **Full name**: Anti-Irontomb Cosmic Alliance
- **Purpose**: To battle the Lord Ravager **Irontomb** in tandem with the **Astral Express crew**.
- **Known member factions**: Astral Express, Doctors of Chaos, Galaxy Rangers, Genius Society, Planet Screwllum, Interastral Peace Corporation (Strategic Investment Department), Intelligentsia Guild, Xianzhou Alliance.
- After the successful campaign, the **Strategic Investment Department** invited participating factions to **Astropolis** to discuss forming the Anti-Destruction Alliance — facing criticism for attempting to assume a leadership role.
- In the final battle, **Herta** led the Cosmic Alliance against Irontomb in space while the Titans battled within Amphoreus.

## Other Amphoreus Factions & Groups

- **Three Hundred Rogues of Dolos** — the rogues' band of the fallen city Dolos (where Khaslana burned the city in Recurrence #134).
- **The Five Gourmet Overlords** — a faction mentioned in Amphoreus lore.
- **The Skyfolk** — the winged people of the Sky Castrum (Eye of Twilight), split into **Sunfolk** and **Rainfolk**; nearly all were cast into the golden magma by Seliose.
- **The Sea Sirens** — Titankin of Phagousa (see `world/fauna.md`), nearly wiped out fighting the black tide.
- **The Venerationists / Grove Schools** — the Seven Schools of the Grove of Epiphany (see `world/city-states.md`).
- **The Kremnoan Dynasty** — Castrum Kremnos' ruling line, from King Gorgo to King Eurypon, ended by Mydei in Year 4931.
- **The Titankin** — Titan-created lifeforms, also an enemy faction (see `world/fauna.md`).
- **The Ladonian Army** — the army of Ladon, later led by Lygus in the sieges of Okhema (Third Siege, Year 4210).
