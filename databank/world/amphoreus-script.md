# The Amphoreus Script

> The writing system of Amphoreus — a script based on the Universal Script of the cosmos, with alterations of its own.

## Overview

- The **Amphoreus Script** *(unofficial name)* is the writing system found on Amphoreus.
- It is **based on the Universal Script with some alterations** — the same relationship the Penacony Script has to the Universal Script.
- The **Universal Script** is the most common writing system in the cosmos, appearing primarily on the Herta Space Station, Jarilo-VI, and in tutorial images; its usage by the Nameless, the IPC, and other major factions marks it as the universal script throughout the cosmos. The scripts of Penacony and Amphoreus are both derived from it.

## Related Writing & Records of Amphoreus

- **As I've Written** — the journal on Amphoreus that catalogs the story of the world, written alongside the Trailblazer; the record left by the Demiurge in which Amphoreus' story is being reborn.
- **Amphoreus Trailblaze log** — Dan Heng's journal recording his experiences on Amphoreus.
- **Eternal Verses of Amphoreus** — a relic set; its texts speak of Amphoreus and "writing an unprecedented ending."
- The **Light Calendar**, the world's time system, is itself a form of recorded writing created by Kephale (see `world/calendar.md`).
- Amphorean writing culture is literacy-rich — e.g., nursery rhymes that "any literate child could recite by heart," rules carved into stone tablets, and stone tablets as law (the test-takers of Okhema study "rules carved into the stone tablets").

## Appendix: Other World Scripts (for reference)

The broader "Universal Script" page also documents:

- **Universal Script** — the common cosmic script (Herta Space Station, Jarilo-VI, tutorials; used by the Nameless, IPC, etc.).
- **Xianzhou Script** — based on Chinese small seal script; first letter of each syllable corresponds to pinyin (e.g., "KFK" for Kafka / 卡芙卡).
- **Hanzi-Based Script** — Xianzhou writing based on traditional Chinese characters and seal script, corresponding with Hanzi rather than the Latin alphabet.
- **Penacony Script** — based on Universal Script with few alterations; some characters appear to be modified versions of the Arnold Böcklin typeface.
- **Latin Script** — real-world Latin script is widely visible to players but has very limited in-universe usage.
