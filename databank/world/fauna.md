# Fauna of Amphoreus

> The creatures of Amphoreus — from the earth-treading dromases of Georios to the sirens of Phagousa's deep. Many are "Titankin": lifeforms created by the Titans to serve their domains.

---

## Dromas

> The great beasts of burden of Amphoreus — creations of Georios, the Titan of Earth.

- **Type**: Species (Titankin — creation of Georios, Earth)
- **Nature**: A domesticated, peaceful species that adopted the tacit and kind nature of their creator.

### Description
- Legends say dromases appeared from Georios's back, can predict disasters, and will stomp their feet and make a ruckus when Georios is asleep.
- As creations of Georios, they possess **natural telepathy** to other creations of Georios, such as Mountain Dwellers.
- The dromas language has **thirty-two different regional variants**.

### Eating Habits
- Georios gave them the ability to **consume earth**. Okheman citizens feed them mostly **Redsoil Feed** (easy to find), though dromases favor dry yellowsoil and sticky blacksoil.
- Allergic to some foods, such as poultry eggs; dislike coal and presumably hay by itself.

### Breeds
- Like cattle, dromases used for meat are a different breed from those used for labor. The meat breed has tender, flavorful meat — but if not prepared properly, it's about as enjoyable as eating redsoil. **Grilled Dromas Steak** is a classic Okheman delicacy, best served on redsoil for the most authentic flavor.

### Recent History
- Okhema's dromases have shown abnormal tendencies: avoidant of the sandpits they used to enjoy bathing in, eyes altered from bright/moist/calm to vigilant and confused.
- The Dromas Workshop in Okhema houses about **1,200 dromases**.

### Known Dromases
- **Asterion**, **Castor**, **Euphemus**, **Pollux** (in the Dromas Workshop)
- **Behemoth** (also known as Handsome Dromas)
- **Heracles** (appears in "I Once Was In Arcadia")
- **Kokopo III**
- **Gorgeous Dromas** (appears in "Heartbroken Dromas"; replaces Castor)
- **Excited Little Dromas**
- **Terravox** — Chrysos Heir and demigod of Earth (the giant walking dragon; see below)

### Trivia
- Anaxa has a deep love for dromas, mocked in the Grove as "a dromas draped in finery" — unbothered by the remarks.

## Chimera

> Small, artificially produced animals — the mascots of Okhema.

- **Type**: Species (artificial)
- **Note**: "Chimera" instances are typically lowercase in-game (e.g., the Little Chimera's dialogue).

### Description
- An artificially produced breed **accidentally created inside the Garden of Life** using the **Seed of Life**.
- Researchers were attempting to cultivate the revival of three prehistoric animals that existed before the black tide: the **Felys**, the **Canis**, and the **Terrazard** (references to cats, dogs, and lizards).
- The team named the new lifeform after the mythological Chimera and made the Garden of Life its habitat.
- **Appearance**: small quadrupedal animals, large head, short body; a small "collar" of fur below the head; a tail that varies; large eyes, small nose, blush-marks on cheeks, natural smile. Most have horns (one central or two on either side; straight or curved).
- **Intelligence**: very intelligent — understands human speech though it cannot speak it. To understand them, one must study extensively in the Grove, receive Cerces's blessing, or be their companion. Common trait: they howl "**Awoo**" while speaking.
- Chimeras get along well with Okhema's civilians and help with their needs. Helping chimeras work in squads led by a human administrator; squads must meet goals or risk disbanding (the administrator pays a hefty fine and receives a memo of criticism at the citizens' assembly — they are mascots, after all).

### Known Chimeras / Event Variants
- The **Awooo Firm** and **Chrysos Awoo Championship** events feature many chimera variants modeled after characters, e.g.: Butterfly Cake (Castorice), Candied Apple (Tribbie), Chimegus (Lygus), Cyrup (Cyrene/Demiurge), Dux Beast (Cerydra), Fig Stew (Mydei), Flamigo (Phainon/Khaslana), Gray-mera (Trailblazer), Gummy Roll (Evernight), Gurgling Fish (Hysilens), Holy Pudding (Sunday), Krenabis (Hyacine), Master Cat-Thief (Cipher), Moonster (Flame Reaver), Nanus (Anaxa), Oatmeal (Aglaea), Plushy Peach (young Cyrene), Sated Dragon (Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae), Sugarcrystal Camera (March 7th), Toasty Dragon (Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae), Vigethos (Phainon).

### Trivia
- Purchasing the **Mysterious Gift (L)** from Janus' Steed grants a **pet chimera** for the player's accommodation in Okhema (four randomized colors).
- Appearance-wise, Amphorean chimeras strongly resemble the **Manticore**.

## Mountain Dweller

> The ancient, towering people of the mountains — creations of Georios, Titan of Earth.

- **Type**: Species (Titankin — creation of Georios, Earth)

### Description
- An ancient race created by Georios, considered the Titan's Titankins.
- **Appearance**: very tall and muscular, broad chest, defined musculature, pale gray-blue skin tone.
- Lived in the mountains dotting the planet — peace-loving inhabitants.
- At some point they were **enslaved by humans**, who made use of their skills as blacksmiths, guards, miners, and keepers.
- **Chartonus** constructed a Sword Vessel to seal divinity using the finest stones of Castrum Kremnos and **Nikador's ichor** — in return for his and his people's freedom.
- When the Titans began destroying the planet, the Mountain Dwellers fled to **Okhema**, where they were slowly accepted. Today they continue their duties in blacksmithing and masonry.

### History — 33,550,337th Eternal Recurrence
- During Cerydra's campaign against Georios, the Mountain Dwellers and Dromases rushed to Georios's side to defend the ravaging Titan from the Flame-Chase Army. Led by **Geocles**, their army's formations were nearly impenetrable — until the emergence of the giant walking dragon **Terravox**, who broke through their defensive lines. Terravox slew Georios and claimed their Coreflame, ascending as the demigod of Earth.

### Known Mountain Dwellers
- **Vulcan** — Aphrea's husband
- **Geocles** — leader in the 33,550,337th recurrence

## Sea Siren

> The Titankin of Phagousa, Titan of Ocean — singers of the deep.

- **Type**: Species (Titankin — creation of Phagousa, Ocean)
- **Affiliation**: Styxia
- **Known**: Hysilens (playable, future demigod of Ocean); examples include the Servant of Tides.

### Description
- When Phagousa began faltering against the black tide and her chalice began to shatter, she promised the sea sirens an **endless feast in the city-states**. Wanting to bear Phagousa's fate and hold on to that promise, the sea sirens hunted for the pieces of the shattered chalice.
- The result of their campaign: many were killed or **corrupted into black tide creatures** (Abyssal Sea Sirens, Servants of Tides, etc.).
- Only **Hysilens**, the future demigod of Ocean, survived the ordeal.

### Biology
- Implied to be **only female**. Fish-like appearance with fins and scales, but able to take on human form. ("My fins became legs, my scales became skin, and the sea foam on my body became a shimmering gown.")
- As humans they look nearly identical to ordinary Amphoreans — except for scales on their cheeks, a **pocket of water replacing their mid-torso**, and the capability to breathe underwater.

### Abilities
- Their pride is their **powerful songs**, capable of myriad effects: enchanting others into doing whatever they want, or casting illusions.
- Songs can be interrupted or repelled — one "antidote" being the **God of Banquets Dew**.

## Sea Seal — "Divine Beasts of Phagousa"

- The Okhema bathhouse lore refers to a giant sea seal species as "**the divine beasts of Phagousa**." A giant sea seal doll is a prized comfort item in Okhema's baths ("sacred beast of Phagousa! Just a touch is enough to enchant you").
- Seals appear in Okhema via the **Seal Slammers** event, with named seal NPCs such as **Mega Seal**, **Spiky Seal**, and **Duotone Seal**.
- **Seal Slammers**: a mini-game where Trailblazers team up with Bubbles to lead a baby seal to "aqua-bumping victory" at the Seal Slammers Arena.

## Other Notable Creatures & Beasts

- **Terravox** — the "giant walking dragon"; a Dromas-type being and the Chrysos Heir/demigod of Earth. Broke the Mountain Dwellers' lines and slew Georios in the 33,550,337th recurrence.
- **Pollux** (the dragon) — the "evil dragon" that attacked Styxia, laying the City-State of Dragons and Waves to ruins. *(Distinct from Pollux the dromas in Okhema's Dromas Workshop.)*
- **The great lion of Tretos** — whose head King Gorgo took and used its hollow skull as a resonator to catch Nikador's eye.
- **Meteoric Eagle** — associated with Aquila, Titan of Sky; source of the belief that Aquila forged the Dawn Device.
- **Divine Foals** — referenced in the readable "Tattered Schedule of Divine Foals."
- **Phagousa's beasts** — various creatures of the Ocean Titan's domain, including the sea seals and sirens.
- **Creation Nymph** — collectible creatures (not strictly fauna but often confused with them); **Golden Scapegoat** and **Spirithief** are puzzle/collectible entities.
- **Prehistoric animals** — the Felys, Canis, and Terrazard existed before the black tide; the chimeras were an attempt to revive them.

## Titankin (Concept & Enemy Faction)

- **Titankin** (泰坦眷属, "Titan's kin") is the term for lifeforms created by the Titans — e.g., Dromases and Mountain Dwellers (Georios), Sea Sirens (Phagousa).
- In-game, **Titankin** is also an **enemy faction** — black tide-corrupted creatures and guardians aligned with the Titans, appearing as normal enemies, elites, bosses, and Echo of War encounters.
