# Black Tide / Destruction Equation

> The Black Tide is the in-simulation manifestation of the Destruction Equation, a byproduct of The Amphoreus Experiment.

## Nature

- **Not a physical entity** — a visual phenomenon within the simulated world of Amphoreus
- Emergent effect of the Destruction Equation's spreading infection
- By cycle 176,100, systemic chaos surpassed critical threshold → mutation/evolution of the Equation
- Simulated spaces that couldn't withstand the strain exhibited the Black Tide as **graphical distortions**
- Infected data constructs showed **corrosive decay**

## Capabilities

### Incomplete Form (Phase 2)
- Only infects **inorganic beings**
- Renders them into chaotic processes

### Complete Form (Phase 3+)
- Infects **organic lifeforms**
- Randomizes established laws of logic into chaos
- Dismantles essential structures of individual lifeforms (down to DNA)
- Below certain threshold: can be rendered harmless via **digital vaccines** (developed by IPC)

## History within Amphoreus (In-Simulation)

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Era Bellica** | Black Tide arrives alongside Three Titans of Calamity. Destroys multiple city-states. Drives many Titans mad. |
| **Tribios's response** | Takes Janus's Coreflame to become first demigod. Leads Amphoreus to victory. Splits into 1000 messengers to spread the prophecy. |
| **Cerydra's unification** | Empress Cerydra unites Chrysos Heirs to kill corrupted Titans and take Coreflames. |
| **Nikador's stand** | First Titan to rise against Black Tide. Unable to sever the root. Tears away Reason (Gnaeus) before going mad. Slain by Chrysos Heirs. |
| **Mydei's oath** | Inherits Strife Coreflame. Promises to hold Black Tide at bay. |
| **ER #33,550,336** | Cipher dies; Dawn Device's light extinguished; Black Tide invades Okhema. |
| **ER #33,550,337** | Black Tide in recessive state (due to Phainon's actions). Infects Phagousa's Titankin → Servant of Tides, Lady of Crashing Waves. Later infects Garden of Recollection memetic entities → Ichor Memosprites. |
| **Final Cycle** | In the world recreated from Worldbearing Titan's memories (Trailblazer), the Black Tide **did not exist**. |
