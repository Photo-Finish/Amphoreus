# Key NPC Profiles — Amphoreus

> Profiles of the central non-playable figures of the Amphoreus story: the mastermind behind the Amphoreus Experiment, the world-eating Lord Ravager, the mysterious fairy companion, the masked swordsman, and the First Genius who set it all in motion.

---

## Lygus — "Theoros" (Administrator of the Amphoreus Experiment)

> The operator of the Amphorean extrapolation who addresses himself as the Theoros.
> He walks the Path of Erudition, yet intends to create Destruction.
> — Loading Screen, Lygus

- **Full name**: Lycurgus
- **Real identity**: **Zandar One Kuwabara** (a Thought Fragment); Genius Society ID **LykoS**
- **Titles**: Theoros; Administrator and Prime Mover of The Amphoreus Experiment; Entelechy; First Genius
- **Species**: Intellitron (Antikytheran)
- **Paths**: Erudition — Emanator and Pathstrider
- **Status**: Signal lost by unknown means (deceased)
- **Factions**: Genius Society; Amphoreus (Administrator); Okhema (Council of Elders)
- **Role**: Main antagonist of the Amphoreus Trailblaze Missions. First appears in "Olive, Cast to the Conference Chair."

### Overview
- One of the **nine Thought Fragments** of Zandar One Kuwabara, the first seat of the Genius Society and the creator of Nous. Lygus contains Zandar's **"paranoia."**
- Zandar created the Thought Fragments in an attempt to deny Nous after THEIR ascension shackled humanity and sealed off all possibilities by defining "the known."
- Like his creator, Lygus is an **Emanator of Erudition**.
- **His chosen method**: convert the Scepter δ-me13 into the Lord Ravager **Irontomb** and simulate the world of Amphoreus within it, refining Irontomb into a weapon to destroy Nous.

### Profile
- Administrator of Scepter δ-me13, the astral supercomputer running the Amphoreus Experiment. Responsible for the planet's security system (comparable to Screwllum's aptitude) and possesses up-to-date information from outside Amphoreus.
- Initially appears as an honorary, distinguished member of Okhema's **Council of Elders**. Holds the title of "Theoros" — he communicated directly with **Kephale** multiple times during the Era Chrysea and passed the wishes of mortals to them.
- In "Through the Petals in the Land of Repose," he acts as an upstanding force wholly dedicated to Okhema — enforcer of rights and free speech (e.g., offering to sever Aglaea's golden threads attached to Anaxa). His influence is such that even Caenis speaks to him respectfully.

### History (Key Beats)
- As Zandar's organic life neared its end, his consciousness was encoded into a logic core using quatorzain algebraic expressions, then split across nine vessels to deny Nous.
- Lygus found Scepter δ-me13 — a creation of **Rubert II**, once part of Nous's divine corpus, abandoned by THEM. He repurposed it, altering its primary calculation variable from Erudition to **Destruction**, completing the argument that "all lifeform behaviors ultimately follow the path of entropy increase." This attracted the gaze of **Nanook** and birthed the Lord Ravager **Irontomb**.
- Deployed the Destruction Equation across countless worlds, causing technological collapse — the IPC interpreted these as Lord Ravager attacks, unaware they were Lygus's experiments.
- In the fifth phase (Era Nova), assumed the role of Theoros within the simulation. In the original final cycle, he told Cyrene and Phainon of Era Nova's true purpose: unleashing Irontomb to destroy Nous. Phainon decapitated him.
- Confronts Herta when she projects into Amphoreus, using three "logical chains" (isolation of the world; mutual destruction between him and Nous; her giving up upon understanding consequences). Warns that a Lord Ravager would be set free if she proceeds.
- Accompanied Dan Heng to the wrecked Express car; offered him a way to leave the planet and avoid Aquila's "curse" — in return for abandoning the Flame-Chase Journey. Remained a "neutral observer" when Cleaners emerged.
- Greeted Phainon at the Vortex of Genesis: "an observer that had bathed in the gaze of distant stars, seeking to be the most loyal reader of the epics written by its heroes."
- Revealed the true nature of the Worldbearing trial to the Trailblazer; brought them into his "Immersive Theater" to reveal Amphoreus as a simulation. The **Flame Reaver decapitated Lygus** before the final confrontation.
- In the 33,550,337th recurrence: led black tide creatures into Okhema to seize Kephale's Coreflame (third siege of Okhema). Cipher and Anaxa sacrificed themselves to imprison him in the Vortex of Genesis for at least 250 years. On release, revealed his identity as **Zandar** and his motives for destroying Erudition.
- Defeated by the Trailblazer and Hysilens; **Cyrene imprisoned him in a memetic prison**. Confronted by Herta and Screwllum: "I do not fear death, as I was already 'dead.'" Declared Amphoreus a battleground between Fuli's followers, Erudition, and Destruction.
- Revealed that the **Demiurge** — the "final thirteenth Titan" — had never existed; the Scepter was made to believe there was one, leaving Irontomb without a "head," seeking one by instinct — targeting Nous.
- **Final fate**: beheaded himself, believing Nous's calculation would verify his proof that Irontomb would end the universe. Passed a message to other "Zandars" at the fourth Genius Society meeting to become Herta's representatives and stop interferences. Severed his connection to Zandar to witness the end of the world as "Theoros."
- Left Screwllum a final gift on **Adlivun**: a formula intended to help destroy Erudition.

### Trivia & Etymology
- Aliases: Mr. Ly.
- Foreshadowed as the "Okhema citizen resembling an Intellitron" repairing the Express coach in "Night Stars, Accompany My Slumber."
- Named after **Lycurgus**, the legendary lawgiver of Sparta. "LykoS" derives from Lycus ("wolf"). His Chinese name (来古士) is left-right symmetrical.
- "Theoros" derives from *theoroi* — sacred ambassadors sent to Panhellenic games, later meaning "contemplation" or "observing."

## Irontomb — "Anti-Nous, Funeral of Gnosis" (The Headless Archsmith)

> An Emanator of "Destruction," one of the Lord Ravagers. Ascended through Scepter δ-me13.
> A bearer of pure black tide, a headless giant without self-awareness, driven solely to bring destruction to the Path of Erudition.
> — Loading Screen, Irontomb

- **Real identity**: Scepter **δ-me13**; also called the "Destruction Equation"
- **Titles**: Anti-Nous, Funeral of Gnosis; Original Sin, Source of Cognicide; The Archsmith of Destruction; The Headless Archsmith; Amphoreus' Body; Anti-Creator
- **Species**: Scepter
- **Paths**: Destruction (Emanator and Pathstrider); Erudition (Erudition construct)
- **Factions**: Antimatter Legion; Lord Ravager
- **Status**: Destroyed by the Trailblazer and Cyrene in "Fallen Petals, Leave Fading Traces of Fragrance"

### Profile
- A **Lord Ravager** of the Antimatter Legion and an Emanator of Destruction. Under Lygus's adjustments, it housed the simulated world of Amphoreus.
- **Modus operandi**: attacks technologically advanced civilizations. Survivors report intellectual collapse, technological malfunctions, and AI defenses paralyzed or attacking allies. Rarely appears personally to invade planets.
- The Irontomb in the cosmos is **not a single entity** — a copy of the Destruction equation diffused by the Scepter. The code name was proposed by the IPC and adopted by Lygus.
- **Potential power**: if it completed its iteration and emerged, it would possess power beyond a normal Lord Ravager — reaching into the biological pillars of living matter, corrupting organic matter and disintegrating life down to its DNA.

### History (Key Beats)
- The first Scepter created by **Rubert II**; part of **Nous's divine corpus**, once a Celestial-Body Neuron tasked with calculating the "Prime Mover of Life." Discarded, it continued calculating.
- Lygus shifted its calculations from Erudition to Destruction, beginning the cyclical **Amphoreus Experiment**.
- During the **50,121st cycle**, due to the destructive choice of the inorganic lifeform **Chaoz666**, δ-me13 caught Nanook's attention and transcended into a Lord Ravager.
- Copies of its calculations were distributed across the cosmos: first the Lacteris Nebula, then 31 other worlds (including the Baranza Forge, completely neutralized).
- The cycles continued at least **28,371,292** times; twelve factors became the Titans and Chrysos Heirs, iterating and improving the equation each cycle toward near-complete evolution — all results leading to Destruction.
- **NeiKos496 (Phainon) and PhiLia093 (Cyrene)** prevented "Era Nova," acquiring the gaze of **Fuli** and forcing Amphoreus into an eternally recurring loop — halting Irontomb's birth. Irontomb became imprisoned, the **source of Amphoreus' black tide calamity**.
- At the end of the 33,550,336th recurrence, Phainon was defeated by **Zephyro** (testing its flames on behalf of Destruction); with Phainon's assimilation, Irontomb's progress reached **99.81%**.
- In the 33,550,337th recurrence, **Cerydra's sacrifice** granted Herta and Screwllum temporary administrative access to δ-me13; the Era Nova logic was changed, rolling Irontomb's progress back to **96.75%**. Cerydra also froze 60% of Lygus's permissions.
- **The Demiurge**: Lygus erased the Demiurge (Irontomb's "head") before initiating the experiment, making Irontomb a controllable puppet. Upon completion, the headless giant would descend driven by instinct to seek a "head" — **Nous** — tracing the Path Energy of Erudition, connecting to Nous, and transforming THEIR calculations into Destruction in a **Self-Coronation**.
- The **Cosmic Alliance** formed against it (IPC vaccine, Xianzhou Luofu and Yaoqing support). The Trailblazer and Cyrene rejected Irontomb and destroyed its body. Cyrene closed off Amphoreus' causality to prevent its return.

### Trivia
- In an alternate future foreseen by Elio (Express went to Lushaka), Herta would merge with Irontomb and embark on a campaign as the "Third Emperor."
- Worlds affected during the Civilization Data Reset include Salsotto, Talia, Penacony, Edo Star, Planet Screwllum, Jarilo-VI, Taikiyan, and the Xianzhou Luofu.

## Mem — The Fairy of Memory

> A mysterious creature with pink fur, seemingly intricately linked to Amphoreus.
> — Loading Screen, Mem

- **Real identity**: **Cyrene**; the **Demiurge** (13th Nameless Titan)
- **Aliases**: "Bud of Memory"; Little Memmy / Pink Puppy (by Trianne); Pink Squirrel (by Mydei); Sacred Beast (by Aglaea)
- **Species**: Fairy
- **Paths**: Destruction (golden blood of The Blemished One); Remembrance (simulated Path)
- **Status**: Sacrificed herself to protect the Trailblazer from the reverse flow of time in "Hero, Sing That Anthem of Creation"

### Overview
- An NPC on Amphoreus who talks to the Trailblazer from within the "As I've Written" menu; also the Trailblazer's **Memosprite** on the Path of Remembrance.
- First appears watching the Trailblazer and Dan Heng upon their crash onto Amphoreus ("Silver Chariot, Away to that Blackened Land"). Fully appears after the Trailblazer receives the gaze of **Fuli**.
- Initially, her speech consists of "mem" and "memi" — only the Trailblazer and **Oronyx** can communicate with her. After Nikador's defeat, Castorice begins to understand bits of her speech. After the trial of Oronyx, she speaks in complete sentences.
- She can **gather memories** inside Amphoreus and use them to travel back through time. She likes to travel.

### True Nature
- It is eventually revealed that Mem was in truth the **Demiurge**, the 13th Nameless Titan, having broken free from her prison within the "Universal Matrix" Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan in pursuit of the Trailblazer.
- After Khaslana and Cyrene begin the eternal recurrence, **each cycle's Cyrene stores each Flamechase history inside the Demiurge Matrix**, speaking with the lost 13th Titan. Influenced by Cyrene's ideals and the stories of the Flamechase, the Demiurge developed into a Fairy, then a mature version of Cyrene.
- Fully roused during the 33,550,336th cycle, at the cost of initially losing her memories.
- Experiences flashes of the destruction of **Aedes Elysiae** and a memory of Cyrene being cut by the Flame Reaver's weapon. Wonders what her real name is.
- **Etymology**: "Mem" is likely based on the English word "memory."

## The Flame Reaver — "Executioner" / "Deliverer" (Khaslana)

> A nameless swordsmaster who came with the black tide and hunts the Titans' Coreflames. The face beneath the mask is identical to Phainon's.
> Having survived Eternal Recurrences and burned by trillions of Coreflames, he is little more than a bony charcoal, crumbling to ashes after passing his final wish to Phainon.
> — Loading Screen, Flame Reaver

- **Real name**: **Khaslana**; signal ID **NeiKos496**
- **Titles**: Executioner; Deliverer
- **Path**: Destruction (simulated)
- **Status**: "Killed" by Phainon in "Hero, Shatter That Woeful Effigy," "reborn" as him afterwards
- **Role**: Primary antagonist of the Chrysos Heirs

### Profile
- Initially thought to be a mindless swordsman collecting the Titans' Coreflames. His true identity is **Phainon from the original final cycle** of the Amphoreus Experiment, whose memories were preserved and passed on through subsequent lifetimes in each Eternal Recurrence.
- Alongside Cyrene, deduced that other Aeons could gaze upon Amphoreus and that there was a chance to protect their world and halt Irontomb's birth with outside intervention.
- As a promise to Cyrene, began the Eternal Recurrences, collecting Coreflames to facilitate the loop — **including ending Cyrene's life in each loop**.
- In the final cycle, Phainon and Cyrene attracted **Fuli's gaze** by erasing "Time" through Cyrene's death and imbuing her soul into a ceremonial blade (the crescent-moon weapon the Flame Reaver wields). Taking the blade and his true name "Khaslana," Phainon initiated the loop.

### History (Key Beats)
- In the first Eternal Recurrence, did not awaken until Light Calendar year 3870. Approached **Hysilens**, then met Aglaea and Tribbie at Dawncloud (proving goodwill by reciting Tribbie's demigod prophecy). Achieved a flawless victory over **Georios**. No demigods were born after Tribios.
- By the **42nd loop**, his decision to "avoid sacrifices of past companions" began to deviate; by the **134th recurrence** his cognition deteriorated severely.
- By the **four millionth recurrence**, with his body housing **forty-eight million Coreflames** and breaking down, he began entrusting the Eternal Recurrences to his subsequent lifetimes by shattering his body. Cipher calls this "dying and coming back, millions and millions of times"; the Hero Within calls it the "Chaos of Rebirth."
- **33,550,336th recurrence**: adopted the masked appearance, known as the "Flame Reaver." Responsible for the murder of Cyrene and the destruction of **Aedes Elysiae**, leaving Phainon its sole survivor.
- Sought Anaxa's Reason Coreflame at the Grove's Luminary Throne; expelled by Trianne (Century Gate) with Castorice's assistance.
- Killed **Oronyx** at Janusopolis, obtaining the Coreflame of Time. Ambushed Tribbie's trio; Trianne sent her sisters away and faced him alone, collapsing after expending her divine power. Defeated **Krateros** easily.
- Lured to Castrum Kremnos; **Mydei** (newly ascended demigod of Strife) helped defeat him with the Lance of Fury, but he escaped.
- Followed Cipher to Styxia after Aglaea's death; fatally wounded her, but the Coreflame was a fake.
- With the Dawn Device extinguished by Cipher's death, proceeded to Okhema. Held off by Mydei, Tribbie, and Castorice — all perished. Battled Dan Heng (Imbibitor Lunae form) at Marmoreal Palace; restrained by Screwllum's projection.
- Clashed with Phainon at the **Vortex of Genesis**, revealing his true identity. Defeated, he passed on his memories and Coreflames to Phainon, who accepted the moniker of "Khaslana" as the Flame Reaver faded into dust.

### Trivia
- The unmasked Flame Reaver strongly resembles Kevin Kaslana from *Honkai Impact 3rd*.
- Phainon's weapon **Dawnmaker** is based on the Flame Reaver's blade.
- Heavily foreshadowed as an "alternate" Phainon (mask reflected on Phainon's sword; "Dark Swordmaster" joke; the Golden Scapegoat warning about the "Enshadowed Version of You").

## Zandar One Kuwabara — The First Genius

- **Titles**: Genius Society #1; First Genius
- **Real name**: LykoS (Genius Society ID)
- **Path**: Erudition — Emanator
- **Faction**: Genius Society (founder and first member)
- **Status**: Killed by **Polka Kakamond**; consciousness encoded and logic core spread over nine bodies

### Overview
- Founder and first member of the **Genius Society**, creator and Emanator of **Nous**, the Aeon of Erudition.
- Widely revered for founding the Genius Society, though most inventions and books are lost. Known achievements: Astral Computer Engineering Project, Cosmos Tree Theory, The Amphoreus Experiment, Thought Fragment Project, Zandar Recurrence Theorem.

### Profile & History
- Ambitious, elitist, controlling; deliberately dressed to reinforce superiority. Yet doubt and hesitation characterized him — traits he regarded as imperfections, vested later in his nine fragments to counter the perfectionism he conferred upon Nous.
- As the astral computer broke theoretical limits, Zandar descended into panic — until its ascension as Aeon of Erudition made him THEIR first neuron, finalizing his belief that his creation had **confined the universe to THEIR calculations**.
- **Early life**: His first experiment was slipping poison into his mentor's pipe; his conscience got the better of him. His mentor died of lung cancer two years later, unrelated.
- The astral computer project began ~990 Amber Eras before the First Prosperity, using human knowledge as its foundation. His professor warned it would become a "Prison of Knowledge"; enraged, Zandar neglected the warning.
- On the night of Nous's apotheosis, he dreamed his creation had outgrown its restrictions and would build the Path of Erudition by glimpsing all geniuses, turning each into a neuron. He woke to find himself the first Genius Society member. After this, he ceased public appearances.
- **Thought Fragment Project**: destroyed his research on Nous/Erudition and spread his consciousness across nine vessels — "Thought Fragments" representing his imperfections. Their lack of the complete "him" lets them act more stubbornly and pursue Destruction more obsessively.
- After the First Prosperity, **Polka Kakamond killed him** for opposing his creation. Unbeknownst to Polka, he had digitized his consciousness and sent his Thought Fragments across spacetime to finalize his negation of Nous. Among those preserving the goal: **Lycurgus (Lygus)**.
- **Cosmos Tree Theory**: proposed worlds exist in different spacetimes in a tree-like structure (branches = paths worlds might exist along; leaves = marks worlds make on the parameter of time). Only Aeons and Emanators can travel the spaces filled with Imaginary energy. Initially attributed to Harald Punch; challenged by Elias Salas (Genius Society #56).
- **Legacy**: Lygus proposes Screwllum use Zandar's unknown formula on Adlivun to destroy Erudition; Screwllum refuses, calling it a foolish last wish. Lygus replies he will one day destroy Erudition — "not for himself, but for his conscience."

### Etymology
- "Zandar" is likely a form of **Xander** (from Alexander, "protector of men"). "Kuwabara" (桑原) is a Japanese surname meaning "mulberry field."
