| Titan | Coreflame | Simulated Path | Status | Associated |
|-------|-----------|----------------|--------|------------|
| **Demiurge** | Genesis | Trailblaze | Expelled by Lygus; persisted; exists in the past | Cyrene |

### Demiurge Details
- The 13th Titan, expelled from extrapolations by Lygus
- Was meant to serve as Irontomb's "head" (consciousness)
- Without it, Irontomb became a headless puppet driven solely by instinct
- A tomb was left in its place to make the Scepter believe it still existed
- Lygus detonated a Stellaron trying to kill it — failed; it survived as a cube
- Eventually became a **Pure Child of Anāsrava**
