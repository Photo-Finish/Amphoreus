The **Kremnoan Dynasty** was the royal family of the rulers of Castrum Kremnos. Following the decree of the demigod and former King Mydeimos, the dynasty is now defunct.

## History
The Kremnoan Dynasty was founded in Year 2506 of the Light Calendar, during the Era Bellica, when Gorgo was crowned as its first king. It lasted for 2,425 years until Mydei renounced his title of king and issued the last royal decree in Year 4931: "The Kremnoan dynasty comes to an official close today."
