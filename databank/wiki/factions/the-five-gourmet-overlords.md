**The Five Gourmet Overlords** are a group of fairies that appeared in . They claimed to be led by Hysilens, who had helped them "clear their backlog of fruits."

## Overview
The Five Gourmet Overlords are a group of four fairies covering three of the five  (sweet, sour, and salty) as well as "spice." They formed sometime around the events of , after Hysilens helped them "clear their backlog of fruits." The four fairies misheard her saying "the deep sea accepts all goods," misconstruing it as "food will always find a way," and forging an identity from it.

After these events, they attempted to disprove the Trailblazer's own food philosophy, only to be defeated by Mydei and Phainon's abundance of dishes in the judging event. Their defeat leads to Hysilens revealing herself as the Fifth Overlord, which she was not aware of, finishing the food to the duo's bewilderment.
