The **Erythrokeramists** are a school within Amphoreus' Grove of Epiphany. They devote themselves to the philosophy of **Erythrokeramism**.

## Overview
Erythrokeramism is the belief that the information people receive directly from their senses is the rawest, most pure, and most refined knowledge, and it is this knowledge that forms the world people see, leading to an enlightened and profound understanding of the world. The Erythrokeramists view art, emotions, and desires as alternative forms of knowledge.

This is in opposition with the school of Nodism because it refutes the understanding that stems from reason and the analysis of relationships between all things. In the eyes of the current Sage, Socrippe, because intellect is not all powerful, people need to be cautious and never believe that it is omnipotent.
