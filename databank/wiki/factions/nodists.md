The **Nodists** are a school within Amphoreus' Grove of Epiphany. They devote themselves to the philosophy of **Nodism**.

## Overview
Nodism is the theory that everything in the universe is made of numbers, and believe that everything can be mapped mathematically. They regard mathematics as the means by which Cerces governs the world.

## History
The Nodist school is the oldest school in the Grove of Epiphany. It specializes in mathematics and aims to cultivate students with a solid theoretical foundation and capable of utilizing rigorous methods of inference to study the relationships between all things, as well as to measure the quantity of all things, their structures, the spaces they exist in, and their changes.
