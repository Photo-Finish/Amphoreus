The **Lotophagists** are a school within Amphoreus' Grove of Epiphany. They devote themselves to the philosophy of **Lotophagism**.

## Overview
Lotophagism is the belief that all life are visible and tangible realities. Their asceticism is a result of believing that their practice can harmonize all things, and can lead to the understanding of the self. This school primarily studies plants and their products.

In cooperation with the priests of Oronyx, they have made the Veil Greenhouse, which blocks the Dawn Device's light with artificial darkness generators and displays different species of plants.
