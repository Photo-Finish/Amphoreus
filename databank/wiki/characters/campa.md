**Campa** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Campa was in a remote location in the Campa Mountains. The city-state was historically at odds with Ladon. The feuds between the Campanians and Ladonians ended up endangering the Mountain Dwellers when the Ladonians once planted explosives around the Campa Mountains. This is what prompted Geocles the Mountainbreaker to force his people out of their homes. In Light Calendar year 3874, Geocles the Mountainbreaker Campa rebelled against Cerydra's Okheman Alliance while Cerydra was busy fighting with Castrum Kremnos. Cerydra sent Seneca and 30,000 elite troops to the Campa Mountains to handle the rebellion. Thanks to the aid of Ladonese guides, Campa was swiftly captured.
