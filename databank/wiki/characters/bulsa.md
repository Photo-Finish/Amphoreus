**Bulsa** was a mentioned city-state on Amphoreus.

## Overview
Bulsa was a city-state that worshipped the Sky Titan, Aquila.

It was also one of the several targets of Nikador and Castrum Kremnos during their war campaign. The king of Bulsa traveled to Janusopolis to seek a way to save his city-state with the help of the Fate Titans. However, Oronyx's prophecy, interpreted by Mortis, the Holy Maiden of Janusopolis at the time, foretold that the city-state would eventually fall into ruin. True to the prophecy, Bulsa was later destroyed by Kremnoan forces.

After its destruction, the surviving warriors of Bulsa became savages and predators, raiding and pillaging other city-states and setting them ablaze.
