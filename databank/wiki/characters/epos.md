**Epos** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster, Epos was a mountainous city that had minimal exposure to Titan worship. Later, when Loukas rebelled against Cerydra's reign, she lead the Hyperborean army through Epos' mountains to reach Loukas.
