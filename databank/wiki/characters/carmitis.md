**Carmitis** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster Carmitis mainly worshipped Mnestia, the Titan of Romance. The city was built near a bay and was mainly known for its arts, with the water lyre being the signature instrument of the city.
