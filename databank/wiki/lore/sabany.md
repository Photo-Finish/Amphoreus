**Sabany** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the Black Tide disaster Sabany was one of the vassal cities of Okhema that mainly worshipped Georios, the Titan of Earth. The Sabanians were mainly known for their majestic buildings and architecture. When the black tide reached Sabany, the Sabanian Council of Elders decided to evacuate everyone in the city and take them to Okhema as refuge.
