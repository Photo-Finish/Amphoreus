**Oleinus** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the Black Tide disaster Oleinus was an agricultural city-state directly connected to Janusopolis, with which they had stable trade relations. During the Chrysos War, Oleinus fell to the black tide and most of the citizens were evacuated thanks to the help of Trissha, a fragment of the demigod of passage Tribios.
