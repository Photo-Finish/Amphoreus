**Ladon** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Ladon was invaded by Mydei's Kremnoan Detachment during the group's campaign from Ladon to Aenionus via Aidonia. It is also where Mydei's friend and comrade, Perdikkas, died from a poison arrow.

The Janus' Steed established contact with Ladon while trying to reach Castrum Kremnos, referred to the locals as "sky-worshiping tribal inhabitants."

Ladon was also among the several city-states that were conquered by Cerydra's forces in the First-Flame Chase Journey.
