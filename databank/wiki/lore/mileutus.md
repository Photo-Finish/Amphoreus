**Mileutus** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster, Mileutus was a coastal city from which Thalesus begin his journey around Amphoreus and eventually led him to founding the Grove of Epiphany.

Janusopolis once tried to create a route to the city, but failed.

Mnestia's butterflies visited the city while searching for rare gifts for Cerces, retrieving black pearls from giant clamps near the city.
