**Stymphalia** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster, Stymphalia was a city known for its olive trees, which were often attacked by avians with metal plumes until the dromas Heracles helped the locals drive them away. The most prominent family of the city was the priest clan of the Temple of Georios.

The city became one of the front lines againist the black tide and Janusopolis created a route to the city, bringing supplies and exchanging information about the black tide creatures.
