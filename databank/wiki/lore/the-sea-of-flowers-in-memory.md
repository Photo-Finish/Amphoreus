**The Sea of Flowers in Memory** is a subarea in "Dragonbone City" Styxia on Amphoreus, unlocked after completing all of the "Chirping Secret" Achievements introduced in Versions 3.0, 3.1 and 3.2: Golden Journey, Fleetfoot Paradox, Blemish of Light, Many-Faced, Lattice of Wisdom, Law of Passage, Path of Pilgrimage and Lost Prayers.
