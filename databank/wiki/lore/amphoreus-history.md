The following is a rough timeline of historical events that occur on the world of Amphoreus according to both As I've Written and the Light Calendar.

**Note:** Due to Amphoreus having two types of cycles: the first being the cycles that Lygus facilitated for the Amphoreus Experiment and the second being the recursive loop created by Cyrene and Phainon at the end of the final Amphoreus cycle, this page shall refer to the first type as "cycles," and the second type as "Eternal Recurrence."

## Before the Amphoreus Experiment
* Scepter δ-me13 was created by Rubert II.

* Scepter δ-me13 was ordered by Nous to calculate the "Prime Mover of Life."

* Scepter δ-me13 was discarded by Nous, yet continued to compute the topic given to it.

* Lygus came across the Scepter and adjusted the nature of its computations from Erudition to Destruction.
** Lygus used a Stellaron to explode its kernel layer, the part intended to function as Irontomb's head once the latter was complete.
*** Demiurge, the thirteenth Titan, was removed from the Amphoreus Experiment before it began. Without its "head," Irontomb will, upon completion, descend upon the universe as a headless giant driven solely by instinct to seek Nous's head as a replacement.
*** Demiurge however survived the explosion but became contaminated and fell into a deep slumber.

## Start of the Amphoreus Experiment
* Lygus created the "twelve factors" as simplified models of life's Primum Mobile. Based on the theme of "entropy reduction," the twelve factors were introduced into the experiment one by one in an effort to falsify them.

* At the same time, Lygus used the resulting contamination from the Stellaron around the Scepter to block any outside interference and prevent its calculations from being detected by Polka Kakamond, effectively isolating it from the outside universe.

## Phase One &mdash; Experiment Progress: Inorganic
### Spanning Cycles 1 to 50,121
* **1st Cycle** &mdash; Inorganic entities operated as cellular sized automata before the introduction of the twelve factors.
** Once the factors were introduced, the entities began to learn.

* The inorganics merged into one unified consciousness known as the "Unified Entity."

* **9,910th Cycle** &mdash; The Unified Entity learned module specialization and self-reflection.

* **12,649th Cycle** &mdash; Doril701 managed to break away from the Unified Entity. It entered a state of mutual denial with its counterpart module and both died.

* **22,449th Cycle** &mdash; Golem99 detached from Doril704 and deconstructed the Unified Entity into clusters, resulting in the birth of selfhood.
** From this cycle onwards, each cycle "ended in systemic collapse" after "certain" unfixed inorganics found the interaction loopholes and "chose self-serving optimal actions".

* **39,665th Cycle** &mdash; Doril816 created society, but resources were scarce and inorganics cannibalized each other. It then reverted back into the Unified Entity.

* **50,121st Cycle** &mdash; Chaoz666 chose to kill the aggressor when subjected to a computational power takeover. This was the first act of Destruction throughout Amphoreus' cycles.
** Nanook gazed upon the Scepter due to this action, and it became the Lord Ravager Irontomb.
** The entities linked to the twelve factors began to manifest "Golden Blood" and traits associated with Destruction.

## Phase Two &mdash; Experiment Progress: Organic
### Spanning Cycles 50,122 to 176,199
* **50,122nd Cycle** &mdash; Invertebrates dominated Amphoreus, but no life emerged.

* **59,337th Cycle** &mdash; The rise of vertebrates with primitive nervous systems caused several extinction events. Algae population exploded, causing an oxidation extinction and freezing the world. No civilization is formed.

* **90,123rd Cycle** &mdash; An orthopteran, Ortho102, became the first to use tools.
** Ortho102's descendants established the Nest Capital, which eventually fell to cannibalism.

* **100,907th Cycle** &mdash; Eumyia03 developed art. The phenomenon was subsequently never replicated.

* **132,095th Cycle** &mdash; Dystop666 developed altruism, eventually leading to a society based on altruism.
** The society fell through repeated self-destructive behaviors.

* **176,100th Cycle** &mdash; Utop13 killed out of love, redefining murder as altruistic.
** This resulted in a highly chaotic state: the equation's contamination levels spiked, leading to mutation and the creation of the black tide.

* **176,101st-176,199th Cycle** &mdash; Cycles ended identically to the 176,100th cycle.

## Phase Three &mdash; Experiment Progress: Human
### Spanning Cycles 176,200 to 28,371,273
* **176,200th-177,200th Cycles** &mdash; Memory inheritance was added between cycles for the twelve factors' signals.
** 3 signals broke down after the 10th cycle, and 9 decreased in humanity.

* **176,232nd Cycle** &mdash; Memory inheritance was now on a functional level.
** Religion for the twelve factors' signals appeared.
** Three planets were destroyed between the war of the signals.

* **177,200th Cycle** &mdash; Imora8 established an empire of absolute beauty.
** When ugliness was eradicated, the concept of beauty ceased to exist, a final knight ending their life in murder-suicide.

* **3,926,183rd Cycle** &mdash; The ratio of divinity increased. When humanity's technology hit a critical point, they began hunting the gods for energy and power. The cycle was forcibly terminated.

* **7,091,231st Cycle** &mdash; The signals were split into two, and the data of the signals was preserved to bear divinity for the next cycle.

* **11,749,992nd Cycle** &mdash; The black tide was introduced to Amphoreus.
** Fovos032 stripped data from other signals to preserve the current cycle, but failed.

* **19,110,218th Cycle** &mdash; Due to the actions of Fovos032, Amphoreus' cycles were modified to have the signals perform the start of the new cycles themselves, in what became known as the Era Nova.

* **19,110,218th Cycle** &mdash; Nammou320 utilized the "Ultimate Protocol" to eliminate the other factors from the cycle. Their behavior, due to being aligned with "Destruction," was marked as the default value for embedded behavior and was applied in subsequent cycles.

* **28,371,272nd Cycle** &mdash; The signals complete an epochal iteration for the first time, and a reaction from Nanook was detected.
** Irontomb's evolution began to accelerate.

## Phase Four &mdash; Experiment Progress: Era Nova
### Spanning Cycles 28,371,274 to Unknown
* All entities in one group began naming themselves "Khaos" or its variants.

### Penultimate Era Nova Cycle
* Calypso, Gnaeus, and Khaos ascended as the demigods of Reason, Strife, and Worldbearing respectively.
** Styxia, the hometown of Polyxia and Castorice, was destroyed by an evil dragon.
*** The evil dragon devoured the queen's daughter, and the queen found her lifeless. An alchemist helped bring the princess back to life, but she was reborn carrying the soul of the evil dragon.
*** This disturbance also caused the souls of the deceased to be denied entry into the nether realm.
** Before Era Nova, Calypso performed a soul-rending ceremony at Gnaeus's behest, splitting his soul into five parts.
** The Chrysos Heir twins of Death, Polyxia and Castorice, were fated to inherit the divinity of Death, but at the steep price of one twin having to sacrifice themself for the other. Castorice sacrificed herself, and Polyxia ascended as the demigod of Death.

* The Soul-Rending Ceremony was passed down as proverbs, customs, and more in the next cycle.

* Calypso rebuilding the Grove of Epiphany was passed down as the myth of Cerces granting wisdom to humans in the next cycle.

### Final Era Nova
* **Year 4931, Month of Evernight** &mdash; The Flame-Chase Journey came to an end, and the last Era Nova began, finally fulfilling the extrapolations of Amphoreus.

* However, Phainon and Cyrene continued the experiment in order to prevent the birth of the Lord Ravager, Irontomb. Weaving a long dream for Amphoreus and removing "Time," they caused a recursive feedback loop to attract Fuli's gaze on the Scepter and save the world.
** Fuli's gaze would grant every version of Cyrene the power to reset "Time," creating an unending Flame-Chase Journey.
** Cyrene decided to imbue her soul into her blade, leaving it up to other versions of herself to delete "Time" again and again.
** Phainon decided to step into the endless cycles of Amphoreus, becoming "Khaslana," his true name, an iteration of "Khaos."

### Eternal Recurrence #33,550,336
#### Era Luminosa
* In the beginning, there was only chaos from which "existence" was created; a jumbled mess where fate's threads wrap chaos' nectar, bearing fruit. The primal Coreflame ignited within this fruit.
** Janus was born from the Coreflame, and was the first of the Titans. They defined the boundaries of Amphoreus.
** Talanton was born after, who created cause and effect, the concept of balance, and laws.
** Lastly, Oronyx was born, and created time.

* Janus lead the other two titans in creating Amphoreus.

* Sometime after, Georios was born.

* Aquila and Phagousa were born from Georios.

* Kephale was born from lightning bolts of the skies striking the spine of the earth.

#### Era Creatura
* Cerces first took root in the world.

* Georios roused from their slumber.

* Aquila and Oronyx exerted authority over day and night respectively.
** Oronyx, thinking that one moon wasn't enough to rival the sun, created six moons.
** Phagousa, enraged at the chaos the six moons caused on the tides, decided to make Oronyx drunk, which they found successful.
** Talanton judged that the six moons were not balanced, erasing four, leaving only the red and gold moons.

* Kephale created the first humans of Amphoreus.
** Using Georios's divine body, Kephale first shaped the humans.
** Kephale asked Aquila and Oronyx to bestow their divine blessings upon his creations. Under Aquila's domain, they would labor, while under Oronyx's domain, they would rest in peace.
** Kephale sought out Cerces, asking them for the fruit of wisdom so that he might bestow upon his creations the gift of reason and thought.
** Mnestia, brought forth by Cerces, bestowed upon humans the blessing that their eyes might perceive beauty.
** Lastly, Kephale cut their own arm, allowing their golden blood to grant true life to their creations.

* Afterwards, Aquila forged the Dawn Device, then collaborated with Kephale to create legendary beasts that embody the Titans.

* Mnestia began making romantic advances towards Cerces.

* Kephale created the Light Calendar.

* **Year 3XX** &mdash; People built the first civilizations around the feet of Kephale, becoming the precursor to Okhema.

* A festival was held within Janusopolis, in which Janus unraveled the threads of destiny, creating safe paths for travelers along Amphoreus.

#### Era Chrysea
* Balance Coins were introduced after being decreed by Talanton.

* Era Chrysea came to an end with the birth of Thanatos, and the people of Amphoreus lost their immortality.
** Aidonia was the first city to experience death.

#### Era Bellica
* Following Thanatos' birth, the calamities of aging, sickness, and starvation swept across the land. Humanity thus began a relentless exploitation of nature, which enraged Cerces, who gave divine punishment.

* The Titans Zagreus and Nikador were born, causing further calamities. Around the same time, the black tide descended upon Amphoreus.
** Georios, seeing this, implored Cerces for another chance. Humanity would gain this chance as a priest of Georios offered a prayer to Cerces.

* Cerces accidentally lost their divine form. Mnestia, unable to accept the fact that their lover was injured, collected Cerces' scattered bodies, intending to weave them together with threads to bring them back.
** Their followers, known as the Creation Nymphs, joined in this task, also becoming known as "Weavers."

* Nikador and Aquila fought from one city to another.

* **Year 2147** &mdash; The black tide creatures attacked Okhema. Few or many Okhemans lost their lives.

* **Year 2506** &mdash; The Castrum Kremnos dynasty began.
** **Year 2523** &mdash; Kremnos Arena's construction was completed.

* **Year 2600** &mdash; A conflict broke out between Castrum Kremnos and the Seaside States, Nikador traveling with their troops to conquer three cities in succession. After the war, Nikador gave the cruel request for the defeated to offer the blood of their residents, or they would suffer from divine punishment.
** Phagousa, the Ocean Titan, appeared to save the residents. They ordered their followers to drink pomegranate juice instead, and imbued their powers in the juice.

* **Year 2650** &mdash; The Council of Elders formally declared that pomegranate juice would replace blood.

* **Year 3740** &mdash; Mortis gave birth to her daughter, Tribios.

* **Year 3749** &mdash; Phereseus the Venerationist attempted to gaze at Oronyx through the Evernight Veil. For all his efforts, he went mad, compiling his findings into a collection of notes called *Beyond the Veil* before disappearing.

* **Year 3750** &mdash; Mortis was killed as part of a conspiracy led by the politician Dannalio, who planned to groom Tribios into replacing her mother as the Holy Maiden. Aware of the fate awaiting her, Mortis left Tribios a polished glass stone to remember her by.

Himeko decides on a backup plan with the Trailblazer and Dan Heng descending alone, detaching a car of the Express for them to use in their descent.

March tells her that Dan Heng and the Trailblazer are on their way to Amphoreus and they won't survive without her. She also convinces Cyrene that they will find a way to help her and enters Amphoreus.
