## History
During the 33,550,336th eternal recurrence, in preparation to face Aquila, Chartonus completed Dawnmaker and gave it to Phainon alongside the loom containing the last of Aglaea's divinity.

While ascending the Eye of Twilight's Fortress, Solabis expressed familiarity with the weapon after Phainon declared he would use it to slay Aquila, confusing him as Dawnmaker was only recently forged. It's then fully put to use during the confrontation with the Titan, using it to bisect their head and kill their revived form alongside the Trailblazer, Hyacine, and Dan Heng.

It is eventually revealed that the Flame Reaver's greatsword is also Dawnmaker itself, used by the previous lifetime of Phainon and assumedly created by similar means.
