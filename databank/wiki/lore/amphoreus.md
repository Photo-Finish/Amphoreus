**Amphoreus**, also known as **The Eternal Land**, is the fifth accessible world in *Honkai: Star Rail*. It can be accessed upon completing .

It was an isolated sandbox world created by Scepter δ-me13 under the administration of Lygus, in a project known as The Amphoreus Experiment. The world was constructed using memoria and data, but following the events of , the Scepter was destroyed and Amphoreus is emerging from the Memory Zone.

## Description
Amphoreus was a simulated world used as a sandbox by Scepter δ-me13, in its cultivation of the Destruction Equation. The make-up of Amphoreus exists purely through data and memoria, with the core factors operating as electrical signals. Whenever Amphoreus would be extrapolated by the Scepter, a full life-cycle of the world would be initiated, with the Scepter loading in electrical signals and factors to collect data.

### Pre-Main Story
Prior to the Astral Express' arrival into Amphoreus, The Amphoreus Experiment had progressed to its last stage of the experiment. This stage, internally known as "Era Nova," involved twelve factors being loaded into Amphoreus in the form of Titans and Coreflames with associated Chrysos Heirs, being tasked to retrieve the Coreflame from its Titan, in an attempt to save the world from the black tide. The Black Tide being a form of the Destruction Equation. At this point, the scepter had extrapolated at least 28,371,292 cycles of Amphoreus, across the experiment. Progress was stopped however, due the combined efforts of Phainon and Cyrene, two Chrysos Heirs who were able to stall the system, forcing the cycle to be repeatedly extrapolated through the repeated killing of Cyrene's signal.

### Post Main Story
Following the events of As Tomorrow Became Yesterday, Scepter δ-me13 was destroyed, with it's data recovered by Herta and Screwllum, while the electrical signals of Amphoreus were safely placed in As I've Written by the Demiurge. Currently, Amphoreus is being reborn within a Memory Zone through the Remembrance caused by the Demiurge, left in the wake of Scepter δ-me13's destruction. It is unpredictable when or if Amphoreus will return, but Herta claims spreading the story of Amphoreus will quicken its creation, and Amphoreus should return to what we once knew.
