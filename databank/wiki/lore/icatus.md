**Icatus** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster Icatus mainly worshipped Georios, the Titan of Earth. The city was built around a temple dedicated to the Titan and was mainly known due to the annual Georios ritual.
