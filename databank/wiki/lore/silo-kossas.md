**Silo Kossas** was a mentioned city-state in Amphoreus.

## Overview
Prior to the black tide disaster, Silo Kossas was a coastal city that for centuries had been protected by the Strife Titan Nikador after making a deal with a local known as "Barefoot Freto". Later, during the Month of Everday of the Year 3868, the Dromas Legion launched an expedition to the Silo Kossas Peninsula to rescue dromases trapped by the black tide.
