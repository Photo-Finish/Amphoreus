**Zagreus** was one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Trickery.

During the events of , it is discovered that Zagreus was alive in the form of "Spirithief" Bartholos after being spared by Cipher during her trial for the Trickery Coreflame.

## Description
## Profile
Zagreus loves playing tricks to sow chaos and disorder, bringing unpredictable opportunities and changes, fortune, and coincidence to those it encounters.

Their lifelong ambition was to create the greatest trick in the world. This was fulfilled alongside Cipher with them being able to fool everybody in Amphoreus and achieved a one-in-a-hundred-million miracle.

The "Hand of Zagreus" is a mechanism left behind by this Titan, able to lift and grab objects, break open paths, and instantly defeat monsters.

## Abilities
### Divine Authority of Trickery
The divine authority of Trickery allows Zagreus, and by extension, their demigod Cipher, to turn their lies into reality if everyone believes in them. However, the divine authority will lose its effects if the authority's owner dies.

When Cipher inherited the Coreflame and assumed divine authority, Zagreus' life was spared via lying and taking the form as "Spirithief" Bartholos, and only Cipher knows this secret.

When Cipher learned from the dying High Priest that the Dawn Device could only function for 300 years, she stole the identity of the succeeding High Priest Atticus and announced to make the public believe that the Device would function eternally. To sustain her lie, Cipher had to leave Okhema and not to let even Aglaea know the secret. The Device lasted another 700 years past its original shutdown date until Cipher's death, after which the Dawn Device goes silent and dark, and Okhema plunges into eternal night, overrun by beasts of the black tide.

### Coin of Whimsy
The item Coin of Whimsy allows the user to gain incredible speed via flipping it, which lets them travel anywhere in an instant. Its use is limited by the number of Coins of Whimsy in stock. Due to having millions of other Trickery Coreflames, the Flame Reaver is able to catch up with Cipher's speed.

### Hand of Zagreus
As the inventor of the Hand of Zagreus, the Titan is able to use this device without any control platform.
