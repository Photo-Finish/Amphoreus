## Description
## Profile
A fleet of skyships by a unknown city-state once tried to leave the planet and head to the stars. In response, Aquila would strike them down, reducing their subjects and the nation to ash once it launched. The Chrysos Heir, Seliose, would challenge Aquila and defeat them, but seeing the very sky about to collapse, abandoned the fight in order to prevent them from crashing down upon the world below.

Following Aglaea's death to Caenis and the Cleaners, Phainon ultimately decided to make a move on Aquila in order to claim their Coreflame, as that was the only one that was yet to be acquired for the Flame-Chase Journey. In order to reach Aquila, they would have to ascend to the "Fortress of Dome" Eye of Twilight. Upon reaching the sky fortress, the group &mdash; consisting of Phainon, Hyacine, the Trailblazer, Dan Heng, and Mem &mdash; encountered Seilose's companions, Lunabis and Solabis, who saw Hyacine's resolve and agreed to help the group face Aquila. Using the fortress' mechanisms, the group trapped Aquila, defeating and killing them. As the fortress began to collapse on itself, Aquila, now corrupted by the black tide, confronted them again. The group defeated them a second time and escaped the fortress.

## Abilities
### Illumination
When Aquila's eyes opened, light fills the world, and when their eyelids fall, darkness envelops the land. In the war against the Calamity Titans, many of Aquila's eyes were "blinded." This is why many city-states in Amphoreus are now shrouded in eternal night.

### Combative Abilities
Aquila was capable of polarizing the weather around them, could create powerful long-ranged attacks, and possessed the strength to destroy entire city-states.
