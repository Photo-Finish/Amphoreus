**Kephale** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus and is known as the father of all people. Their Coreflame is  Worldbearing. In the previous cycle of Amphoreus, they were a demigod of Worldbearing named Khaos.

## Description
## Profile
One of the "Three Titans of Creation," representing omniscience and omnipotence. As the God of Creation, Kephale is a symbol of saviors and heroes.

Kephale was entrusted by the pantheon with the burden of carrying life. They relinquished their throne of dominion over the world, solely to leave a glimmer of light for humanity.
