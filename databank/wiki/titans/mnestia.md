**Mnestia** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Romance.

## Description
## Profile
One of the "Three Titans of Creation". They and their Titankins pursue the beauty of the world, weaving it into golden threads to craft the romance of existence, granting the inhabitants of Amphoreus the gift of love and affection.

The conquerer of their Coreflame is Aglaea.
