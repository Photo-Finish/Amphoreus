**Oronyx's Miracle** is a major exploration mechanic found in Amphoreus, related to the Titans, Oronyx and Janus.

## Overview
Oronyx's Miracle is the name for a phenomena present on Amphoreus that trained individuals are capable of tapping into through Oronyx's Prayer, performing miraculous feats in the process. It works by extrapolating a potential past or future and bringing that into possibility. By using Oronyx's Miracle, anyone, often Priests of Janus or Oronyx, are able to change reality to match a new past or future state, such as repairing a broken pillar.
