## Text
Prologue*This manual is the guidance of the tripartite prophecy,**may Janus bestow upon us paths,**may Talanton bestow upon us fairness,**and may Oronyx bestow upon us revelations.*

Chapter 1: Ritual HoursMorning Ritual (Entry Hour)1. When opening the door of the temple, one must pray to Janus first.2. Light the three everbright lamps to symbolize the tripartite of destiny.3. Sweep the floors of the temple and change the offerings.

Dawn Ritual (Lucid Hour)1. Recite the Tripartite Ode.2. Record the questions asked by the visitors.3. Prepare offerings.

Dusk Ritual (Parting Hour)1. Light incense sticks to purify the altar.2. Place the offerings of the day.3. Recite the oracle's text.

Chapter 2: Ritual RulesBasic RulesFollow Talanton's Words: The offerings must bear the same weight as the prayer. This is the law of the scale, and must not be disobeyed.

Common Offerings and Their meanings1. Balance Coins: symbolizes the changes of destiny.2. Crystals: symbolizes the clarity of the prophecies.3. Honey: symbolizes the sweetness of languages.4. Clothes: symbolizes the curtain of Oronyx.5. Mirrors: symbolizes the two faces of Janus.6. Scale: symbolizes the fairness of Talanton.Note: Offerings must be complete and of the purest quality.

Taboos1. One must not offer items of misfortune.2. One must not offer items of others.3. One must not offer items during the Entry Hour.

Chapter 3: PrayerMorning RitualLady of the paths shall show us the way, the king of fairness shall keep the balance, and the spirit of the night shall grand us wisdom. I knock on the door to ask for an audience with the divine, I make offerings to ask about the Hereafter, and I bow down to ask for guidance.

Inquiry PrayerLife is but a leaf, adrift with waves. Tripartite fate, to what end does it lead? Janus illuminates my path, Talanton steadies my course, and Oronyx shows me the truth.

Gratitude PrayerI thank the tripartite fate for heeding me and offering guidance. May the eternal shine never dim, and may the divine words forever endure.

Chapter 4: Prophecy RulesPreparation1. The maiden must bathe and purify herself.2. She must wear a white ritual robe and a silver mask.3. She must hold the two-faced staff of Janus.4. She must light the tri-colored holy fire.

Prophecy Procedures1. The assistant priest will light the incense and guide the way.2. The maiden will ascend to the seat of destiny.3. The visitor will place their offerings.4. The maiden will read the revelations.5. The maiden will read the prophecy once destiny indicates the result.

Prophetic Records1. All prophecies must be recorded on special sheepskin scrolls.2. Prophecies must be recorded with ink made from silver powder.3. Three witnesses must provide their signatures.4. Prophecies will be kept in a secret chamber within the room of prophecy.

Chapter 5: Special CeremoniesNew Moon CeremonyThe New Moon ritual must be held at the beginning of every month. At that time:1. All the gates of the tripartite temple shall be opened.2. A twelve-sided mirror shall be placed to symbolize the wheel of destiny.3. The maiden shall lead everyone around the temple three times.4. The Long Ballad of Fate shall be recited.

Janus' Maze Revelational CeremonyExclusively held for important questions:1. Set up an inquiry magic circle.2. The maiden casts a curse to open the gateway.3. Place a double-sided mirror into the magic circle.4. Decipher the reflection in the mirror.5. Record the revelation.

AfterwordThis manual was created and edited by the maidens of past generations. May these rules be followed to obtain the truth. Violators shall face the punishment of destiny.
