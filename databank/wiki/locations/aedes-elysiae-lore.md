**Aedes Elysiae** is a small village on Amphoreus. It is the hometown of Cyrene and Phainon.

## Overview
According to Phainon, Aedes Elysiae was destroyed by the Flame Reaver at some point in the past. During his time undergoing the Trial of the Strife Coreflame, Phainon was shown memories of this incident, including a "blood-red half-sun hung in the sky" and the people "lying in a sea of flames," as well as the murder of his childhood friend, Cyrene.

### Etymology
* Aedes is an alternate form of the Latin word *aedis*, meaning "temple" or "tomb." Elysiae is the genitive form of the feminine Latin word *Elysia*, meaning "of Elysium." Together, Aedes Elysiae could mean "Temple of Elysium" or "Tomb of Elysium."
