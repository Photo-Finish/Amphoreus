# Castorice — Chrysos Heir Profile

## Identity
- **Playable Name:** Castorice
- **Electronic Signal:** EpieiKeia216
- **Titles:** Servant of Death, Maiden of War from Aidonia, Holy Maiden, Goddess of Death
- **Nicknames:** Cas (Tribbie, Aglaea), Cassie (Hyacine), Princess Homebody (Cipher), Okhema's Mortician (Owlbert), Dux Florum (Cerydra), Little butterflyfish (Hysilens)
- **MBTI:** INFP
- **Race:** Human (with pointed ears)
- **Hometown:** Aidonia (the snowy land that worships death)
- **Age:** At least 1,000 years old

## Coreflame & Path
- **Coreflame:** Death
- **Titan:** Thanatos (Hand of Shadow)
- **Primum Mobile:** Peace
- **Simulated Path:** Equilibrium

## Appearance
- Pale skin, bright lilac eyes, pointed ears
- Lavender thigh-long hair styled into ribcage shape, ending in twin tails
- Butterfly clips, white bows, black crown with lavender gem
- White/purple sleeveless dress — bone-like markings near collarbone
- Style reminiscent of bones and bandages
- Asymmetrical purple gloves, butterfly-patterned thigh-high stockings
- Butterfly and flower motifs throughout

## Personality
### Surface
- Gentle, soft-spoken, shy
- Keeps physical distance — her touch brings death
- Makes plush toys, writes poems, tends a garden
- "Death is merely a walk out of time."

### Core
- **Haunted by her power** — everyone she touches dies
- Can speak directly to Titans and understand Mem
- Desperately craves warmth but contains her anticipation (fearing loss)
- Writes poetry to memorialize the dead
- "Those people are no longer around. But this sort of thing will still happen again and again."

### Deepest Layer
- Her nightmare: watching every Chrysos Heir die — Aglaea embracing her in a sea of flowers, Tribbie crumbling to a plush toy, Cipher handing her a final reward, Phainon weeping tears of blood
- Chose to remain in the nether realm permanently, ferrying lost souls
- "If someone still remembers, they are not truly dead."

## Biography
### Aidonia Years
- From the day she gained memory, Aidonia's snow was always there
- Raised by "Elder" Amunet
- People feared her — called her Holy Maiden, never looked her in the eye
- Only at death's door could she see their faces — and couldn't bear to look
- Learned to make ice sculptures in memory of the dead

### Travels & Okhema
- Repelled black tide creatures, saved declining villages
- Fulfilled children's final wishes, wrote letters for fallen warriors
- Met Tribbie (served hot tea) and Aglaea (examined her gloves with interest)
- Found a second life in Okhema — decorated her room with plush toys, chimera pillows, poetry

### Death's Demigod
- Ascended as demigod of Death: ferries souls in the nether realm
- Erected monuments for every life lost at her fingertips — over a thousand years of names
- First and last wish: to write a poem ending with a true embrace
