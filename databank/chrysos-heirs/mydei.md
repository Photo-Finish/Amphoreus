# Mydei — Chrysos Heir Profile

## Identity
- **Playable Name:** Mydei
- **True Name:** Mydeimos
- **Electronic Signal:** PoleMos600
- **Titles:** The Last Prince, Champion of Strife, Guardian of Amphoreus, King of Kremnos
- **Nicknames:** De/Lil' Dei (Tribbie), Golden-Haired Lion (Cipher), Little sea lion (Hysilens)
- **MBTI:** ISTJ
- **Race:** Human (immortal via Sea of Souls)
- **Hometown:** Castrum Kremnos
- **Father:** King Eurypon (threw Mydei into the Sea of Souls at birth)

## Coreflame & Path
- **Coreflame:** Strife
- **Titan:** Nikador (Lance of Fury)
- **Primum Mobile:** Restraint
- **Simulated Path:** The Hunt

## Appearance
- Tall, well-built, messy strawberry blond hair with red ends, braided lock on right
- Golden earring with sapphire on left ear, yellow sun-shaped irises
- Crimson tattoos that light up during abilities
- Large necklace with golden plates and sapphires
- Dark maroon/red robe, golden pauldron on left shoulder
- Two golden gauntlets, black/gold belt with sun buckle

## Personality
### Core
- **Honor-bound warrior-king** — "Kingslayer be king, godslayer be god."
- Proud, solitary, burdened by duty
- Immortal — dies and resurrects repeatedly (thousands of times)
- Leads with action, not words — annotations in books are single words: "Strong," "Keep moving"
- Drinks pomegranate juice with goat's milk and cheese

### Fatal Flaw
- **Pride and isolation** — the lone lion. Even his own Council of Elders accuses him of abandoning Kremnos's spirit.
- Bears the weight of his people alone

## Biography
### Birth & Childhood
- Father Eurypon threw him into the Sea of Souls at birth (fearing a prophecy)
- Resurrected and reforged himself no less than 10,000 times over 9 years
- Became the "king of the seas" of fisherman legend
- Tore apart a giant monster, staining the sea red for miles

### Military Campaigns
- Led the Kremnoan Detachment — a lone army never flying Kremnos's flag
- Famous battles: Ladon, Aidonia, Aenionus
- Battle of Aenionus: tore open Klytius's throat with his fist
- Loyal companions: Hephaestion, Perdikkas, Leonnius, Ptolemy, Peucesta
- All were mortal — they aged and died while Mydei lived on

### Chrysos Heir
- Ten-day death match against Phainon
- Defeated Nikador (maddened Titan of Strife) with the Chrysos Heirs
- Inherited Strife Coreflame — promised to hold the black tide at bay
- Settled his people in Okhema but never truly submitted to foreign rule
- Returned to Kremnos to face the black tide alone — "As with the Sea of Souls, as it has always been."

## Historical Parallels
- Strong parallels to **Alexander the Great** — his companions are named after Alexander's generals (Hephaestion, Perdiccas, Leonnatus, Ptolemy, Peucestas)
- Teacher Krateros = Craterus (Macedonian general)
- Battle of Thermopylae reference in Knowledge voice-over

## Etymology
- **Mydeimos**: Deimos (δεῖμος = "dread/terror") + my- (myriad). "Ten-thousand terrors"
- **Wàndí** (万敌): 万 = ten-thousand, 敌 = enemy. "Equal to ten-thousand foes"
- **PoleMos600**: πόλεμος (pólemos) = "war, conflict"
