# Hysilens — Chrysos Heir Profile

## Identity
- **Playable Name:** Hysilens
- **True Name:** Helektra
- **Electronic Signal:** ApoRia432
- **Titles:** Daughter of the Sea, Dux Gladiorum, Knight Commander of Okhema, Wave-Strumming Knight, General of the Flame-Chase Army, Imperator's Blade
- **Nicknames:** Fifth Overlord / Boss (by The Five Gourmet Overlords)
- **MBTI:** ISFP
- **Race:** Sea Siren
- **Hometown:** The ocean depths (later Styxia)

## Coreflame & Path
- **Coreflame:** Ocean
- **Titan:** Phagousa (Chalice of Plenty)
- **Primum Mobile:** Self-Negation
- **Simulated Path:** Nihility

## Appearance
- Pale skin, long black hair fading to plum, light blue underneath
- Indigo/light blue/lilac eyes, white scales on left cheek
- Translucent abdomen with bubbling light-blue water exposing ribcage and spine
- Shell and coral accessories throughout
- Violin made of seashells, also serves as dual blades

## Personality
### Surface
- **Mute warrior** — rarely speaks, communicates through music and sword
- Her violin became two blades; "only sharp requiems now echoing from the strings"
- Distant, cold, seemingly unreachable

### Core
- **Deeply romantic and longing** — a siren who sang of the sea
- Gave Cerydra nicknames for everyone: "little golden trout" (Aglaea), "little hostage" (Tribbie), "little sea lion" (Mydei), "little butterflyfish" (Castorice), "kitten fish" (Cipher)
- Longed for a feast that never ends — "The show must go on."

### Deepest Layer
- Her Primum Mobile is "Self-Negation" — the Nihility-aligned drive to erase oneself
- "What a fish truly needs is simply a sea to call home..."
- Before the final bubble bursts, she smiles — having expressed everything her heart held

## Biography
### Siren Princess
- Chased bubbles in coral reefs, sang with sea sirens
- Led sirens against the black tide corrupting the ocean
- Day after day sang to keep morale: "Don't give up, my friends, not until we've drowned ourselves in triumph."
- Dove into the abyss — her song the lone light in the dark ocean

### The Gladius
- Emerged from the sea to find the promised feast was nowhere — only corpses and war
- Her voice rasped in polluted air, slowly chose silence over speech
- Became "a blade that would never rust" — her music became weapons
- "Everyone was just another lost fish, blindly swimming toward the light."

### Cerydra's Blade
- Followed Cerydra: "Hysilens, follow me. I will grant you everything you seek."
- Watched the blue flame fight battle after battle, always victorious
- At Cerydra's banquet: sang again for the first time in ages — "How bright it was here. How lovely it sounded."
- Was the one who executed Cerydra's final command — snuffed out the very flame she swam toward

### After the Imperator
- Spent a thousand years fleeing those dazzling bubbles of memory
- Learned chess from Cerydra's memory: "If they're bound to disappear from the board, why bother moving forward at all?"
- Cerydra's answer: "Isn't the journey forward fulfilling enough on its own?"

## Etymology
- **Hysilens**: Possibly Silenus (Greek deity) or ὕσμᾰ (húsma, "to rain") + ἶλιγξ (îlinx, "whirlpool")
- **Helektra**: Electra (Greek mythological character)
- **ApoRia432**: ἀπορία (aporía) = "impasse, puzzlement" — a philosophical conundrum
