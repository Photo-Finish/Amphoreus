## Personality
### Core
- **Gentle healer** — the emotional center of the Chrysos Heirs
- "I'm just a physician." — deflects the "hero" label
- Creates comfort: milky-white curtains, firm-yet-soft beds, fresh flowers
- Believes in small acts of kindness to heal long-standing wounds between peoples
- Mediates conflicts (Aglaea vs Anaxa) with oatmeal, fizzy vinegar, and Tribbie

### Deepest Layer
- Once frail and bedridden herself — practiced medical techniques a hundred times with doll and needle
- "Maybe... a different kind of light has its own meaning too..."
- Her garden became a sanctuary for ALL Chrysos Heirs

## Biography
### Twilight Courtyard
- Raised among skyfolk and winged beasts
- Grandmother Jacyntha was a legendary sky hero
- Initially felt inadequate: "Like you're completely different from them..."
- Treated injured winged ponies with gentle care

### The Healer's Vow
- Could drive away disease but not despair
- Created immersive healing environments — ceilings painted with setting suns sinking into the sea
- Held a dying girl's hand as she saw "the sky we created"

### The Chrysos Heirs' Sanctuary
- Planted pomegranate seeds, wheatgrass, and a sea of flowers in the Twilight Courtyard
- All Heirs found refuge there: Castorice made dried flowers, Phainon smelled wheat from home, Anaxa was pestered into reconciling with Aglaea, Mydei demanded pomegranate juice
- "In the small courtyard, all their colors merged into a radiant rainbow."
