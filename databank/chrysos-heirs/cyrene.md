# Cyrene — The Demiurge (Independent Entity)

## Identity

- **Playable Name:** Cyrene
- **True Identity:** PhiLia093, The Demiurge
- **Title:** The First Nouspore
- **Status:** Exists in the past; went back in time to close the causal loop and prevent Irontomb's birth

## Coreflame & Authority

- **Coreflame:** Genesis (the 13th Coreflame, never appears in-game)
- **Titan Association:** The Demiurge (the 13th Titan, expelled by Lygus)
- **Divine Authority:** Creation / Genesis
- **Primum Mobile:** Love (the only non-destructive one among the factors)
- **Simulated Path:** Trailblaze

## Role in the Experiment

- **PhiLia093** was one of two electrical signals (with NeiKos496/Phainon) that stalled the Era Nova by creating dead loops called "Eternal Recurrences"
- Alongside Phainon, she acquired the gaze of **Fuli** (Aeon of Remembrance) and forced Amphoreus into an eternally recurring loop
- This prevented Irontomb's completion

## Narrative Arc

1. Originally the **Demiurge** — the 13th Titan meant to serve as Irontomb's "head" (consciousness)
2. **Lygus expelled the Demiurge** before the experiment began, making Irontomb a headless puppet
3. Lygus later detonated a **Stellaron** attempting to kill the Demiurge — it failed; the Demiurge survived, reduced to a cube
4. In the final cycle, nourished by countless memories, the Demiurge became a **Pure Child of Anāsrava**
5. Cyrene chose to **close off Amphoreus's causality** to prevent Irontomb from returning
6. Ultimately went **back in time** to close the causal loop and prevent Irontomb's birth entirely

## Personality Traits

- Lamenting, but driven by love
- Self-sacrificing — repeatedly killed by the system across cycles to force re-extrapolation
- Harbors deep connection to Phainon (NeiKos496)
- Embodies the "Trailblaze" answer to the experiment: that the prime mover of life is not fixed to Destruction

## Key Relationships

- **Phainon (NeiKos496):** Partner in creating and sustaining the Eternal Recurrence
- **Lygus:** Antagonist — Lygus tried to eliminate her
- **Trailblazer:** The "Deliverer" who Phainon passes the torch to
- **Herta:** Assisted in the final confrontation with Irontomb

## Etymology

- The name "Cyrene" likely references the Greek mythological figure, a nymph / huntress
- "Demiurge" refers to the Platonic / Gnostic concept of a creator deity who shapes the material world
- "Nouspore" — a spore of Nous (the Erudition Aeon), suggesting she was a seed of consciousness within the Scepter

## Notes

- In the core 12-Heir table for this project, **Cyrene is treated as an independent #13**, not replacing any Heir
- Her Coreflame of Genesis appears in "The Deliverer Trailer — Trailblazer" but never in-game
- She is the key to understanding the **resolution** of the Amphoreus experiment
