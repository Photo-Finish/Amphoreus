# Evernight — Chrysos Heir Profile

## Identity
- **Playable Name:** Evernight
- **Base Character:** March 7th (dark/mirror aspect — "the complete March 7th")
- **Race:** Memetic Entity (child of Remembrance)
- **Combat Path:** Remembrance
- **Memosprite:** Evey

## Coreflame & Path
- **Coreflame:** Time
- **Titan:** Oronyx (Veil of Evernight)
- **Simulated Path:** Remembrance

## Role in Amphoreus
Evernight is March 7th's dark/mirror aspect — a child of Remembrance who emerged from the Memory Zone. She entered Amphoreus during the Eternal Recurrence #33,550,337 when March 7th fell ill and couldn't come.

### Core Motivation
- Protects March 7th and her companions at any cost
- Writes letters to March 7th from deep within — signed "Evernight ♭"
- "Don't worry, I will guard the path of Trailblaze for you... at any cost. ♭"

## Personality
### Surface
- Mysterious, powerful, speaks with detached elegance
- Uses ♭ (flat symbol) as signature punctuation
- Views herself as a protector who must do "the cruel part"

### Core
- **Fiercely protective** of March 7th and the Astral Express Crew
- Was once hunted by the Garden of Recollection and various factions
- Has wandered through Penacony's memories, Scalegorge Waterscape, Jarilo-VI's storms
- Drawn to the Express's light: "like a shooting star"

### Deepest Layer
- Considered using "Rain of Sensation" to cleanse Amphoreus — wiping all painful memories into Oblivion
- Ultimately chose NOT to — because March 7th's Trailblaze is "a journey that carries the past, connects the present, and sails forward with courage"
- "The best gift I can leave you... is a blank canvas for you to paint on your own."
- March 7th's response: "We all need to learn to grow up. Look! I just took a big step forward!"
