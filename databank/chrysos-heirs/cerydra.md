## Biography
### The Beggar Princess
- A wounded beggar girl with unusual blue hair stood out in the snow of Hyperborea
- An ambitious aristocrat took her in, spread rumors of a lost princess
- Golden blood confirmed her "royalty" — but she was just a pawn
- The aristocrat became regent; she became a puppet princess
- Learned chess from a teacher — mastered every strategy

### The Chessmaster's Rise
- Built a network: favors to maids/guards (eyes), charity to citizens (rooks), conspirators as knights, chancellor as bishop
- Sent to lead a doomed military campaign — turned it into conquest
- Crushed Loukas's rebellion, recruited General Fortunado
- Returned triumphant: "From this day forward, I shall bring justice to Hyperborea!"
- Sentenced the false regent-king to death by fire, crowned herself Empress

### Chrysos Heir Leadership
- United all Chrysos Heirs to kill corrupted Titans and take Coreflames
- With Hysilens: defeated Loukas/Icaria/Corinth alliance; ended the Chrysos War; started first Flame-Chase Journey
- Established Okhema's Council where Chrysos Heirs and commoners shared the right to debate
- "To challenge gods who see themselves as rulers, and even venture beyond the sky... now that's a game worth playing!"

### The Ultimate Sacrifice
- Rewrote Scepter δ-me13's Ultimate Protocol
- Marked Lygus as malicious intruder, froze 60% of his admin access
- Transferred access to Herta and Screwllum
- Additional directive: if Era Nova becomes abnormal, wait for Irontomb
- "Only through a worthy sacrifice can we gain a befitting victory."
