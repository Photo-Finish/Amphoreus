## Role in Amphoreus
Dan Heng arrived in Amphoreus aboard the Astral Express coach with the Trailblazer. The coach was attacked and crashed. Separated from the Express, he became an "invading variable" in the Eternal Recurrence #33,550,337.

### Key Moments
- **Crash landing**: Wrecked car, Trailblazer gravely wounded. Used spirit water to heal. Shielded Trailblazer from Black Tide Creatures. Cloud-Piercer shattered.
- **The Archiver**: Explored Amphoreus, recorded everything, dueled Mydei, studied with Hyacine
- **Witnessed all farewells**: Tribbie's thousand forms withered, Anaxa reduced to ashes, Castorice's final farewell
- **Earth Coreflame**: The fading Terravox entrusted him the Coreflame. Fused his Vidyadhara bloodline with the earth of Amphoreus.
- **"Rise from your dormancy, and follow me as soaring dragons of the wilderness!"**

## Personality (in Amphoreus context)
- **Protective guardian** — shields Trailblazer, worries about March 7th
- Observer and archiver by nature, but this journey couldn't be reduced to "cold entries"
- Haunted by nightmares of losing Trailblazer
- "If one day, we too shall come to this..."
- Ultimately: "I am... the one who guards the path of Trailblaze!"
