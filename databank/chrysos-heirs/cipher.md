## Appearance
- Light gray hair with white streaks, left side braided with white bow
- Bright cyan eyes, dark blue pupils, golden rings around irises
- Cat ears (covered by hood), white-to-dark-brown tail
- Black, blue-lined hooded bodysuit with gold details, open back
- Long fingerless gloves with sharp yellow-painted nails
- Golden thigh-high armor, boots gifted by Aglaea
- Cat-eyed gems on shoulders and back

## Personality
### Surface
- Playful, irreverent, always hungry
- "Tryna trick me? Not a chance!"
- "All I can do, is run!"
- "Hmph! What Flame-Chase? What heroes? I smell shady schemes afoot!"
- Loves fish, suffers aquaphobia, can't eat chocolate
- Tosses coins in the air, leaves paw-print proclamations

### Core
- **Survivor** — grew up starving on the streets of Dolos
- Collects and unites outcasts — the 300 Rogues of Dolos
- Deep loyalty to those who showed her kindness (Aglaea, Tribbie)
- "For those with golden blood, losses are a constant on the Flame-Chase journey..."
- "Only you know where your path ends..."

### Deepest Layer
- Stole the Gem of All Worlds and fled Okhema — not out of greed, but as part of a larger plan
- Returns centuries later, hood drawn low, watching from shadows
- Helps from the dark while pretending to be just a thief

## Biography
### Dolos Street Rat
- Starving thief in Dolos — stole bread, dried fruit, candles from temples
- Chased by hounds through streets, fell from a 300-meter-high wall into icy waters
- "So... hungry..." — pyrrhic victory in a race with her life on the line

### The Weaving Workshop
- Exiled to Okhema, targeted Aglaea's workshop
- Drank honey brew, ate dolmadakia, toyed with crystal chess pieces
- Passed out drunk — woke to a perfumed letter from Aglaea
- "Little Glutton: Did you enjoy your fun?"
- Aglaea never held her accountable — instead fed her and told stories
- Tribbie always had food ready, called her "Ciphy"

### The 300 Rogues
- United all 300 Rogues of Dolos
- Staged the most spectacular performance in Amphoreus
- The Coin of Whimsy fell — three hundred Thief Stars streaked across the sky
- Tyrants toppled, slavers fled, gold rained upon the starving
- Zagreus couldn't resist — revealed themselves, fell into Chrysos Heirs' ambush

### The Gem Theft
- Stole the Gem of All Worlds and fled Okhema — became a legendary phantom thief
- Centuries later, returned — helped from the shadows
- Watched Phainon return in triumph, communicated with Aglaea via golden threads
- "Seamstress, all these years, and your golden threads are sharp as ever."
