## Personality
### Surface
- Cheerful, innocent, childlike
- Calls everyone by nicknames: Snowy (Phainon), Agy (Aglaea), De (Mydei), Naxy (Anaxa), Cas (Castorice), Ciphy (Cipher)
- Sings nursery rhymes
- "See you tomorrow!"

### Core
- **Ancient and exhausted** — the first demigod. Has watched a thousand of her selves die.
- Her diary entries grow progressively weaker in handwriting
- "Losses are a constant on the Flame-Chase journey."
- Carries the weight of every Tri▇ who died spreading the prophecy

### The Tri-Personality
- **Tribbie**: The leader/main consciousness, most energetic
- **Trianne**: The gentle one, taken hostage by soldiers, escaped via Century Gate
- **Trinnon**: The quiet writer who kept the diary
- They share one consciousness but are three bodies. Each time one "dies," consciousness grows hazier.

## Biography
### The First Demigod
- Originally **Tribios** — Holy Maiden of Janusopolis
- Black tide arrived in Era Bellica, destroyed multiple city-states
- Tribios took Janus's Coreflame — became the **first demigod**
- Paid the price: **self-fragmentation** — split into a thousand messengers
- Spread the prophecy of the Chrysos Heirs across all Amphoreus

### The Thousand Deaths
- Over centuries, her fragments died one by one — struck by arrows, assassins' daggers, poisoned
- By the time of the story, only three remain: Tribbie, Trianne, Trinnon
- The Century Gate (Janus's power) allows them to escape — but each use costs more consciousness

### Okhema Years
- Became teacher to young Aglaea — taught her about leadership and politics
- The Cleaners (Council of Elders assassins) repeatedly targeted them
- Through the Century Gate, they escaped — but consciousness grew hazier still
- Final dream: walking endless paths in darkness, then growing so large they could touch the sky — reunited with all their dead selves at the destination
