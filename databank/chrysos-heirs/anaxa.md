## Appearance
- Young man with long light green hair in ponytail over right shoulder
- Pale aqua eyes with magenta pupils
- Red tattoo on right hand
- Black eyepatch over left eye (gold-patterned)
- Black/teal jacket, white capelet, teal 8-pointed star on chest
- Crystal teal shoes with red soles, golden jewelry

## Personality
### Core
- **Radical skeptic** — actively defies the prophecy despite being a Chrysos Heir
- Pursues truth above all else: "In a world full of lies, I am the only truth."
- Challenges authority, encourages students to challenge his own authority
- Cold, analytical, but deeply passionate about knowledge
- Loves dromases

### Fatal Flaw
- **Hubris** — believes he alone sees truth. Willing to be a blasphemer, a heretic.
- "It is already a sin to transcend the gods, so what if you become a god?"

## Biography
### Childhood
- Parents departed early; raised by older sister Diotima
- Reclusive, isolated — hid in tree shadows picking up fallen leaves
- Constantly questioned: "Why do dromases not fly toward the sky?" "If gods are omnipotent, why do they fear death?"
- Frequently thrown out of the temple by furious priests
- Sister bought him books and tools with meager income
- At 5: declared he'd become the most knowledgeable person in the world
- Sister raised travel funds for him to study at the Grove
- Halfway there: black tide struck — returned to find his home a corroded ruin

### Scholar Years
- Studied at the Grove of Epiphany
- Conducted forbidden experiments: golden blood analysis, Titan soul dissection, soul fusion
- His left eye now sees "souls" of humans — acquired through near-death
- Branded with three great sins: academic defamation, corrupting youths, religious heresy
- Taught Phainon and Castorice — they could render him speechless

### The Blasphemer's End
- Proposed that Titan souls and human souls are no different — constructed from seeds of wisdom
- Raced toward death to prove his theory
- Body became vessel to forge Coreflames, soul became chains that force gods to bow
- "My future students... step over my body and take the torch from my hands. Use me as the firewood to ignite the flames that will bring light to the truth!"
