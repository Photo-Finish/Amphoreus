## Appearance
- Tall woman with short golden-blonde hair, blue-green/yellow eyes
- White flowers and golden laurel wreaths in hair
- Cream toga-style gown, open-shoulder right sleeve, golden embellishments throughout
- Golden choker with teardrop gem, golden tattoo on left chest
- Golden gladiator sandals, golden nail polish
- Gold cape-like fabric draped down right side

## Personality
### Surface
- Elegant, refined noblewoman who leads with grace
- The "Dressmaster of Okhema" — fashion trendsetter
- Maintains the World Wound Web (Amphoreus's internet)

### Core
- **Pragmatic and calculating** — uses ruthless methods (interrogation, surveillance golden threads)
- Sees through people's inner turmoil despite blindness
- Deeply committed to the prophecy and Chrysos Heirs' mission
- "If one thread isn't enough, then let a myriad of threads lift the sky"

### Deepest Layer
- Has not crafted "beautiful garments" in a long time — instead weaves the threads of destiny
- Bears the weight of every comrade lost on the Flame-Chase Journey
- Dresses in plain clothes — the ultimate beauty isn't garments, it's the golden future she glimpsed upon taking Mnestia's Coreflame
- "In that golden future, there is no more strife, no more death."

## Biography
### Childhood
- Born to a family devoted to Mnestia for generations
- From a young age: determined to find "the existence that surpasses all others in beauty"
- Dismissed painting, music, sculpture, drama as insufficient
- Priest recognized her brilliance: "You're destined to craft works that even the gods would envy"

### Chrysos Heir Years
- Became priest of Mnestia and Dressmaster of Okhema
- Collected tattered fabric scraps from fallen warriors
- Destroyed lavish garments when nobles proved unworthy
- Took Mnestia's Coreflame — upon taking it, suddenly "saw" the golden future despite blindness
- Gathered the world's heroes and led the Flame-Chase Journey

### Leadership
- Entered the political fray, sparked debates in the Council of Elders
- Weaves diverse threads of fate into a resilient net
- Every Chrysos Heir passes through her workshop
- Uses golden threads for surveillance and security
- "The Chrysos Heirs will together weave a dazzlingly bright future."
