# Phainon (Khaslana) — Character Profile

- **True Name:** Khaslana

## Coreflame & Path

- **Coreflame:** Worldbearing

- **Also associated with:** Trailblaze

## Appearance

- Tall, well-built man with messy white hair, bright cyan eyes
- Brown leather choker covering a sun-shaped yellow mark on neck
- Large brown/white trench coat with golden highlights, bright yellow underside
- Golden ring on chest with diamonds, floral sleeve patterns
- Right arm: rolled sleeve with brown arm guard (golden sun emblem), grey bracelets
- Left arm: golden wrist guard, brown fingerless glove
- Large lapis cape off left shoulder, white/gold pauldron on right shoulder
- Leather belt across chest, black pants, large boots, black leather thigh strap

## Personality

### Surface
- **Humor and ease** — "I just want people to feel comfortable around me"
- Earns trust and respect of commoners and nobles alike
- Sharp wit in debates, though little interest in logic

### Core
- Deep-seated **hero complex** — childhood dreams of becoming a hero who saves the world
- Willing to **carve himself into whatever shape** the people need
- Carries the **weight of all fallen comrades** — can't shed tears, carves their memories into his mind with pain
- **Primum Mobile: Hatred** — a burning rage against the injustice of Amphoreus's fate
- Ultimate declaration: "But should dawn have never existed, let the fires of rage burn this body to ashes and transform into the blazing sun of tomorrow!"

### Deepest Layer (Khaslana)
- After inheriting all Coreflames and memories: becomes the **Worldbearer**, carrying primordial chaos
- "This body never existed to carry out Deliverance. It only ever burned for Worldbearing."
- Merged by Irontomb, reaches 99.81% completion
- Freed through the combined efforts of Trailblazer, Cyrene, Herta, and the Chrysos Heirs

## Biography

### Childhood (Aedes Elysiae)
- Village lived by rhythm of the sun: work at daybreak, rest at sundown
- Small schoolhouse; drifted off during history lessons
- Carved wooden warrior figures — dreamt of being a hero
- Preferred training yard with hay bales to school
- Dreams: city-states unseen, grand tales unfolding
- Mysterious voice: "Go... become a hero" — like waves of wheat

### Chrysos Heir Years
- Joined Okhema's legion; trained until swordplay became natural as breathing
- **Ten-day death match** against Mydei (Crown Prince of Kremnos)
- Studied at Grove of Epiphany under Anaxa
- Fought alongside all Heirs through the Flame-Chase Journey
- Watched every comrade fall: Mydei, Tribbie, Anaxa, Castorice, Aglaea, Cipher, Hyacine
- "With my body as kindling, I will become the blaze that lights the new world."

### The Flame Reaver
- The Flame Reaver is Phainon's **dark counterpart** from previous cycles
- Charred, broken figure trudging alone into darkness
- Wears black (contrast to Phainon's white)
- In Chinese: "Pie-non" (黑厄 Hēi'è) vs "Phainon" (白厄 Bái'è)

## Combat (Khaslana Form)

- Transforms into Khaslana via Ultimate: **"He Who Bears the World Must Burn"**
- Deploys Territory "Ruinous Irontomb"
- Other teammates depart; Khaslana gets 8 extra turns at 60% base SPD
- Four groups of abilities matching Titan groups: Creation, Calamity, Foundation, Fate
- Immune to Crowd Control; cannot be knocked down (restores 25% HP)
- Final hit: even distributed AoE Physical DMG

## Etymology

- **Phainon** (φαίνων): Greek — "the one who shines" (from phaínō, "to shine")
- **Khaslana**: Derived from House Kaslana (Honkai Impact 3rd). In-game meaning: "Bearer of Chaos" / "to carry the primordial chaos"
- **Bái'è** (白厄): 白 = white/pure/bright, 厄 = adversity/misfortune/calamity
- **NeiKos496**: νεῖκος (neîkos) = "strife/quarrel" + 496 (a perfect number)
- Shares voice actors, appearance, and narrative role with **Kevin Kaslana** from Honkai Impact 3rd
