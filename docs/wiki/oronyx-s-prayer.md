**Oronyx's Prayer** is an exploration mechanic found in Amphoreus.

## Description
Oronyx's Prayer is an ability gained by the Trailblazer while in Amphoreus, allowing them to tap into the power of Oronyx, the Time Titan, and allows them to locally rewind time on specific objects. This ability can be used to repair many of the damaged objects around Amphoreus as well as used in many puzzles found throughout.
