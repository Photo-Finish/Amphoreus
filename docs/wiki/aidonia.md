**Aidonia** is one of the major cities found on Amphoreus.

## Overview
Aidonia is known as the snow-dusted kingdom of death, located in Amphoreus. It was established by Eleusis, the leader of the Ascetics, who sought an end to the endless cycles of life. This eerie city is deeply connected to the faith of Thanatos, one of 12 titans of Amphoreus. The Aidonians built colossal monuments on the snow plains to honor the Holy Maiden who brings them the blessing of death, which whom is currently Castorice.
