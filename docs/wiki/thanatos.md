**Thanatos** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Death. In the previous cycle of Amphoreus, they were a demigod of Death named Polyxia.

## Description
## Profile
Thanatos, using the tides from the River of Souls, guides the dead to the afterlife. They, alongside the Titan of Strife, Nikador, and the Titan of Trickery, Zagreus, descended upon Amphoreus and incited a civil war.

Castorice is one of their servants.

## Abilities
### Hand of Shadow
The authority of death is to pass judgment on the living, operating on a pair of hands. The left hand grants death and welcomes souls into the nether realm so they can reincarnate, while the right hand rejects souls and returns them to the mortal realm.

Due to the circumstances behind Castorice's birth, she received the left hand, while the right hand remained with Thanatos. This caused any mortal she came into contact with to immediately disintegrate, preventing her from properly embracing anyone until she acquired the right hand by becoming a demigod.
