**Styxia** is one of the major cities found on Amphoreus.

## Overview
Before being attacked by an evil dragon, Styxia was the city that primarily worshipped Phagousa, the Titan of Ocean. Styxia is located near the River of Souls and its inhabitants were mostly alchemists.
