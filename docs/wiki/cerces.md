**Cerces** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Reason. In the previous cycle of Amphoreus, they were a demigod of Reason named Calypso.

## Description
## Profile
One of the "Three Titans of Creation," Cerces manifested in the world as a great sacred tree, permitting humans to walk beside them, awaken enlightenment, and reflect deeply to gain knowledge.
