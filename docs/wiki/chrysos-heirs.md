The **Chrysos Heirs** are a group of gold-blooded individuals on Amphoreus, some of whom by prophecy are tasked with plucking the Coreflames from the Titans and upholding the world, also referred to as the "Flame-Chase."

Both the Chrysos Heirs and Titans are later revealed to be electrical signals within The Amphoreus Experiment, with their blood being a manifestation of Nanook's golden blood.

## Overview
While there are only twelve Coreflames, there exist many more Chrysos Heirs "not mentioned in the prophecy." By conquering a Coreflame's Trial, a Chrysos Heir inherits its respective Titan's divinity and becomes a demigod, although only a chosen few possess the capacity to do so.

In the logs of Scepter δ-me13, the Chrysos Heirs and Titans are described as electrical signals created from the basis of the "twelve factors," conjugate constructs mirroring Aeons and Pathstriders. The twelve factors, a core variable created by Lygus and inserted into The Amphoreus Experiment, are simplified models of life's Primum Mobile. By having Chrysos Heirs inherit the Titans' Coreflames, the electrical signals iterated and improved through rivalry and inheritance.

Additionally, the golden blood of the Chrysos Heirs was the product of the first experimental phase of The Amphoreus Experiment. During the experiment's 50,121st cycle, due to the destructive choice of inorganic lifeform Chaoz666, the Scepter housing Amphoreus was chosen to become a Lord Ravager by Nanook, later referred to publicly as Irontomb. All entities linked to the twelve factors began to manifest Nanook's golden blood, with traits associated with Destruction within their extrapolation circuits. As a result, the experiment also successfully entered its projected trajectory.

### Final Amphoreus Cycle
#### An Eternal Page
At the end of the penultimate cycle of Amphoreus, the Chrysos Heirs of the 33,550,337th Eternal Recurrence who possessed a Titan's authority became the new Titans in the final cycle. After the events of , they, along with all other Amphoreus characters, are present within Beyond Time: An Eternal Page.

In An Eternal Page, some Chrysos Heirs who were previously recorded with specific flaws no longer possess them. In addition, Castorice lost her authority over Death, while Aglaea lost her authority of Romance, though it's unknown if this is the case for all of the Heirs.
