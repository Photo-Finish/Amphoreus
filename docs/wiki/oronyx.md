**Oronyx** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Time.

## Description
## Profile
Oronyx governs the past, present, and future of Amphoreus. They safeguard memories to prevent them from being lost to oblivion.

To have an audience with Oronyx, the person must go through the Abyss of Fate. A phenomena known as "Oronyx's Miracle" is able to create illusions, change reality, and restore certain objects to their past state. This is only effective for a limited time.

## History
### Origin Myth
In the beginning, there was only chaos, from which "existence" was created, a jumbled mess where fate's threads wrap chaos' nectar, bearing fruit. The primal Coreflame ignites within this fruit. When Oronyx cried, the fruit ripens, and chaos spills out, flooding the cosmos, and as such, time flows.

### Unknown Time Period
* During the time where Oronyx and Aquila shared authority over the sky, Oronyx utilized gems to absorb Aquila's radiance, intertwining it with the satin of the night and shaping the moon. However, one moon cannot rival the sun, so Oronyx created six moons to rival the brightness of the sun. However, Talanton eliminated four moons, leaving only the red and gold moon.
* Oronyx ordered Mnestia to weave romance across the earth, and hands them a golden spindle.
* When Amphoreus hosted a beauty pageant where several women had to pluck a fruit from the golden apple tree and deliver it to Mnestia intact, a peasant girl instead returned with a new seedling, explaining that she was saddened by the decaying of the golden apple, and planted the seeds from it instead. Georios, moved by the peasant girl's actions, urged Mnestia to crown the peasant girl as the most beautiful, however Mnestia was reluctant, as they were unable to see beauty in the girl. At that moment, Georios called upon Oronyx to cover Mnestia's eyes with the Evernight Veil. When they lost their sight, Mnestia saw the brilliance of the girl's heart and awarded her the title.

### Present
During the events of , a band of Mydei, Phainon, Trailblazer and Dan Heng failed to kill the Titan Nikador. In response to this, a new party of Phainon, Dan Heng, Trailblazer and Castorice went to obtain an audience with Oronyx to discover what made Nikador unkillable.

On the way to Oronyx's temple in the Janusopolis, Oronyx attempted to shoo the unwelcome guests away, claiming that they brought suffering to the Titans. Ultimately, the group's attempts failed, as Oronyx refused to help the Chrysos Heirs kill the other Titans, even when threatened by Phainon. However, they soon took an interest in the Trailblazer, realizing that they could help them find "Mother," as they had encountered them in the past. Oronyx then looked into the Trailblazer's past, unscrambling a chaotic memory during their time with the Stellaron Hunters. This act resulted in Fuli gazing upon the Trailblazer, which was a precious memory to Oronyx as they had finally caught THEIR gaze. Believing they could help locate THEM, Oronyx ordered Mem to accompany the Trailblazer, as they would help restore the world's memories and find "Mother."

During the events of , Oronyx is slain by the Flame Reaver, leaving their Coreflame behind. The Trailblazer then faces their trial in the Vortex of Genesis, where Oronyx reveals that they "have no future," wherein the Trailblazer and Mem realize that the Trailblazer had died when they arrived on Amphoreus. Oronyx tells the two that the Trailblazer has a chance to be revived and could save the planet, but would have to find their stolen future and cheat death. Oronyx proceeds to entrust their divinity to them before completely fading away.

## Abilities
### Prophecies
Oronyx's priests are able to gather prophecies of the future from Oronyx through the echoes across the Evernight Veil.

### Extrapolation
Oronyx is able to extrapolate a state in which a location or object filled with memories previously held.

### Dreams
It is said that dreams are the veils cast by Oronyx.

### Memories
Oronyx is able to restore forgotten memories of the past.
