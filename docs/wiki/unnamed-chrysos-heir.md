The **Unnamed Chrysos Heir** is a mission-exclusive NPC on Amphoreus, and the ancestor of Hyacinthia.

## Profile
The Unnamed Chrysos Heir was the last of the Skyfolk, only surviving because of an agreement with Seliose.

## History
After seeing the Skyfolk descend into madness and fight each other, Seliose's love for humanity turned into fury. She sent Solabis and Lunabis to execute and purify every Skyfolk in burning gold.

In an act of desperation and tenacity, the Unnamed Chrysos Heir pleaded with Seliose to spare her life. She reasoned with Seliose that even though humanity is flawed and imperfect, humans can be redeemed and bear the weight of the world, and that her descendants shall be the ones to prove it. Seliose found her proposal amusing, and agreed to spare her.

After her descent to the ground, she founded the original Twilight Courtyard, wandering with her family between city-states to find a home and spread the revised tale of Seliose. She told her children and grandchildren stories of the Sky Castrum alongside her husband, allowing records of Skyfolk culture to be recorded by one grandchild in *A Letter Home*. Her name went unrecorded by the Twilight Courtyard despite all she had done, and at some point, she passed away. Her descendants came to believe that they were descended from Seliose herself.

In the Light Calendar Year 4932, Hyacine, Phainon, Dan Heng, the Trailblazer, and Mem witnessed her story through a Fragment of Recollection, exposing the millennia-long lie about Hyacine's bloodline. Despite the revelation, Hyacine still chose to fight Seliose and won, proving the Unnamed Chrysos Heir's words and changing Seliose's view on humanity.
