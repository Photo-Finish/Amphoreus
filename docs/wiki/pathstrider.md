A **Pathstrider** is a term referring to those whose wills align closely with a Path, regardless of their views on it, and are therefore able to draw power from it. According to Screwllum, those who possess Path Energy aren't necessarily Pathstriders.

#### Fate Collaboration
During the Fate/stay night Event, Screwllum uses the memories of Pathstriders to create a repeatable simulation of the Holy Grail War. The game mentions to not take the lore implications seriously while within the Ritual Archives Enemy Servants screen, though the collaboration itself is confirmed to be canon through later references to it in the story. The following characters are featured within the simulation, and are thus implied to be Pathstriders:
* Argenti
* Castorice
* Cipher
* Clara

* Hyacine
* Kafka
* Seele
* Silver Wolf

* Jingliu
* Jing Yuan
* Yanqing
* Yunli

#### Divergent Universe
Sometime after Penacony's Charmony Festival, Screwllum and Dr. Ratio collaborated to created the Divergent Universe, starting a project known as "The Human Comedy." As part of this research, the Trailblazer could enter the Divergent Universe, and through the extrapolation of Equations, generate themselves as a materialized Pathstrider. This suggests that all Divergent Universe: The Human Comedy Equations are different Pathstriders.
