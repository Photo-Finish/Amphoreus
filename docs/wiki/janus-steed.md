The **Janus' Steeds** are a group of NPCs on Amphoreus, located in "Eternal Holy City" Okhema, "Sanctum of Prophecy" Janusopolis, and "Fallen Twilight City" Okhema.

## Profile
The Janus' Steeds are disciples of Janus based in Janusopolis. Their mission is to save Amphoreus from the black tide and seek alliance with other cities.

At present, the Janus' Steed in Okhema is presumably the last of them and the same character as the past one in "Sanctum of Prophecy" Janusopolis.
