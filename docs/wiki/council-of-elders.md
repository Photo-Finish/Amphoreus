The **Council of Elders** is a faction in Amphoreus. They are the main governing bodies within the cities of Amphoreus.

## Overview
The Council of Elders are in charge of the day-to-day affairs of the cities in Amphoreus. Each city has their own Council of Elders with their roles varying between cities.

### Okhema
Okhema's Council of Elders have temporarily transferred their power to Aglaea and the Chrysos Heirs due to the dire situation on Amphoreus. Most of the Council of Elders seem to dislike the Chrysos Heirs and the prophecy. According to Caenis, the Council of Elders' positions are "entrusted to them by the people of Okhema" hinting there might be a democratic process for electing elders in Okhema. Okhema's Council of Elders mostly despise the Chrysos Heirs as almost half of the council has voted against transferring power to the Chrysos Heirs and are continuely plotting against the Chrysos Heirs to this day.

### Castrum Kremnos
Revealed by Mem through a Memory Fragment, before the fall of Castrum Kremnos, the Council of Elders in Castrum Kremnos gave the Mountain Dwellers in their service freedom after Chartonus agreed to create a Sword Vessel capable of sealing divinity.
