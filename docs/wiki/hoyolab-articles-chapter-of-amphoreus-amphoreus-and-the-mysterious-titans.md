Amphoreus and its Mysterious TitansChapter of Amphoreus

The Titans were born from fire. Three carved the heavens and the earth, three wove the threads of fate, three molded life with their hands, and three guided calamity's gate.
---
Amphoreus the Realm of Gods

When the Titans created the world, the god of creation, Kephale, the Throne of Worlds, shaped the first humans in their image.
Humans built countless city-states across the vast lands of Amphoreus, each with distinct traditions and beliefs regarding the Titans.
Kephale blessed Okhema, Nikador protected Castrum Kremnos, and the priests of Janusopolis chanted the prophecy of the Three Titans of Fate.

Three Titans of Foundation
Three Titans carved the heavens and earth

Sky Titan
Eye of Twilight, Aquila
When Aquila's eyes that gaze upon the land open, the world welcomes light, and as Aquila's eyes close, the world plunges into darkness.

Earth Titan
Pillar of Stone, Georios
All mountain ranges were once manifestations of Georios. They are the biggest, most gentle, and benevolent Titan, the guardian of the mortal world.

Ocean Titan
Chalice of Plenty, Phagousa
Phagousa enjoys drifting through the mortal world, appearing in the ocean's waves, the river's rapids, and the goblets at feasts, bringing song, dance, and laughter.

---
Three Titans of Creation
The three molded life with their very hands

Romance Titan
Chrysalis of Gold, Mnestia
They and their Titankins pursue the beauty of the world, weaving it into golden threads to craft the romance of existence, granting the inhabitants of Amphoreus the gift of love and affection.

Worldbearing Titan
Throne of Worlds, Kephale
Kephale was entrusted by the pantheon with the burden of carrying life. They relinquished their throne of dominion over the world, solely to leave a glimmer of light for humanity.

Reason Titan
Bough of Rift, Cerces
Cerces manifested in the world as a great sacred tree, permitting humans to walk beside them, awaken enlightenment, and reflect deeply to gain knowledge.
Reason Titan

---
Three Titans of Calamity
The three guided calamity's gate

Trickery Titan
Coin of Whimsy, Zagreus
Without the velleity to carry out their divine responsibilities, Zagreus relish in creating disagreements amongst the gods, tricking them, and creating a mélange of chaos and disorderliness.

Strife Titan
Lance of Fury, Nikador
They declared war on the pantheon, plunging the world into endless strife. In ballads of bards, their divine blade of retribution could level heavens and earth in a single strike.

Death Titan
Hand of Shadow, Thanatos
When countless souls find no place to rest, then the tides of the River of Souls will surge onto the land, and Thanatos will steer their vessel to guide the dead to the afterlife.

---
Three Titans of Fate
Three wove the threads of fate

Time Titan
Veil of Evernight, Oronyx
Oronyx weaves the threads of time, upholding the past, present, and future of the world, ensuring the flow of all things.

 Titan
Scale of Justice, Talanton
Talanton permits neither favoritism nor injustice, safeguarding Amphoreus's taboos and boundaries while prescribing logic and law for all things.

Passage Titan
Gate of Infinity, Janus
Janus knows the existence and destination of all pathways. They govern not only the passages people traverse, but also the journeys that bind their fates.
