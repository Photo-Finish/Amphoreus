**Georios** is one of the Titans on Amphoreus. Their Coreflame is  Earth.

## Description
## Profile
One of the "Three Titans of Foundation". All mountain ranges were once manifestations of Georios. They are the biggest, most gentle, and benevolent Titan, the guardian of the mortal world.

The Dromas and Mountain Dwellers are creatures of Georios.
