## Idle Text
The Creation Nymph found next to Old Garmentmaker will have different idle quotes depending on how many nymphs have been found in this area:

;(If one or less nymphs have been found)
:It's lonely being a nymph...

;(When 2-9 nymphs have been found)
:You have found [number] nymphs so far!

;(After all nymphs are found, before solving the prophecy puzzle)
:Ring, ring, ring~

;(After all nymphs are found, after solving the prophecy puzzle)
:My family is all here!

## Additional Dialogue
After all nymphs are found, Aedes Elysiae's Creation Nymph next to Prototype Garmentmaker in "Eternal Holy City" Okhema will display the text below:

:I actually wanted to try the swing...
:But it's way too small!

The Creation Nymph found next to Old Garmentmaker displays the following for the prophecy puzzle, when interacted with, after collecting all nymphs:

: Investigate
:The nymph is trying its best to lift the chime. Looks like it's trying to tell you something. Hope it doesn't get too tired.
